1. Introduction

📌 The session will cover:

  • The structure of judicial decisions and how rulings are written.

  • The distinction between binding and persuasive precedents in Common Law systems.

  • Key legal terms used in court judgments and case law interpretation.

  • The challenges of translating complex legal reasoning and terminology.

A. Overview: Why Judicial Decisions Matter

Judicial decisions create legal precedent, guiding future cases with similar facts. The way a court ruling is worded affects interpretation, enforcement, and legal arguments.

For example:

  • A binding precedent must be followed by lower courts.

  • A persuasive precedent can influence rulings but is not mandatory.

  • Judicial opinions often include complex legal reasoning, requiring precise translation.

2. Key Legal Terms in Judicial Decisions

A. Common Terminology in Court Rulings

Term

Meaning

Example in Context

Ruling

A decision made by a judge or court

"The judge issued a ruling in favor of the plaintiff."

Precedent

A past court decision that guides future cases

"The Supreme Court decision set a precedent for similar cases."

Binding Precedent

A legal decision that must be followed by lower courts

"The lower court was bound by the Supreme Court’s ruling."

Persuasive Precedent

A legal decision that is influential but not mandatory

"The ruling in another jurisdiction served as persuasive precedent."

Injunction

A court order requiring or preventing an action

"The company received an injunction to stop selling the product."

Dissenting Opinion

A judge’s written disagreement with the majority ruling

"The dissenting opinion argued that the law was unconstitutional."

B. The Structure of a Judicial Decision

Section

Purpose

Example in Context

Case Facts

A summary of the legal dispute

"The case involved a breach of contract between two companies."

Legal Issue

The question the court must decide

"The issue was whether the employer’s policy violated labor law."

Holding

The court’s official decision

"The holding stated that the defendant was liable for damages."

Ratio Decidendi

The legal reasoning behind the ruling

"The ratio decidendi emphasized consumer protection laws."

Obiter Dictum

Additional comments that are not binding

"The judge noted, in obiter dictum, that future laws may change this rule."

Term

Meaning

3. Challenges in Translating Court Decisions

A. Literal vs. Contextual Translation

  • Legal terms often have no direct equivalents in other languages.

  • Judicial opinions use formal and archaic language (whereas, hereby, aforementioned).

  • Sentence structure in legal rulings is complex, requiring rewording for clarity.

English Term

Literal Translation (Problematic)

Functional Translation (Better)

Dismiss the case

Rejeitar o caso

Arquivar a ação

Issue an injunction

Emitir uma injunção

Conceder uma liminar

Enforce the judgment

Executar o julgamento

Cumprir a sentença

B. Ambiguity in Court Rulings

  • Judges may include broad legal interpretations that require careful translation.

  • Ambiguous phrasing can alter legal meaning, leading to misinterpretations.

4. Reading Practice: Judicial Rulings in Context

📖 Instruction: Read the following judicial excerpts aloud and analyze the legal terms used.

Text 1: Excerpt from a Supreme Court Decision

"The Court holds that the defendant’s actions constitute a breach of fiduciary duty. This ruling establishes a binding precedent for all lower courts within this jurisdiction. However, the dissenting opinion argues that the ruling misinterprets statutory law."

🔍 Comprehension Questions:

  1. What does the term binding precedent mean in this ruling?

  2. What is the significance of the dissenting opinion?

  3. How does this ruling affect future cases?

Text 2: Excerpt from an Injunction Order

"The Court grants the plaintiff’s request for a preliminary injunction. The defendant is hereby prohibited from engaging in the disputed activity until further notice. Failure to comply will result in contempt of court proceedings."

🔍 Comprehension Questions:

  1. What legal restriction does this injunction impose?

  2. What does “until further notice” mean in this context?

  3. What consequences does contempt of court imply?
