Grammar Point:

Archaic Terms

Explanation:

In legal English, many terms from past centuries are still in use. These archaic terms can make legal documents harder to understand but are often retained for historical or formal reasons. Understanding their meanings and knowing when to replace them with modern equivalents is crucial for accurate legal translation.

Key Archaic Terms

  1. Herein – in this document

  2. Heretofore – before this time

  3. Whereof – of which

  4. Thenceforth – from that time onward

  5. Whereas – considering that

  6. Shall – will (used to express a future action or requirement)

  7. Hitherto – until now

  8. Notwithstanding – in spite of

  9. Forasmuch as – since

  10. Therein – in that place

Examples of Archaic Terms in Legal Sentences

  1. Herein

    • "The parties agree to the terms and conditions set forth herein."

    • Modern version: "The parties agree to the terms and conditions stated in this document."

  2. Heretofore

    • "The defendant has not heretofore filed any objections."

    • Modern version: "The defendant has not previously filed any objections."

  3. Whereas

    • "The parties acknowledge, whereas, both sides agree to the settlement terms."

    • Modern version: "Considering that, both parties agree to the settlement terms."

  4. Notwithstanding

    • "Notwithstanding the above, the terms are still valid."

    • Modern version: "In spite of the above, the terms are still valid."

  5. Shall

    • "The tenant shall pay the rent on the first of each month."

    • Modern version: "The tenant will pay the rent on the first of each month."

2. Reading Practice

  • 📖 Instruction: Read the following passage aloud. Pay attention to the archaic terms used in the text and their meanings.


The parties heretofore mentioned have agreed to the following terms, wherein it is stipulated that the defendant, notwithstanding the objections raised, shall comply with the conditions set forth therein. Forasmuch as the settlement has been reached, thenceforth both parties shall be bound by the agreement. Whereas the plaintiff has accepted the settlement offer, all further claims are hereby dismissed.
