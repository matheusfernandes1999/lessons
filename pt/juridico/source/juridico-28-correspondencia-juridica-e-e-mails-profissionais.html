A. Overview: Why Legal Writing Matters

Legal correspondence is a critical skill for lawyers, translators, and business professionals. Poorly written legal documents can cause misunderstandings, disputes, and even legal liability.

For example:

  • Contracts must use clear, enforceable language.

  • Legal notices must be concise, authoritative, and legally binding.

  • Emails between legal professionals require a balance of formality and efficiency.

2. Key Legal Writing Structures and Conventions

A. Legal Correspondence Types and Their Purposes

Type

Purpose

Example in Context

Contract Clause

Defines rights and obligations in a contract

"The Seller shall deliver the Goods no later than 30 days after execution of this Agreement."

Formal Legal Letter

Communicates official legal information

"We hereby notify you that legal proceedings will be initiated."

Notice of Termination

Informs a party of contract termination

"Pursuant to Section 5, this contract is hereby terminated effective 30 days from receipt of this notice."

Demand Letter

Requests compliance or payment before legal action

"Unless payment is made within 14 days, we will pursue legal remedies."

Legal Email

Communicates legal matters professionally and efficiently

"Please find attached the revised contract for your review and signature."

B. Common Phrasing in Legal Writing

Legal Expression

Purpose

Example in Context

We hereby notify you that…

Formal notification

"We hereby notify you that your lease agreement has expired."

Pursuant to…

Referring to a legal provision

"Pursuant to Clause 10 of the Agreement, the Buyer shall assume liability."

Shall vs. May

Shall = obligation; May = discretion

"The Tenant shall pay rent on the first of each month." / "The Landlord may inspect the premises with prior notice."

Without prejudice…

Ensures a statement does not waive legal rights

"Without prejudice, we are open to negotiation before filing suit."

For the avoidance of doubt…

Clarifies potential ambiguity

"For the avoidance of doubt, this clause shall apply only to international transactions."

3. Challenges in Translating Legal Correspondence

A. Literal vs. Functional Translation

  • Legal documents require precision—word-for-word translation may lose legal enforceability.

  • Some expressions have no direct equivalent in other languages.

  • Cultural and jurisdictional differences affect legal phrasing.

English Term

Literal Translation (Problematic)

Functional Translation (Better)

Notice to Quit

Aviso para sair

Notificação de despejo

Time is of the essence

O tempo é essencial

O prazo deve ser rigorosamente cumprido

Without prejudice

Sem prejuízo

Sem renúncia de direitos

🔍 Exercise: Rewrite these legal phrases in clear, functional translations:

  1. "This agreement shall remain in full force and effect until terminated by either party."

  2. "Please be advised that failure to respond within 10 days will result in legal action."

  3. "Pursuant to the terms of the contract, the payment deadline is non-negotiable."

B. Ambiguity in Legal Writing

  • Vague wording can lead to disputes in legal correspondence.

  • Passive voice may obscure responsibility (e.g., "The contract was breached" vs. "The supplier breached the contract.").

  • Overuse of legal jargon can reduce clarity.

4. Reading Practice: Legal Correspondence in Context

📖 Instruction: Read the following excerpts aloud and analyze the legal terms used.

Text 1: Excerpt from a Contract Clause

"The Seller shall deliver the Goods no later than 30 days following the Effective Date. Failure to do so shall constitute a material breach of contract, entitling the Buyer to remedies under applicable law."

🔍 Comprehension Questions:

  1. What is the Seller’s obligation under this clause?

  2. What does “material breach” mean?

  3. What legal consequences does the Buyer have if the Seller fails to perform?

Text 2: Excerpt from a Legal Notice

"Dear Mr. Smith,
Pursuant to Section 8 of the Lease Agreement, we hereby notify you that your tenancy shall terminate on December 31, 2024. You are required to vacate the premises within 30 days of this date. Failure to comply may result in legal action.
Sincerely,
Legal Department"

🔍 Comprehension Questions:

  1. What is the purpose of this letter?

  2. What is the deadline for the recipient to vacate the premises?

  3. How does the phrase “Failure to comply may result in legal action” strengthen the notice?
