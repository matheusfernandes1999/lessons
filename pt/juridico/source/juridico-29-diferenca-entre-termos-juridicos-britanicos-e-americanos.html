A. Overview: Why Legal Terminology Differs

Although the UK and the US both follow Common Law traditions, their legal systems have evolved differently. This affects terminology, legal procedures, and even court structures.

For example:

  • A solicitor in the UK is not the same as an attorney in the US.

  • A barrister in the UK has a specialized role, while the term is rarely used in the US.

  • UK contracts often use shall, while US contracts may prefer must for obligations.
