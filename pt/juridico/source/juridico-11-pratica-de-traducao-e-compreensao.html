Practical Exercises in Legal Translation

🎯 Objectives:

  • Apply legal vocabulary, grammar, and translation techniques learned in previous lessons.

  • Develop strategies for accurate and idiomatic translation of legal documents.

2. Translation Exercise: Identifying Errors in Legal Texts

  • 📖 Instruction: Read the following legal excerpts and identify any errors or inconsistencies. Then, suggest a revised version using proper legal English. This is a grammar revision.

Text 1: Contract Clause with Incorrect Prepositions

"This contract is subject on approval by the Board of Directors. The Tenant is liable to any damages caused by negligence. The parties agreed in the terms before signing."

🔍Task: Identify incorrect prepositions and replace them with the correct ones.

Text 2: Binomials and Redundancy

"The agreement is null and void and must be entirely and completely removed from the records. The company must cease and desist from its actions and give and grant full rights to the claimant."

🔍Task: Simplify redundant binomials while maintaining legal precision.

Text 3: Polysemy and Ambiguity

"The judge will consider the case and execute a ruling. The lawyer executed the will in the presence of the executor. The defendant was given a sentence which was difficult to read."

🔍Task: Identify polysemous words and clarify their meaning based on context.

Text 4: Gerund vs. Infinitive in Legal Translation

"The court decided requiring the defendant to pay damages. The company is responsible for ensure compliance with regulations. The witness refused answering the question."

🔍Task: Correct the incorrect use of gerunds and infinitives.

Text 5: Complete Translation of a Legal Notice

📌 Translate the following legal text into clear and precise legal English:

"Este contrato deve ser executado de acordo com as leis aplicáveis. A parte que não cumprir os termos será responsabilizada por quaisquer danos decorrentes da violação. O locatário concorda com os termos e condições estabelecidos no presente documento."

🔍Task: Pay attention to prepositions, binomials, and formal legal vocabulary.

3. Reading Practice: Legal Document Samples

📖 Instruction: Read the following texts and discuss any challenging words or phrases.

Text 1: Excerpt from a Commercial Contract

"This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the applicable laws. The parties agree to resolve any disputes pursuant to the arbitration clause set forth herein. Any party failing to comply with the terms shall be held liable for breach of contract."

🔍Focus: Identify prepositional phrases, binomials, and formal legal structures.

Text 2: Excerpt from a Court Ruling

"The defendant was found guilty and sentenced to five years in prison. The court considered all evidence before issuing the ruling. The execution of the sentence shall be carried out in accordance with judicial procedures."

🔍Focus: Identify polysemous words, gerunds vs. infinitives, and formal legal phrasing.

5. Writing Exercise: Drafting a Legal Contract Clause

✍️ Task:
Write a short legal clause (4–5 sentences) for a rental agreement, employment contract, or service contract.

📚 Use at least three of the following:

  • Binomials (null and void, terms and conditions, fit and proper)

  • Prepositional phrases (subject to, in accordance with, pursuant to)

  • Polysemous words (execute, assign, issue, party)

  • Gerunds and infinitives (agreeing to, required to, responsible for ensuring)
