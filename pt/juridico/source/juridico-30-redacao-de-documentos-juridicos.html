A. Overview: Why Clarity and Precision Matter

Legal documents must be accurate, clear, and enforceable. Poor drafting can lead to disputes, misinterpretation, and legal challenges.

For example:

  • Ambiguous contract clauses can create enforcement issues.

  • Overuse of archaic legal terms can reduce readability.

  • Unclear pronoun references can make obligations uncertain.

2. Principles of Legal Drafting and Translation

A. Key Guidelines for Clear Legal Writing

Principle

Definition

Example in Context

Be Clear

Use straightforward, unambiguous language

"The Tenant shall pay rent on the first of each month." vs. "The Tenant shall make rental payments in a timely manner."

Be Concise

Avoid unnecessary words and redundancy

"Null and void" → "Void"

Use Consistent Terminology

Avoid synonyms that may create confusion

"Lessee" and "Tenant" should not be used interchangeably

Avoid Passive Voice

Clearly indicate responsibility

"The Seller shall deliver the goods." vs. "The goods shall be delivered by the Seller."

Define Key Terms

Clarify specific meanings

"For the purpose of this Agreement, ‘Goods’ shall mean all items listed in Schedule A."

B. Common Translation Errors in Legal Documents

Error Type

Problem

Example

Correction

False Friends

Words that look similar but have different meanings

"Execution" (UK: signing a contract, US: carrying out a death sentence)

"Signing" for contracts

Over-Literal Translation

Direct translation that does not convey legal meaning

"Contract will be nulo e vazio."

"Contract will be void."

Jurisdiction-Specific Terms

Using the wrong legal term for a jurisdiction

"Solicitor" (UK) instead of "Attorney" (US)

Use correct jurisdictional term

Ambiguity

Unclear meaning due to improper phrasing

"The company must follow applicable rules."

"The company must comply with all regulations under [specific law]."

3. Challenges in Translating Legal Documents

A. Literal vs. Functional Translation

  • Legal language must convey enforceability, not just literal meaning.

  • Some legal concepts do not exist in all jurisdictions and require adaptation.

  • Overuse of archaic legal terms can reduce clarity and accessibility.

Legal Term

Literal Translation (Problematic)

Functional Translation (Better)

Time is of the essence

O prazo deve ser rigorosamente cumprido

Without prejudice

Sem prejuízo

Não pode ser usado como prova

Force majeure

Força maior

Circunstâncias imprevistas que impedem o cumprimento do contrato

B. Avoiding Ambiguity in Contract Clauses

  • Unclear contract terms can lead to disputes.

  • Avoid vague references (e.g., "as soon as possible", "reasonable efforts").

  • Use specific, enforceable language.

4. Reading Practice: Legal Writing in Context

📖 Instruction: Read the following legal excerpts aloud and analyze the legal terms used.

Text 1: Excerpt from a Contract Clause

"The Seller shall deliver the Goods within thirty (30) days from the Effective Date. In the event of a delay, the Buyer shall have the right to terminate this Agreement and seek damages."

🔍 Comprehension Questions:

  1. What is the Seller’s obligation under this clause?

  2. What does “shall have the right” imply legally?

  3. How could this clause be misinterpreted if poorly translated?

Text 2: Excerpt from a Legal Notice

"Pursuant to Clause 5 of the Lease Agreement, we hereby notify you that failure to remit payment within ten (10) days shall constitute a breach of contract. Without prejudice to our rights, we may initiate legal proceedings to recover outstanding amounts."

🔍 Comprehension Questions:

  1. What legal consequence follows from non-payment?

  2. What does "without prejudice to our rights" mean?

  3. How does this clause ensure enforceability?
