📘 O que é o verbo To Be no presente e no passado?

O verbo to be é um dos verbos mais importantes em inglês, usado para falar sobre identidade, estados, características, localização, entre outros.

No presente:

Sujeito

Verbo To Be (Presente)

Exemplo

I

am

I am happy.

You

are

You are a student.

He/She/It

is

She is a teacher.

We

are

We are friends.

They

are

They are at home.

No passado:

Sujeito

Verbo To Be (Passado)

Exemplo

I

was

I was tired yesterday.

You

were

You were late.

He/She/It

was

He was happy last week.

We

were

We were at the park.

They

were

They were busy.

🧠 Na prática!

Exemplos de frases no presente:

  • I am a student.

  • She is my friend.

  • We are happy.

Exemplos de frases no passado:

  • I was at school yesterday.

  • He was sick last week.

  • They were on vacation.

✍️ Sua vez de praticar!

🔹 Atividade 1 – Complete com am, is, ou are (presente):

  1. I ___ a teacher.

  2. They ___ friends.

  3. She ___ happy today.

  4. We ___ students.

  5. You ___ late.

🔹 Atividade 2 – Complete com was ou were (passado):

  1. He ___ at home yesterday.

  2. We ___ tired last night.

  3. I ___ at the party.

  4. You ___ very kind.

  5. They ___ in the park.

🔹 Atividade 3 – Perguntas e respostas no presente e passado:

  • Pergunte ao aluno: “Are you happy?” / “Was she at school yesterday?”

  • O aluno responde com frases completas.

🔹 Atividade 4 – Corrija as frases incorretas:

  1. I is a student. → ___

  2. They was at home. → ___

  3. She are my friend. → ___

🔹 Atividade 5 – Criação de frases:

  • Peça para o aluno criar 3 frases usando to be no presente e 3 no passado.

📖 Texto de Prática

Yesterday and Today

Yesterday I was tired because I was at work all day. My friend was happy because she was with her family. We were all very busy. Today, I am happy and relaxed. She is at home, and they are at school.

Responda:

  • Where was the speaker yesterday?

  • How was his friend feeling?

  • What are they doing today?

  • Cite 2 frases no passado do texto.

  • Cite 2 frases no presente do texto.

📌 Revisão da Aula

  • To be no presente: am, is, are.

  • To be no passado: was, were.

  • Usado para estados, características, localização.

  • Diferença clara entre uso no presente e no passado.
