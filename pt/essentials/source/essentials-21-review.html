📘 O que são Tempos Verbais e Verbos Importantes?

Os tempos verbais são ferramentas que usamos para situar ações no tempo — passado, presente ou futuro — e indicar se a ação foi concluída, habitual ou em progresso. Verbos modais expressam possibilidade, obrigação, permissão, e outras nuances.

🧠 Na prática!

Exemplos:

Past Simple

  • I visited my grandparents last weekend.

  • She didn’t go to school yesterday.

  • Did you watch the game?

Past Continuous

  • I was cooking when the phone rang.

  • They weren’t playing football at 5 pm.

  • Was he sleeping during the movie?

Present Perfect

  • I have finished my homework.

  • She hasn’t seen that movie yet.

  • Have you ever been to Paris?

Used To

  • I used to live in New York.

  • He didn’t use to like vegetables.

  • Did you use to play basketball?

First Conditional

  • If it rains, I will take an umbrella.

  • If you study, you will pass the test.

  • Will you call me if you arrive late?

📖 Texto de prática

A Trip Last Year

Last year, I went to the beach with my family. We stayed there for a week. While we were swimming, it suddenly started to rain. I have never seen so much rain on vacation! I used to go to the beach every summer, but this year I stayed home because it was too cold. If the weather is nice next year, I will go again.

Perguntas:

  • When did the trip happen?

  • What were they doing when it started to rain?

  • Has the speaker ever seen a lot of rain on vacation before?

  • What did the speaker use to do every summer?

  • What will the speaker do if the weather is nice next year?
