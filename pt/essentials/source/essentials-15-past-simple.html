📘 O que é Past Simple?

O Past Simple é o tempo verbal usado para falar de ações que aconteceram no passado e já foram concluídas.

Forma afirmativa:

  • Sujeito + verbo no passado (regular ou irregular)

    • I watched a movie yesterday.

    • She went to the market.

Forma negativa:

  • Sujeito + did not (didn’t) + verbo no infinitivo

    • He didn’t like the food.

Forma interrogativa:

  • Did + sujeito + verbo no infinitivo

    • Did you study English?

Verbos regulares: terminam em -ed → play → played

Verbos irregulares: mudam completamente → go → went

🧠 Na prática!

Frases com verbos regulares:

  • I visited my grandma last weekend.

  • They cleaned the house.

  • We played soccer.

Frases com verbos irregulares:

  • She ate pizza.

  • He drank coffee.

  • I saw a movie.

  1. ___ you (visit) your cousin?

📖 Texto de Prática

Last Sunday

Last Sunday, I woke up early and had breakfast with my family. Then, we went to the park. My brother played soccer and I read a book. In the afternoon, we visited our grandma. She cooked a delicious lunch. At night, we watched a movie together.

Perguntas:

  • What did they do in the morning?

  • What did the brother do?

  • Where did they go in the afternoon?

  • What did they do at night?

📌 Revisão da Aula

  • Past Simple é usado para ações concluídas no passado.

  • Verbos regulares terminam em -ed.

  • Verbos irregulares mudam de forma.

  • Uso do did/didn’t para negar e perguntar.
