📘 O que é There is / There are / There was / There were?

Usamos essas expressões para dizer que algo existe ou existia em um lugar.

Presente:

  • There is → algo no singular.
    Ex: There is a cat on the table.

  • There are → mais de um (plural).
    Ex: There are two books on the shelf.

Passado:

  • There was → algo no singular no passado.
    Ex: There was a party yesterday.

  • There were → plural no passado.
    Ex: There were many people at the party.

🧠 Na prática!

Exemplos variados:

  • There is a pen on my desk.

  • There are three students in the room.

  • There was a dog in the park yesterday.

  • There were two concerts last weekend.

Mostre imagens e pergunte:

  • “What is in the picture?”

  • “Is there a cat?” → “Yes, there is!” / “No, there isn’t.”

    • “There are ___.”

  • Depois, pedir para falar sobre ontem:

    • “There was ___ at the park.”

    • “There were ___ in my house.”

  • O aluno desenha um lugar (ex: cozinha, sala, parque).

  • Depois, descreve com frases:

📖 Texto de Prática

At the Park

Today, there are many people at the park. There is a man playing the guitar. There are children running and laughing. Yesterday, there was a fair here. There were games, food, and music. It was a fun day!

Responda:

  • Where are the people today?

  • What is the man doing?

  • What was there yesterday?

  • Encontre e copie 3 frases com there is / there are / there was / there were.

📌 Revisão da Aula

  • Usamos there is / there are para falar do presente.

  • Usamos there was / there were para o passado.

  • Singular: there is / there was

  • Plural: there are / there were
