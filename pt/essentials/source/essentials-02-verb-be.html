📘 O que é o Verb to be – Present and Past?

O verbo to be significa “ser” ou “estar”. Ele é um dos mais importantes em inglês.

Presente (Present Simple):

Pronome

Verbo

I

am

You

are

He/She/It

is

We/You/They

are

Ex:

  • I am happy.

  • She is a student.

  • They are at school.

Passado (Past Simple):

Pronome

Verbo

I/He/She/It

was

You/We/They

were

Ex:

  • He was tired.

  • We were in Rio last week.

🧠 Na prática!

Exemplos variados:

  • I am hungry. → I was hungry yesterday.

  • She is at home. → She was at home last night.

  • They are friends. → They were classmates in 2020.

  • It is cold. → It was cold in the morning.

📖 Texto de Prática

A Day in My Life

Today I am happy. It is Saturday. My brother and I are at home. We are watching a movie. Yesterday, we were at school. It was a long day. My mom was tired, but she made dinner. We were all together at night.

Responda:

  • What day is it today?

  • Where are they now?

  • Where were they yesterday?

  • Who was tired?

  • Identifique 3 frases no passado com to be.

📌 Revisão da Aula

  • O verbo to be significa “ser” ou “estar”.

  • No presente: am, is, are.

  • No passado: was, were.

  • O verbo muda conforme o pronome.

  • Praticar com frases ajuda a memorizar.
