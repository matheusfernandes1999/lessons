📘 O que são Reflexive Pronouns?

Reflexive Pronouns são usados quando o sujeito da frase realiza a ação sobre si mesmo, ou para dar ênfase a quem executa a ação.

Pessoa

Reflexive Pronoun

I

myself

You (singular)

yourself

He

himself

She

herself

It

itself

We

ourselves

You (plural)

yourselves

They

themselves

Quando usar:

  • Quando o sujeito e o objeto são a mesma pessoa ou coisa:
    She taught herself to swim.

  • Para dar ênfase:
    I did the work myself.

🧠 Exemplos variados para cada pronome:

Myself:

  • I accidentally hurt myself while cooking.

  • I told myself to stay calm during the exam.

Yourself (singular):

  • Be proud of yourself for finishing the race.

  • Did you enjoy yourself at the party last night?

Himself:

  • He fixed the broken chair himself.

  • He often talks to himself when he’s thinking.

Herself:

  • She prepared herself for the big interview.

  • She found herself lost in the new city.

Itself:

  • The cat cleaned itself after eating.

  • The machine shuts itself off after 10 minutes.

Ourselves:

  • We treated ourselves to a nice dinner.

  • We need to remind ourselves to stay positive.

Yourselves (plural):

  • You should prepare yourselves for the test.

  • Did you all enjoy yourselves on the trip?

Themselves:

  • They organized the event by themselves.

  • The children amused themselves with games.

  1. I bought a new car. I bought the car myself. → ____

  2. The children made the decorations. → ____

  3. She finished the report. → ____

  4. You should be proud of you. → ____

📖 Texto de Prática ampliado

Learning New Skills

When Maria moved to a new city, she didn’t know anyone. She decided to teach herself how to cook local dishes. Every day, she watched videos and practiced by herself in her small kitchen. Sometimes she burnt the food, but she told herself to keep trying.

Her friends invited her to parties, and she enjoyed herself while making new friends. One day, Maria looked at herself in the mirror and felt proud. She realized she had grown a lot by relying on herself.

Maria and her roommates prepared themselves for a big exam. They studied together but also spent time encouraging themselves not to give up. After the exam, they treated themselves to a nice dinner to celebrate their hard work.

Perguntas para o texto:

  • Como Maria aprendeu a cozinhar?

  • O que Maria sentiu ao final?

  • Como Maria e suas amigas se prepararam para o exame?

  • Cite 3 pronomes reflexivos usados no texto.

📌 Revisão da Aula

  • Reflexive pronouns indicam que o sujeito e o objeto são a mesma pessoa/coisa.

  • Usados para ênfase ou ação feita por si mesmo.

  • Terminam em -self (singular) ou -selves (plural).

  • Exemplos variados ajudam a entender melhor o uso.

🏠 Homework detalhado

📅 Dia 1 – Leitura e identificação

  • Leia um texto simples em inglês (pode ser de um livro ou internet).

  • Destaque todos os pronomes reflexivos que encontrar.

📅 Dia 2 – Escrita de frases

  • Escreva 10 frases diferentes usando pronomes reflexivos.

  • Use contextos variados (ex: escola, família, esportes).

📅 Dia 3 – Reescrita de frases

  • Escolha 5 frases comuns e reescreva substituindo sujeito + objeto pelo pronome reflexivo.

  • Ex: She cleaned the room. → She cleaned the room herself.

📅 Dia 4 – Exercícios de correspondência

  • Complete um exercício combinando sujeitos com pronomes reflexivos corretos.

  • Exemplo: I → myself, They → themselves.

📅 Dia 5 – Redação curta

  • Escreva um parágrafo (8-10 frases) contando uma experiência onde você fez algo sozinho(a).

  • Use pronomes reflexivos pelo menos 4 vezes.

📅 Dia 6 – Conversação e apresentação

  • Prepare um pequeno diálogo ou apresentação sobre momentos em que você fez algo sozinho(a).

  • Pratique a pronúncia e tente usar diferentes pronomes reflexivos.

  • Se possível, grave e escute para autocorreção.
