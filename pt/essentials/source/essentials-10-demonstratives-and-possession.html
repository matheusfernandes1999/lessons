📘 O que são Demonstratives e Possessives?

🔹 Demonstratives (Pronomes Demonstrativos)

Usamos para apontar ou mostrar algo.

Singular

Plural

This – este/esta (perto)

These – estes/estas (perto)

That – aquele/aquela (longe)

Those – aqueles/aquelas (longe)

Exemplos:

  • This is my book. (perto)

  • That is your car. (longe)

  • These are my shoes. (perto)

  • Those are his dogs. (longe)

🔸 Possessives (Pronomes Possessivos)

Usamos para mostrar posse, ou seja, que algo pertence a alguém.

Pessoa

Possessive

I

my

You

your

He

his

She

her

It

its

We

our

They

their

Exemplos:

  • This is my bag.

  • Is that your phone?

  • Her name is Ana.

  • Their house is big.

🧠 Exemplos de uso

  1. This is my pen. (perto + posse)

  2. That is his bike. (longe + posse)

  3. These are our books. (plural perto)

  4. Those are their cats. (plural longe)

🧠 Dica visual:
Use objetos reais ou imagens para ensinar o conceito de perto/longe e singular/plural.

✍️ Sua vez de praticar!

Atividade 1 – Complete com o demonstrativo correto:

  1. ___ is my teacher. (apontando alguém perto)

  2. ___ are my friends. (foto de grupo longe)

  3. ___ is my pencil. (pencil está na sua mão)

  4. ___ are your keys? (chaves na mesa distante)

Atividade 2 – Complete com o possessivo correto:

  1. I have a dog. This is ___ dog.

  2. She is Maria. ___ car is red.

  3. We live here. This is ___ house.

  4. He has a new phone. ___ phone is expensive.

Atividade 3 – Combine:

Combine demonstrativos + possessivos:

  • This / my → This is my pen.

  • That / her → That is her laptop.

Atividade 4 – Jogo de perguntas rápidas:

  • What’s this?

  • Is that your notebook?

  • Whose book is this?

  • Are these your glasses?

📖 Texto de Prática

A Visit to My Friend’s House

Hi! I’m Ana. Today I’m visiting my friend Lucas. This is his room. These are his books. That is his guitar in the corner. Those are his old toys. I like his room because it’s colorful and fun. We are doing our homework together. I have my notebook, and he has his laptop.

Responda:

  1. Quem está visitando quem?

  2. O que é “that” no texto?

  3. Quais palavras mostram posse?

  4. Encontre 3 demonstrativos no texto.

📌 Revisão da Aula

  • This / These → perto (singular / plural)

  • That / Those → longe (singular / plural)

  • Possessivos mostram posse: my, your, his, her, our, their

  • Podemos combinar os dois: This is my phone.
