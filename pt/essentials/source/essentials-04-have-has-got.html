🔹 Have Got / Has Got

✅ Revisão da Aula Anterior

Revisão rápida com o aluno:

  • Peça frases com there is / there are para falar do presente.

  • Peça frases com there was / there were para falar do passado.

  • Corrija e incentive o aluno a usar formas no singular e plural.

📘 O que é Have Got / Has Got?

Usamos have got e has got para falar sobre posse, características ou para descrever algo que alguém tem.

  • Have got é usado com I, you, we, they

  • Has got é usado com he, she, it

Exemplos:

  • I have got a car. (Eu tenho um carro.)

  • She has got blue eyes. (Ela tem olhos azuis.)

  • They have got two dogs. (Eles têm dois cachorros.)

Forma contraída (mais usada na fala):

  • I’ve got

  • You’ve got

  • He’s got

  • She’s got

  • It’s got

  • We’ve got

  • They’ve got

🧠 Na prática!

Exemplos variados:

  • I have got a new phone.

  • You have got a big family.

  • He has got a bike.

  • She has got curly hair.

  • It has got four legs.

  • We have got tickets for the show.

  • They have got many friends.

🔹 Atividade 4 – Perguntas e respostas:

  • Pergunte ao aluno: “Have you got a pet?”

  • Faça perguntas com pronomes diferentes, e o aluno responde com frases completas.

Ex:

  • Have you got a pet? → Yes, I have got a dog.

  • Has she got a bike? → No, she hasn’t got a bike.

🔹 Atividade 5 – Criação de frases:

O aluno cria 5 frases falando de posse, usando pronomes diferentes.

📖 Texto de Prática

My House

I have got a big house. It has got three bedrooms and a garden. My brother has got a small room. We have got a dog and two cats. My parents have got a car. They have got many friends too.

Responda:

  • Who has got a big house?

  • How many bedrooms does the house have?

  • Who has got a small room?

  • What pets do they have?

  • Find 3 sentences with have got or has got.

  • Contrações são comuns (I’ve got, she’s got).
