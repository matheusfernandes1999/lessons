📘 O que é Past Continuous?

O Past Continuous é usado para descrever:

  • Ações em progresso no passado.

  • Situações de fundo (background) para outra ação.

🔹 Estrutura:

Sujeito + was/were + verbo com -ing

Exemplos:

  • I was studying at 8 p.m.

  • They were watching a movie.

  • While I was cooking, he arrived.

🧠 Exemplos Variados

Afirmativas:

  • She was reading a book.

  • We were playing soccer.

Negativas:

  • I wasn’t sleeping.

  • They weren’t listening.

Interrogativas:

  • Was he working yesterday?

  • What were you doing at 6 p.m.?

Combinação com Past Simple:

  • I was taking a shower when the phone rang.

  • They were playing while we were studying.

✍️ Exercícios com o Professor

🔹 Atividade 1 – Complete com o tempo correto:

  1. She ___ (cook) when I arrived.

  2. We ___ (not play) at 9 a.m.

  3. What ___ you ___ (do) last night?

🔹 Atividade 2 – Combine frases:

  • Crie duas colunas com ações em Past Simple e Past Continuous.

  • Aluno combina: Ex: The phone rang + I was sleeping → I was sleeping when the phone rang.

🔹 Atividade 3 – Mímica + descrição: O professor faz uma ação, o aluno descreve usando Past Continuous. Ex: You were pretending to dance.

🔹 Atividade 4 – Responda com detalhes:

  • What were you doing yesterday at 10 p.m.?

  • Was it raining when you woke up?

📖 Texto de Prática

An Unexpected Call

Yesterday, I was watching a movie when my phone rang. It was my friend Ana. She was feeling sad, so we talked for a while. While we were talking, my dog was barking loudly. I wasn’t paying attention to the movie anymore.

Perguntas:

  1. What was the person doing at the beginning?

  2. Who called?

  3. What was the dog doing?

  4. Find 3 verbs in Past Continuous.

📌 Revisão da Aula

  • Past Continuous descreve ações em progresso no passado.

  • Estrutura: was/were + verbo com -ing

  • Pode ser combinado com Past Simple para mostrar ações simultâneas ou interrupções.
