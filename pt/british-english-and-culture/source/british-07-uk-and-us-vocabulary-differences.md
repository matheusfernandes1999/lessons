## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** palavras que mudam entre o inglês **britânico** e o **americano** (*flat/apartment, holiday/vacation, queue/line*), com foco em **adaptar** textos para um público britânico.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** reescrever avisos e mensagens para o vocabulário britânico e explicar equivalências.
**Tarefa verificável:** adaptar oito avisos do inglês americano para público britânico.

## 2. Apresentação e exemplos (10 min)
### Casa e cidade (UK ← US)
- **flat** ← apartment · **lift** ← elevator · **ground floor** ← first floor · **first floor** ← second floor · **garden** ← yard · **rubbish / bin** ← trash / trash can · **pavement** ← sidewalk · **car park** ← parking lot · **high street** ← main street
### Transporte
- **lorry** ← truck · **petrol** ← gas · **motorway** ← highway · **the Underground / the Tube** (Londres) ← subway · **return ticket** ← round-trip ticket · **single** ← one-way · **queue** ← line · **timetable** ← schedule
### Comida e compras
- **biscuit** ← cookie · **chips** ← fries · **crisps** ← chips · **sweets** ← candy · **the bill** ← the check · **takeaway** ← takeout/to go · **shop** ← store · **trolley** ← cart
### Outros
- **holiday** ← vacation · **mobile (phone)** ← cell phone · **trousers** ← pants (UK *pants* = cueca/calcinha!) · **jumper** ← sweater · **toilet / loo** ← restroom/bathroom · **football** ← soccer · **post** ← mail · **postcode** ← zip code · **term** ← semester (escola)
### ⚠️ Cuidado
*pants, chips, first floor* mudam de sentido. Pense em **quem vai ler/ouvir**.

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **lorry** = LOR-ee · **queue** = kyoo · **trolley** = TROL-ee · **loo** = loo · **petrol** = PET-rəl
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> There's a long queue at the car park, so let's take the lift to the ground floor.

## 3. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 3.1 Troque e fale: americano → britânico
> *Take the elevator to the first floor.* → *Take the lift to the ground floor.*
> *Put your trash in the trash can.* · *I'm going on vacation.* · *There's a line at the store.* · *I left my cell phone in the parking lot.* · *Can we get the check?* · *I'd like a round-trip ticket.*
### 3.2 Explique uma equivalência
Modelo: *In the UK, people usually say “biscuit” for what Americans call a “cookie.”*
### 3.3 Falsos amigos
Explique o problema: *I spilled coffee on my pants.* (dito em Londres) · *Do you want chips with that?* (num pub)
