✍️ Prática

📌 Aula 1: Alfabeto e Comandos Básicos


  • 📝 Traduza as frases:

    • 🔹 Repeat after me.
      🔹 Translate this word to Portuguese.
      🔹 One more time, please.

  • 🔊 Pronuncie as palavras em inglês:

    • 🏠 Home | 💼 Job | ✏️ Mark | 🎵 Xylophone

📌 Aula 2: Pronomes Pessoais


  • 📝 Complete com o pronome correto:

    • 🔹 ___ am a student.
      🔹 ___ are teachers.
      🔹 ___ work in a bank.

  • 📝 Traduza para o inglês:

    • 🔹 Eu cozinho.
      🔹 Nós estudamos.
      🔹 Eles têm um livro.

📌 Aula 3: Números e Cores

  • ❓ Responda em inglês:

    • 🔹 How many pencils do you have?
      🔹 What color is your shirt?

  • 📝 Escreva por extenso:

    • 🔹 3 ➝ _______
      🔹 7 ➝ _______
      🔹 9 ➝ _______

📌 Aula 4: Dias da Semana e Cumprimentos

  • 📝 Traduza para o inglês:

    • 🔹 Eu vi Maria na segunda-feira.
      🔹 Eu trabalho de segunda a sexta.
      🔹 Nos vemos na quarta-feira!

  • 💬 Crie um diálogo curto, incluindo:

    • 🔹 Um cumprimento inicial.
      🔹 A pergunta How are you? e a resposta.

      🔹 A menção a um dia da semana.

📌 Aula 5: Substantivos e Verbos

  • 📝 Identifique substantivos e verbos:

    • 🔹 I have a car.
      🔹 We go to school every day.
      🔹 They make delicious food.

  • 📝 Crie frases com as palavras:

    • 📖 Book | 🚗 Car | 🍔 Food | ✏️ Pen

📄 Prática de Compreensão

I have a red pen and a blue book. I teach English at school Monday to Friday. I love my job and my students. Weekends are for family. We often go to the park, have dinner, or see a movie and get ice cream (chocolate is my favorite). My family is important to me: Mom (nurse), Dad (doctor), and younger sister (student, likes to read and draw).

During the week, I have toast and coffee for breakfast, take the bus to school (listening to music or reading), teach, go to the gym, cook dinner for my family, and relax by reading, watching TV, or seeing friends. I'm learning guitar, planning a Europe trip (Italy and France) next summer, and learning basic Italian and French. I'm happy with my life, always learning, positive, and grateful.

Comprehension Questions:

  1. What color is the pen and the book?

  2. What is the narrator's job?

  3. What does the narrator do on weekends?

  4. What are the names and professions of the narrator's parents?

  5. What does the narrator's sister like to do?

  6. What does the narrator usually eat for breakfast?

  7. How does the narrator get to school?

  8. What subject does the narrator teach?

  9. What does the narrator do after school?

  10. What are the narrator's hobbies?
