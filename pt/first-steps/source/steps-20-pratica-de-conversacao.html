Revisão

Nas aulas anteriores, você aprendeu a formar frases simples, a fazer perguntas básicas e a usar vocabulário do dia a dia. Esses conhecimentos são fundamentais para iniciar uma conversa em inglês.

Saberia responder essas perguntas: How are you today? Do you eat breakfast everyday?

Objetivo

No final desta aula, você será capaz de:

  • Iniciar e manter uma conversa simples em inglês.

  • Utilizar cumprimentos, perguntas e respostas básicas de forma natural.

  • Aplicar estruturas e vocabulário já estudados para expressar ideias e opiniões.

  • Ganhar confiança para interagir oralmente em situações cotidianas.

Por que é importante?
Conversar em inglês é essencial para aprimorar a fluência e a compreensão oral. Praticar a conversação permite que você integre o vocabulário e as estruturas aprendidas, desenvolvendo a habilidade de se comunicar de forma clara e natural em diversas situações do dia a dia.

Conteúdo Principal da Aula: Greetings, Small Talk, Questions, Responses

Prática de conversação - Small talks

Nesta aula, vamos focar em desenvolver habilidades para iniciar e manter conversas simples em inglês. Conversar é um exercício prático que integra diversos aspectos da língua: vocabulário, estrutura gramatical e entonação.

Hoje, vamos quase que literalmente apenas fazer e responder MUITAS perguntas. Tente responder o mais completo possível e se não souber responder use as frases que aprendeu na primeira aula de conversação.

Cada pergunta tem duas variações mais fácil caso a primeira não seja entendida

Cumprimentos e Informações Pessoais:

  • What's your name? / Tell me your name. / Your name is?

  • How are you today? / Are you okay today? / How do you feel today?

  • Where do you live? / You live where? / What city do you live in?

  • How old are you? / Your age? / Tell me your age.

  • What do you do? (What's your job?) / What is your work? / What job do you have?

Família e Amigos:

  • Do you have brothers or sisters? / You have brothers? Sisters? / Family? Brothers? Sisters?

  • What does your father/mother do? / Your father's work? / Your mother's job?

  • Do you have pets? / Animals at home? / You have a dog? Cat?

  • Do you like animals? / You like dogs? Cats? / Animals good?

  • What do you do with your friends? / You and friends do what? / Play with friends?

Rotina Diária:

  • What time do you wake up? / You wake up when? / Time to wake up?

  • Do you drink coffee or tea in the morning? / Coffee or tea? Morning? / Drink in morning? Coffee? Tea?

  • How do you go to work/school? / Go to work how? / Go to school by? (car, bus)

  • What do you eat for lunch? / Lunch? What food? / You eat what? Lunch?

  • What do you do in the evening? / Evening? You do what? / Night? Activities?

Preferências e Gostos:

  • Do you like music? What kind? / Music? Yes? What type? / You like music? What music?

  • Do you like sports? Which ones? / Sports? You like? Which? / Play sports? Which sports?

  • Do you like to read books? What kind? / Read books? Yes? What books? / Books? You like? What type?

  • Do you like to watch movies? What kind? / Movies? You watch? What movies? / Watch films? Which films?

  • What is your favorite food? / Food you like most? / Best food?

Viagens e Experiências:

  • Have you ever traveled to another country? / You travel to other country? / Go to other country?

  • What countries would you like to visit? / Want to visit what country? / Countries you want to see?

  • What did you do on your last vacation? / Last vacation? You do what? / Vacation? What happened?

  • Do you like to try new foods? / New food? You like? / Try new food? Good?

  • What is the most interesting place you have visited? / Best place you go? / Interesting place? Where?

Planos e Futuro:

  • What are your plans for the weekend? / Weekend? You do what? / Plans for weekend?

  • What are you going to do next year? / Next year? You do what? / Plans for next year?

  • What do you want to learn in the future? / Learn what? Future? / Want to learn what?

  • Do you have any goals for your career? / Work goals? You have? / Goals for work?

  • Where do you see yourself in five years? / Five years? You where? / Future? Five years?

Opiniões e Discussões:

  • What do you think about social media? / Social media? Good? Bad? / You like social media?

  • Do you think it's important to learn English? / English important? Yes? No? / Learn English? Important?

  • What are the advantages and disadvantages of living in a big city? / Big city? Good? Bad? / City living? Pros and cons?

  • Do you prefer to live in the city or in the countryside? / City or country? You like? / Live? City? Country?

  • What do you think about environmental issues? / Environment? You think? / Nature? Problems?

Situações do Dia a Dia:

  • What do you do when you have free time? / Free time? You do what? / Time free? Activities?

  • How do you relax after a busy day? / Relax after work? How? / After busy day? You relax?

  • Do you prefer to cook or to eat out? / Cook or eat outside? / You like cook or eat restaurant?

  • What is your favorite way to spend a rainy day? / Rainy day? You do what? / Rain? How to spend time?

  • How do you stay healthy? / You stay healthy? How? / Healthy? What you do?

Perguntas Adicionais:

  • Revisão de vocabulário: Peça ao aluno para descrever imagens ou objetos.

  • Prática de gramática: Peça ao aluno para criar frases usando tempos verbais específicos.

  • Discussão de textos curtos: Leia um texto curto e faça perguntas de compreensão.

  • Role-playing: Crie situações do dia a dia para praticar a conversação.

  • Checklist
    Você consegue...

  • Iniciar uma conversa com um cumprimento simples.

  • Fazer perguntas abertas para manter o diálogo.

  • Responder de forma adequada utilizando estruturas básicas.

Homework


📝 Elabore um diálogo simples com duas pessoas, incorporando cumprimentos, perguntas e respostas.
🔹 Exemplo:

  • A: "Hello! How are you?"

  • B: "Hi! I'm good, thanks. What do you do?"

  • A: "I study English and I work part-time. And you?"

  • B: "I study and enjoy reading in my free time. Nice to meet you!"
