## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** a família **take**: *take off · take on · take up · take over · take after · take back · take in · take out · take down · take apart.* Muitos têm **vários sentidos** — o contexto decide.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** reconhecer o sentido certo pelo contexto e usar os principais com naturalidade.
**Tarefa verificável:** interpretar e usar phrasal verbs com take em contextos diferentes e explicar cada sentido.

## 1. Revisão e aquecimento (5 min)
- Revisão da aula 25: diga *Sales picked up.*
- How many phrasal verbs with *take* do you know?
- Who do you take after in your family?

## 2. Apresentação e exemplos (10 min)
### take off (4 sentidos)
1. decolar · *The plane took off.* · 2. tirar (roupa) · *Take off your shoes.* · 3. fazer sucesso rápido · *Her career really took off.* · 4. tirar folga · *I'm taking Friday off.*
### take on (3)
1. assumir (trabalho) · *I can't take on more work.* · 2. contratar · *The company is taking on new staff.* · 3. enfrentar · *She took on the champion.*
### take up (3)
1. começar (hobby) · *I took up yoga.* · 2. ocupar (espaço/tempo) · *This sofa takes up too much space.* · 3. aceitar (oferta) · *I'll take you up on that.*
### take over
- assumir o controle · *A big company took over our firm.* · *Can you take over while I'm at lunch?* · *a takeover* (substantivo)
### take after
- puxar a (parecer-se com um familiar) · *She takes after her mother.*
### take back (2)
1. devolver · *I took the shirt back.* · 2. retirar o que disse · *I take it back — you were right.*
### take in (3)
1. entender, absorver · *It was a lot to take in.* · 2. acolher · *They took in a stray cat.* · 3. enganar · *Don't be taken in by the ad.*
### take out (2)
1. tirar / levar para fora · *Take out the trash.* · 2. levar para sair (encontro) · *He took her out to dinner.*
### take down / take apart
- **take down** — anotar / desmontar / remover · *Take down this number.* · *Take down the poster.* · **take apart** — desmontar (peças)

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- *The PLANE TOOK OFF.* · *She TAKES AFter her MOTHer.* · *I TAKE it BACK.* · *It was a LOT to TAKE IN.*
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> I took up running, took on a new project and took Friday off.

## 3. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 3.1 Qual sentido? (escolha o número)
1. *We're taking on ten new engineers.* (take on: assumir / contratar / enfrentar) · 2. *The news was a lot to take in.* (take in: entender / acolher / enganar) · 3. *Take off your jacket.* (take off: decolar / tirar roupa / sucesso) · 4. *I'm taking Monday off.* · 5. *Don't be taken in by fake reviews.*
### 3.2 Complete com o take certo
1. *My sister really ______ after our grandmother.* · 2. *Can you ______ over the meeting? I have to leave.* · 3. *Let me ______ down your email address.* · 4. *They ______ in two refugees.* (formal/neutral)
### 3.3 Troque e fale
> I **took up** **guitar** last year.
> → took on / extra work → took off / two weeks in August → took apart / my old computer

## 4. Tarefa comunicativa (15 min)
