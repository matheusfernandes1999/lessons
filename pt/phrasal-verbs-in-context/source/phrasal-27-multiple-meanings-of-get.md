## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** a família **get**: *get up · get on · get off · get over · get by · get along · get around · get away · get back · get through · get across · get at · get ahead · get behind · get out of · get round to (UK) / get around to (US).*
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** reconhecer o sentido certo de *get + partícula* pelo contexto e usar os principais.
**Tarefa verificável:** interpretar e usar phrasal verbs com get em contextos diferentes e explicar cada sentido.

## 2. Apresentação e exemplos (10 min)
### Movimento
- **get up** — levantar · **get on / get off** — embarcar/desembarcar (ônibus, trem) · **get in / get out of** — entrar/sair (carro) · **get around** — se locomover · **get away** — escapar / viajar para descansar · *We got away for the weekend.* · **get back** — voltar
### Relações e progresso
- **get along (with)** — dar-se bem · **get on (with)** — (UK) dar-se bem / continuar · *Get on with your work.* · **get ahead** — progredir (na carreira) · **get behind** — atrasar-se (no trabalho, pagamentos)
### Dificuldades
- **get over** — superar · *I can't get over how beautiful it is!* (= não acredito) · **get through** — passar por (algo difícil) / conseguir contato · *We got through a tough year.* · **get by** — se virar · **get out of** — escapar de (obrigação) · *I got out of the meeting.*
### Comunicação
- **get across** — transmitir (ideia) · **get at** — querer dizer · *What are you getting at?* · **get through to** — fazer entender · **get back to** — retornar (contato)
### Adiar
- **get around to** (US) / **get round to** (UK) — finalmente fazer (algo adiado) · *I never got around to calling him.*
### Um verbo, vários sentidos
- **get through:** *We got through the exam.* (passar) · *I couldn't get through to the bank.* (ligar) · *We got through three pizzas.* (consumir)
- **get on:** *Get on the bus.* (embarcar) · *How are you getting on?* (UK: como vai indo?) · *I get on well with her.* (UK: dar-se bem)

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- *I can't GET OVer it!* · *What are you GETting AT?* · *We GOT aWAY for the weekend.*
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> We got through a tough year, but we finally got away for a week.

## 3. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 3.1 Qual sentido?
1. *We got through three pizzas.* · 2. *I couldn't get through to customer service.* · 3. *She got through a difficult divorce.* · 4. *I can't get over how cheap it is!* · 5. *He got out of washing the dishes.*
### 3.2 Complete
1. *I'm falling ______ with my payments.* (get behind → *getting*) · 2. *If you want to get ______, you need new skills.* · 3. *I'll get ______ to you by Friday.* · 4. *She gets ______ well with everyone.*
### 3.3 Troque e fale
> I **got over** **the flu** in a week.
> → got through / a tough exam period → got away / to the mountains → finally got around to / cleaning my closet
