## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** phrasal verbs de **decisões** e **oportunidades**: *think over · weigh up (UK) · narrow down · settle on · go for · opt out · back out · turn down · take up (an offer) · jump at · pass up · miss out (on) · come along · hold off · decide on · go ahead.*
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** pensar sobre opções, aceitar ou recusar oportunidades e explicar decisões.
**Tarefa verificável:** discutir uma oportunidade de carreira, pesar opções e decidir usando phrasal verbs de decisão.

## 1. Revisão e aquecimento (5 min)
- Revisão da aula 23: diga algo que *cheers you up*.
- What was a big decision you made recently?
- Have you ever missed an opportunity?

## 2. Apresentação e exemplos (10 min)
### Pensar
- **think over / think through** — pensar com calma · *Let me think it over.* · **weigh up** (UK) / *weigh* (US) — pesar (prós e contras) · **narrow down** — reduzir opções · *I've narrowed it down to two jobs.* · **mull over** — refletir longamente
### Decidir
- **settle on / decide on** — decidir por · *We settled on the blue design.* · **go for** — escolher, ir em frente · *I'll go for the cheaper option.* · **go ahead (with)** — prosseguir · *We're going ahead with the plan.* · **hold off (on)** — esperar antes de decidir · *Let's hold off on buying until prices drop.*
### Oportunidades — aceitar
- **jump at** — agarrar (com entusiasmo) · *She jumped at the chance to work in New York.* · **take up** (an offer) — aceitar · *I took them up on their offer.* · **come along** — surgir · *A great opportunity came along.*
### Oportunidades — recusar ou perder
- **turn down** — recusar · *He turned down the job.* · **pass up** — deixar passar · *Don't pass up this chance!* · **miss out (on)** — perder (algo bom) · *You'll miss out on the fun.* · **opt out (of)** — optar por não participar · **back out (of)** — desistir (depois de aceitar)
### Mini texto
> *Last month, a job offer in Seattle came along. I didn't jump at it immediately — I thought it over for a week and weighed up the pros and cons. I narrowed my choices down to two: stay or go. In the end, I went for it. My friend turned down a similar offer last year and still feels he missed out.*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- *Let me THINK it OVer.* · *I'll GO FOR it.* · *He TURNED it DOWN.* · *Don't MISS OUT.*
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> I thought it over, weighed up the options and decided to go for it.

### 3.2 Complete
1. *I've ______ it down to two universities.* · 2. *She ______ down the promotion because she didn't want to move.* · 3. *We're going ______ with the launch next week.* · 4. *Let's hold ______ on the decision until Friday.* · 5. *A great opportunity came ______.*

> **A job offer in Toronto** came along, and I **jumped at it**.

## 4. Tarefa comunicativa (15 min)
**Oportunidade fictícia:** você recebeu uma oferta para trabalhar 2 anos em **Boston**: salário 30% maior, mas longe da família e com um custo de vida alto.
**Parte 1 — Pensar (6 min):** com o professor (amigo), *think it over*, *weigh up* prós e contras e *narrow down* as opções.
**Parte 2 — Decidir (4 min):** decida (*go for it / turn it down / hold off*) e explique.
**Parte 3 — Reação (5 min):** o professor muda o cenário (*They also offered a remote option*). O aluno reavalia e responde ao recrutador (aceitar, recusar ou pedir mais tempo).
**Professor anota:** 10 phrasal verbs ☐ · prós e contras ☐ · decisão justificada ☐ · resposta ao recrutador ☐
