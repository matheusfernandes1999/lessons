## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** phrasal verbs de **mudança** e **melhoria**: *speed up · slow down · step up · scale up/down · build up · cut down · phase in/out · do away with · bring about · turn around · pick up · level off · catch on · die out · shake up.*
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** descrever transformações em empresas, cidades e hábitos e propor melhorias.
**Tarefa verificável:** apresentar uma proposta de melhoria, comparando antes e depois com phrasal verbs de mudança.

## 1. Revisão e aquecimento (5 min)
- Revisão da aula 24: diga *I thought it over and went for it.*
- What has changed in your city in the last 10 years?
- What would you like to improve in your routine?

## 2. Apresentação e exemplos (10 min)
### Mais rápido / mais lento
- **speed up** — acelerar · *We need to speed up the process.* · **slow down** — desacelerar · **step up** — intensificar · *We stepped up our marketing.*
### Maior / menor
- **scale up / scale down** — ampliar / reduzir a escala · **build up** — aumentar aos poucos · **cut down (on)** — reduzir
### Introduzir / eliminar
- **phase in / phase out** — introduzir / eliminar gradualmente · *Paper forms are being phased out.* · **do away with** — acabar com · *They did away with the dress code.* · **bring in** — introduzir (regra, sistema)
### Causar / transformar
- **bring about** — provocar, causar (mudança) · *The new CEO brought about big changes.* · **turn around** — reverter (situação ruim) · *She turned the company around.* · **shake up** — reorganizar radicalmente · *a shake-up*
### Tendências
- **pick up** — melhorar, crescer · *Sales picked up in spring.* · **level off** — estabilizar · **catch on** — pegar, virar moda · *The idea caught on quickly.* · **die out** — desaparecer · *Some traditions are dying out.*
### Mini texto
> *Three years ago, the company was in trouble. The new director shook up the team, did away with old processes and phased in a new system. At first, sales slowed down, but then they picked up. Remote work caught on, so they scaled down the office. Today, profits have leveled off (US; UK levelled), but the company has completely turned around.*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- *We need to SPEED it UP.* · *SALES PICKED UP.* · *It CAUGHT ON.* · *She TURNED it aROUND.*
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> The new director shook up the team, and sales picked up within a year.

## 3. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 3.1 Aumentar, diminuir, introduzir, eliminar ou tendência?
> *scale up · phase out · pick up · cut down · bring in · die out · step up · level off*
### 3.2 Complete com o mini texto
1. *What did the new director do?* · 2. *What happened to sales?* · 3. *Why did they scale down the office?*
### 3.3 Troque e fale: antes e depois
> **Before**, the process was slow. **Now**, we've **sped it up**.
> → we used paper forms / we've phased them out → the team was small / we've scaled it up → nobody used the app / it's caught on
