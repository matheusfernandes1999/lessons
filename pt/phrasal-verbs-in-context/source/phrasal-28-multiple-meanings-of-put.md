## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** a família **put**: *put on · put off · put up · put up with · put away · put down · put out · put through · put forward · put together · put aside · put across · put back.*
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** reconhecer o sentido certo de *put + partícula* pelo contexto e usar os principais.
**Tarefa verificável:** interpretar e usar phrasal verbs com put em contextos diferentes e explicar cada sentido.

## 1. Revisão e aquecimento (5 min)
- Revisão da aula 27: diga *I never got around to it.*
- How many phrasal verbs with *put* do you know?
- What's something you always put off?

## 2. Apresentação e exemplos (10 min)
### put on (3)
1. vestir · *Put on your coat.* · 2. ligar (luz, música) · *Put some music on.* · 3. engordar / ganhar · *I put on two kilos.* · 4. montar (um show/evento) · *The school put on a play.*
### put off (2)
1. adiar · *Don't put it off.* · 2. desanimar, causar aversão · *The smell put me off.* · *I was put off by the price.*
### put up (3)
1. hospedar · *Can you put me up for a night?* · 2. montar, pendurar · *We put up a tent / a poster.* · 3. aumentar (preço, UK) · *They put up the rent.*
### put up with
- aguentar, tolerar · *I can't put up with the noise anymore.*
### put away / put aside / put back
- **put away** — guardar · *Put your toys away.* · **put aside** — reservar (dinheiro, tempo) / deixar de lado · *Let's put our differences aside.* · **put back** — devolver ao lugar / adiar · *The meeting was put back a week.*
### put down (3)
1. pôr no chão/mesa · *Put the box down.* · 2. anotar · *Put your name down.* · 3. humilhar, criticar · *He's always putting people down.*
### put out (2)
1. apagar (fogo, cigarro) · *Put out the fire.* · 2. incomodar · *I don't want to put you out.*
### put through / put forward / put together / put across
- **put through** — transferir (ligação) / fazer passar por · *I'll put you through.* · *They put us through a tough test.* · **put forward** — propor · **put together** — montar, organizar · **put across** — transmitir (ideia)

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- *Don't PUT it OFF.* · *I can't PUT UP with it.* · *Can you PUT me UP?* · *PUT it aWAY.*
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> I can't put up with the noise, so I've put off the meeting and put on some music.

## 3. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 3.1 Qual sentido?
1. *I put on three kilos on vacation.* · 2. *They put on a great concert.* · 3. *We put up a tent by the lake.* · 4. *The landlord put up the rent.* · 5. *I don't want to put you out.* · 6. *The launch was put back to June.*
### 3.2 Complete com a partícula certa
1. *I've put ______ some money for the trip.* · 2. *She put ______ an interesting proposal.* · 3. *Can you put your phone ______ during dinner?* · 4. *I can't put ______ ______ his jokes anymore.*
### 3.3 Troque e fale
> I **put off** **going to the dentist** for months.
> → put up with / a noisy neighbor → put aside / $100 every month → put together / a great team

## 4. Tarefa comunicativa (15 min)
**Parte 1 — Sentido pelo contexto (5 min):** o professor diz 10 frases com put; o aluno explica o sentido em inglês simples.
**Parte 2 — Perguntas pessoais (6 min):** responda com um phrasal verb com put diferente:
- *What do you always put off?* · *What can't you put up with?* · *Have you ever put someone up at your home?* · *What are you putting aside money for?* · *What puts you off a restaurant?*
**Parte 3 — Revisão take/get/put (4 min):** o professor diz uma situação; o aluno escolhe o phrasal verb de *take, get* ou *put* (*the plane left the ground → took off · I tolerate the noise → put up with · I recovered → got over*).
**Professor anota:** sentidos corretos __/10 · respostas variadas ☐ · revisão take/get/put ☐
