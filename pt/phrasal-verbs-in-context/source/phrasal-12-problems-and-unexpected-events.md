## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** phrasal verbs de **problemas** e **imprevistos**: *break down · run out of · come up · go wrong · mix up · hold up · put out · deal with · sort out · cope with · get over · bounce back · go off.*
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** contar imprevistos e explicar como lidou com eles.
**Tarefa verificável:** relatar um dia de imprevistos e explicar como resolveu cada um usando phrasal verbs.

## 1. Revisão e aquecimento (5 min)
- Revisão da aula 11: diga algo que você *took up* ou *gave up*.
- What's the worst day you've had recently?
- How do you usually react to problems?

## 2. Apresentação e exemplos (10 min)
### Quando algo dá errado
- **break down** — quebrar (máquina, carro) · *My car broke down on the highway.* · **go wrong** — dar errado · *Everything went wrong today.* · **go off** — disparar (alarme) / estragar (comida, UK) · *The fire alarm went off.* · **run out of** — acabar · *We ran out of gas.* (US *gas*; UK *petrol*) · **come up** — surgir · *Something came up at work.*
### Confusões e atrasos
- **mix up** — confundir · *I mixed up the dates.* · *a mix-up* (substantivo) · **hold up** — atrasar · *a hold-up* · **lock out** — trancar do lado de fora · *I locked myself out.*
### Lidar
- **deal with** — lidar com · **sort out** — resolver · **cope with** — aguentar, lidar com (dificuldade) · **put out** — apagar (fogo) · **figure out** (aula 10)
### Recuperar-se
- **get over** — superar · *It took me a week to get over the stress.* · **bounce back** — se recuperar · **calm down** — acalmar-se
### Mini texto
> *Yesterday was a disaster. My alarm didn't go off, so I woke up late. Then my car broke down, and I had to deal with the insurance company. At work, there was a mix-up with the client's order — someone had mixed up two addresses. We sorted it out by lunchtime. When I got home, I realized I'd locked myself out! (US realized; UK realised) Luckily, my neighbor (US; UK neighbour) had a spare key.*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- *My car BROKE DOWN.* · *I MIXED UP the dates.* · *We SORTED it OUT.* · *I LOCKED myself OUT.*
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> My car broke down, but I sorted it out and got over the stress quickly.

## 3. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 3.1 Problema ou solução?
> *break down · sort out · run out of · deal with · go wrong · bounce back · mix up · calm down*
### 3.2 Complete com o mini texto
1. *Why did the person wake up late?* · 2. *What happened to the car?* · 3. *What was the mix-up at work?* · 4. *How did they get into the house?*
### 3.3 Troque e fale
> **My laptop** **broke down**, so I **had to deal with** **IT**.
> → We ran out of / coffee / go to the store → The alarm went off / leave the building → I mixed up / the meeting times / apologize to the client

## 4. Tarefa comunicativa (15 min)
**Parte 1 — Dia de azar (7 min):** conte um dia (real ou inventado) com pelo menos 4 imprevistos e como lidou com cada um. Use **10 phrasal verbs**.
**Parte 2 — Reagir (4 min):** o professor conta 3 imprevistos; o aluno reage e sugere soluções (*Oh no! Did you manage to sort it out? You should…*).
**Parte 3 — Ligação (4 min):** o carro do aluno *broke down*; ele liga para o seguro (professor) e explica o problema.
**Professor anota:** 10 phrasal verbs ☐ · sequência ☐ · reações ☐ · ligação ☐

## Homework da semana (7 dias)
Estude **10–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Caderno.** Anote os phrasal verbs em 4 grupos: dar errado, confusões, lidar, recuperar.
**Dia 2 — Frases.** Escreva uma frase pessoal para cada.
**Dia 3 — História.** Escreva seu dia de azar (120 palavras).
**Dia 4 — Mensagens.** Escreva 4 mensagens avisando de imprevistos (*Sorry, my car broke down…*).
**Dia 5 — Escuta.** Assista a uma cena de comédia com imprevistos e anote 4 phrasal verbs.
**Dia 6 — Gravação.** Grave 60–90 s contando seu dia de azar.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
