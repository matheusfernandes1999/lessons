## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** phrasal verbs da **rotina**: *wake up · get up · sleep in · get dressed · put on · head out · get to work · get back · chill out · wind down · go to bed · stay up · doze off.*
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** contar seu dia do começo ao fim com phrasal verbs naturais.
**Tarefa verificável:** descrever a rotina diária e comparar com a de outra pessoa usando phrasal verbs.

## 1. Revisão e aquecimento (5 min)
- Revisão da aula 2: diga *Turn it off* e *Look after them*.
- Are you a morning person?
- What's the first thing you do after you wake up?

## 2. Apresentação e exemplos (10 min)
### Manhã
- **wake up** (acordar) · **get up** (levantar) · **sleep in** (dormir até mais tarde) · **turn off** the alarm · **get dressed** (não é phrasal, mas útil) · **put on** clothes/makeup · **make up** for lost time (compensar — outro sentido!) · **head out / head off** (sair de casa) · **run late** (estar atrasado)
### Trabalho / estudo
- **get to** work/school (chegar) · **log in / log on** (entrar no sistema) · **check in** (fazer check-in / dar notícias) · **knock off** (informal: parar de trabalhar) · **clock in / clock out** (bater ponto)
### Tarde e noite
- **get back** (home) (voltar) · **pick up** the kids · **eat out** (comer fora) · **chill out** (relaxar, informal) · **wind down** (desacelerar antes de dormir) · **stay up** (ficar acordado até tarde) · **go to bed** · **doze off / nod off** (cochilar sem querer) · **set** the alarm (não é phrasal)
### Mini texto
> *On weekdays, I wake up at 6:30, but I don't get up until 6:45. I put on my running shoes and head out for a quick run. I get to work at 8:30 and log in. I usually knock off at 5:30 and get back home by 6:30. In the evening, I wind down with a book, but sometimes I stay up too late watching series and doze off on the couch. On Saturdays, I sleep in!*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- *I WAKE UP at six.* · *I SLEEP IN on Saturdays.* · *I DOZED OFF on the couch.* (*couch* = US; UK: *sofa*)
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> I wake up at six-thirty, but I don't get up until six forty-five.

## 3. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 3.1 Ordene o dia
> *go to bed · get up · get back home · wake up · head out · get to work · wind down · knock off*
### 3.2 Complete com o mini texto
1. *What time does the person get up?* · 2. *What does he/she do in the evening?* · 3. *What happens on Saturdays?*
### 3.3 Troque e fale
> On weekdays, I **wake up** at **7**.
> → get to work / at 9 → get back home / at 7 → stay up / until midnight → On Sundays, I sleep in / until 10

## 4. Tarefa comunicativa (15 min)
**Parte 1 — Meu dia (5 min):** descreva um dia típico da semana com pelo menos **10 phrasal verbs**.
**Parte 2 — Entrevista (5 min):** entreviste o professor sobre a rotina dele (*What time do you usually get up? Do you ever sleep in? How do you wind down?*). Anote.
**Parte 3 — Comparação (5 min):** compare as duas rotinas (*I get up earlier than you · We both stay up late · Unlike you, I eat out a lot*).
**Professor anota:** 10 phrasal verbs ☐ · perguntas corretas ☐ · comparação ☐ · pronúncia da partícula ☐
