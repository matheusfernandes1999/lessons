## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** phrasal verbs de **dinheiro**: *pay off · pay back · save up · put aside · cut back (on) · cut down (on) · run up (a bill) · shell out · rip off · add up · break even · come to · live off · get by.*
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** falar de gastos, dívidas, economia e orçamento com naturalidade.
**Tarefa verificável:** montar e discutir um orçamento mensal e um plano de economia usando phrasal verbs de dinheiro.

## 2. Apresentação e exemplos (10 min)
### Economizar
- **save up (for)** — juntar dinheiro · *I'm saving up for a car.* · **put aside / set aside** — separar (dinheiro) · *I put aside $200 a month.* · **cut back (on) / cut down (on)** — reduzir · *I'm cutting back on eating out.*
### Gastar
- **shell out (for)** (informal) — gastar (muito) · *I shelled out $1,000 for a new phone.* · **run up** (a bill / debt) — acumular · *He ran up a huge credit card bill.* · **rip off** — cobrar demais (*a rip-off*) · *That taxi driver ripped us off.*
### Dívidas
- **pay back** — devolver (dinheiro emprestado) · *I'll pay you back on Friday.* · **pay off** — quitar · *I finally paid off my student loan.* (também: *valer a pena*) · **pay for** — pagar por
### Contas e totais
- **add up** — somar / fazer sentido · *The numbers don't add up.* · **come to** — totalizar · *The bill came to $85.* · **break even** — empatar (sem lucro nem prejuízo) · **work out** — calcular / custar · *It works out to $10 a day.*
### Sobreviver
- **get by (on)** — se virar · *It's hard to get by on minimum wage.* · **live off** — viver de · *She lives off her savings.* · **scrape by** — viver com muito pouco
### Mini texto
> *Last year I ran up a big credit card bill, so this year I'm cutting back. I put aside $300 every month and I've already paid off half the debt. I'm also saving up for a trip to Canada. It's not easy to get by on a tight budget, but the numbers finally add up.*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- *I'm SAVing UP for a car.* · *I'll PAY you BACK.* · *I'm CUTting BACK.* · *The bill CAME to $85.*
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> I'm cutting back on eating out and saving up for a trip.

## 3. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 3.1 Economizar, gastar, dívida ou total?
> *put aside · shell out · pay off · add up · run up · cut down on · come to · pay back*
### 3.2 Complete com o mini texto
1. *What did the person run up last year?* · 2. *How much do they put aside every month?* · 3. *What are they saving up for?*
### 3.3 Troque e fale
> I'm **cutting back on** **coffee** to **save up for** **a new laptop**.
> → eating out / a vacation → online shopping / a car → taxis / an apartment

## 4. Tarefa comunicativa (15 min)
**Orçamento fictício:** Alex mora em Austin, ganha $3,200/mês. Gastos: aluguel $1,400 · comida $500 · delivery e restaurantes $450 · transporte $200 · assinaturas $90 · cartão de crédito (dívida de $2,000).
**Parte 1 — Análise (6 min):** com o professor, analise o orçamento: o que Alex deve *cut back on*, quanto pode *put aside*, em quanto tempo pode *pay off* a dívida.
**Parte 2 — Plano (5 min):** monte um plano para Alex *save up for* uma viagem de $1,500.
**Parte 3 — Pessoal (4 min):** fale dos seus hábitos (sem detalhes privados): *What do you cut back on? What would you save up for?*
