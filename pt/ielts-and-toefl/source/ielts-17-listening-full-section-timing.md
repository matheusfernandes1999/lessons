## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** fazer as **4 partes do Listening** em sequência, como na prova: ler perguntas nos intervalos, não parar quando perde uma resposta e **transferir/revisar** com cuidado.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** manter a concentração por todo o bloco e classificar seus erros.
**Tarefa verificável:** completar seção de prática cronometrada e revisar erros.
**Observação:** versão reduzida (4 partes curtas). Faça um Listening completo oficial fora da aula nesta semana.

## 2. Checklist de prova (3 min)
- **Antes de cada parte:** leia as perguntas, sublinhe palavras-chave, preveja o tipo de resposta.
- **Durante:** escreva rápido, siga em frente, atenção a *but / actually*.
- **Depois:** ortografia, plural, limite de palavras, maiúsculas em nomes próprios.
- **Tipos de erro:** **S** som/número · **O** ortografia · **L** limite de palavras · **D** distrator · **P** perdeu o lugar

## 3. Bloco de Listening (24 min)
🎧 **Professor:** leia cada parte uma vez, com 30 s de leitura antes de cada uma.
### Part 1 — Form completion (ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER)
> **Woodside Community Centre — Course Booking**
> Course: (1) ______ for beginners · Day: (2) ______ · Time: 6:30–(3) ______ · Cost: £(4) ______ for 8 weeks · Bring: (5) ______
> “Hi, I'm calling about your courses. — Of course. Which one? — The pottery course for beginners. — Lovely. That's on Mondays… sorry, it's moved to Thursdays this term. It starts at half past six and finishes at eight fifteen. — How much is it? — It's ninety-five pounds for the eight weeks, which includes all the clay. You just need to bring an apron — it gets messy!”
### Part 2 — Multiple choice
6. *What is new at the library this year?* A) longer opening hours B) a café C) a children's area
7. *What should visitors do to use the computers?* A) book online B) ask at reception C) bring their own laptop
> “Welcome to the Central Library. Many of you have asked if we'll extend our opening hours — I'm afraid that's not possible yet. However, I'm happy to say that the new children's area on the ground floor opened in March. We were also planning a café, but that's been postponed until next year. If you want to use one of our computers, you don't need to book online any more — just ask at reception and they'll give you a code.”
### Part 3 — Matching (A, B or C)
> **Who suggests each idea?** A Leo B Nina C both
8. use videos in the presentation ___ 9. interview a local business ___ 10. shorten the introduction ___
> **Leo:** I think we should include a couple of short videos — it'd make the presentation more lively. **Nina:** Definitely, I was going to say the same. And what about interviewing the owner of the bakery near campus? **Leo:** Good idea, I hadn't thought of that. **Nina:** Also, I think your introduction is a bit long. **Leo:** Fair enough, I'll cut it down.
### Part 4 — Note completion (ONE WORD ONLY)
> **Desert Plants**
> - Cactus stores water in its (11) ______
> - Spines reduce water loss and protect from (12) ______
> - Some seeds can wait for years until there is (13) ______
> - Roots: either very deep or very (14) ______ to catch rain quickly
> “Desert plants have developed remarkable strategies. The cactus, for example, stores water in its thick stem, while its spines — which are actually modified leaves — reduce water loss and protect it from animals. Other plants survive as seeds, which can stay dormant for years until there is enough rain. Roots are also adapted: some go extremely deep to reach underground water, while others are very shallow and spread widely, so they can catch rain the moment it falls.”
