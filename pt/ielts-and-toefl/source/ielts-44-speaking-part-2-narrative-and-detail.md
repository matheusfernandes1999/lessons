## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** muitos cartões da Part 2 pedem **histórias** (*a time when…, an event…*). Para band 7, use **tempos narrativos variados** (passado simples, contínuo, perfeito), **detalhes descritivos** e **sentimentos**.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** contar uma experiência em 2 minutos com sequência clara, detalhes vivos e gramática variada — sem decorar texto.
**Tarefa verificável:** executar long turn claro sem memorizar redação.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Conte em 30 s: *what you did yesterday evening.* Depois o professor pede: **“Add details!”** (onde? com quem? o que você viu/ouviu/sentiu?) e o aluno conta de novo em 45 s.
**Revisão da aula 43:** quais são as 4 partes da estrutura de uma fala de Part 2?

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Tempos narrativos
- **Passado simples** (ações principais): *We arrived at 8 and went straight to the beach.*
- **Passado contínuo** (cenário/ação em andamento): *It was raining and people were running for cover.*
- **Passado perfeito** (algo antes): *I had never seen snow before.*
- **Used to / would** (hábitos no passado): *We would spend every summer at my grandparents'.*
### Sequência
- *At first · Then · After a while · Suddenly · All of a sudden · Eventually · In the end · By the time…*
### Detalhes vivos
- **Sentidos:** *I could hear… · It smelled of… · The view was breathtaking*
- **Sentimentos:** *I was thrilled / nervous / relieved / disappointed / embarrassed*
- **Adjetivos fortes:** *exhausted (very tired) · freezing (very cold) · delighted (very happy) · terrified (very scared) · stunning (very beautiful)*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- -ed: *relieved* /d/ · *exhausted* /ɪd/ · *embarrassed* /t/ · *terrified* /d/
- *had never seen* → *əd-NEV-er-SEEN* · *all of a sudden* → *ALL-ə-və-SUD-den*
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> All of a sudden, while we were walking, it started pouring, and by the time we got home we were absolutely freezing.

:::quiz vocab

? w|grammar|It ______ raining when we left. (be — passado contínuo)

> sudden

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Esqueleto de uma história (2 min)
1. **Contexto** (quando, onde, quem) — passado simples/contínuo
2. **O que aconteceu** (2–3 eventos) — sequência + detalhes
3. **Ponto alto/problema** — *suddenly, then…*
4. **Final + sentimento + “explain”** — *In the end… I felt… The reason it was memorable…*
### Modelo
> **Cue card:** *Describe a time when you got lost.* You should say: where you were · how you got lost · what you did · and explain how you felt.
> *I'd like to tell you about the time I got lost in Lisbon, about three years ago. I was there on a work trip, and on my last evening I decided to explore the old neighbourhood of Alfama on my own.*
> *At first, everything was fine — I was taking photos of the colourful houses and listening to music coming from little restaurants. But the streets there are like a maze: narrow, steep and full of stairs. After a while, I realised I had no idea where I was, and to make things worse, my phone battery had died.*
> *I was starting to feel quite nervous because it was getting dark. Eventually, I went into a small bakery and, with my basic Portuguese — well, Brazilian Portuguese — I asked for directions. The owner was incredibly kind; she drew me a little map on a napkin.*
> *In the end, I found my hotel about an hour later, absolutely exhausted but relieved. Looking back, I actually feel grateful it happened, because it was the most authentic moment of the whole trip.*

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: cenário + ação
> **I was walking home** **when** **it started to rain**.
> → I was cooking dinner / the power went off → We were driving to the beach / the car broke down → I was waiting for the bus / I saw an old friend
### 4.2 Passado perfeito
> *By the time I arrived, the train **had** already **left**.*
> → By the time we got to the cinema / the film / start → When I opened the fridge / someone / eat my cake → I was nervous because I / never / give a presentation in English
### 4.3 Adicione detalhes (sentidos + sentimento)
Transforme: *“I went to a concert. It was good.”* em 4 frases com o que você **viu, ouviu, sentiu**.

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**. 1 min notas + 2 min fala, grave.
**Dia 1 — Banco de histórias.** Liste 8 histórias reais que podem servir para vários cartões (uma viagem, um problema, uma surpresa, uma conquista, ajudar alguém…).
**Dia 2 — Cartão:** *Describe a time you made a mistake.*
**Dia 3 — Cartão:** *Describe a journey that took longer than expected.*
**Dia 4 — Cartão:** *Describe a time you were proud of yourself.*
**Dia 5 — Escute.** Marque nos áudios onde usou passado contínuo/perfeito. Faltou?
**Dia 6 — Adaptação.** Use **a mesma história** do banco para 2 cartões diferentes (ex.: “a surprise” e “a journey”).
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
