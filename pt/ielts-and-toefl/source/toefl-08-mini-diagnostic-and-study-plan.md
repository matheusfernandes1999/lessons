## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** medir o que melhorou desde a aula 1 e montar um **plano de estudo semanal realista**.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** dizer em inglês quais são seus pontos fortes, seus gargalos e o que vai fazer em cada dia da semana para melhorar.
**Tarefa verificável:** executar uma miniavaliação cronometrada e produzir um plano de estudo.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- What was the most useful lesson so far? Why?
- What is easier now than in lesson 1?
- How many minutes a day can you really study?
**Frases úteis:**
- *I've improved my…* · *I'm still struggling with…* · *I can study about … minutes a day.*
**Troque e fale:**
> I've improved my **pronunciation**, but I'm still struggling with **listening**.
> → vocabulary / grammar → reading speed / fast speech → sentence structure / articles

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
Palavras para planejar estudos:
- **goal** — objetivo · **realistic** — realista · **routine** — rotina
- **consistent** — constante · **progress** — progresso · **track** — acompanhar/registrar
- **bottleneck** — gargalo · **struggle with** — ter dificuldade com
- **set aside (time)** — reservar tempo — *I set aside 20 minutes every morning.*
- **review** — revisar · **mock test** — simulado · **accuracy** — precisão
**Chunks para o plano:**
- *On Mondays, I'm going to…*
- *I'll spend 15 minutes on…*
- *My goal for this month is to…*
- *I'll track my progress by…*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **goal / gold / go** — goal termina em /l/ escuro: “gôul”.
- **track / truck / trick** — *track* /æ/, *truck* /ʌ/, *trick* /ɪ/.
- **review / revue** — re-VIEW (tônica no final).
- **progress** (n) = PRO-gress · **progress** (v) = pro-GRESS
- **routine** = rou-TINE (tônica no final, “i” longo).
Frase: > My goal is to track my progress and review every day.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Um bom plano tem 4 partes
1. **Meta clara e datada:** *Get a 4.5 in Listening by March.*
2. **Tempo real:** quantos minutos por dia você **realmente** tem.
3. **Tarefas concretas por dia:** não “estudar listening”, mas “ouvir 2 anúncios e anotar datas e mudanças”.
4. **Registro:** como você vai medir (acertos, tempo, gravações).

### Modelo — fale junto com o professor
> My goal is to reach 4.5 in all sections by March. My strength is reading, but my bottleneck is listening to fast speech. I can study for about 30 minutes on weekdays and one hour on Saturdays. On Mondays and Wednesdays, I'm going to practice listening with short announcements. On Tuesdays and Thursdays, I'll record myself answering interview questions. On Fridays, I'll review vocabulary, and on Saturdays, I'll do a timed practice set. I'll track my progress in a spreadsheet.

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale (planos)
> On **Mondays**, I'm going to **practice listening**.
> → Tuesdays / record my answers → Wednesdays / read two notices → Thursdays / write an email → Fridays / review vocabulary
> I'll spend **15 minutes** on **vocabulary**.
> → 20 minutes / shadowing → half an hour / reading → 10 minutes / pronunciation

## 5. Miniavaliação cronometrada (15 min)
### A. Reading — Complete the Words (3 min)
> Most univer___ (1) offer free tutor___ (2) for first-year students. Students can book a ses___ (3) online and meet a tutor in the libr___ (4). These sessions are especially use___ (5) before final exa___ (6).
**Acertos:** __/6

### B. Reading — aviso (2 min)
> **Writing Center — New Hours.** Starting October 3, the Writing Center will open at 10 a.m. instead of 9 a.m. Evening sessions (6–8 p.m.) will continue on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Walk-ins are welcome, but students with appointments have priority.
1. What changes on October 3? 2. When are evening sessions? 3. Who has priority?
**Acertos:** __/3

### C. Listening — Choose a Response (2 min)
🎧 Professor lê cada fala uma vez; o aluno escolhe a melhor resposta.
1. “Have you finished the reading for today?” — a) Not yet, I'm on page ten. b) Yes, I'm reading. c) It's a good book.
2. “Could you lend me your notes?” — a) I wrote them. b) Sure, here you go. c) Notes are important.
3. “The seminar was canceled.” — a) Really? Why? b) I canceled it. c) The seminar is good.
**Acertos:** __/3

### D. Speaking (4 min)
**Repita:** 1. The tutoring center is on the second floor. 2. You should book your session at least two days in advance.
**Entrevista (40 s cada):** 3. What is the best way to learn new vocabulary? 4. Describe your study routine.
**Professor (1–3):** clareza __ fluência __ gramática __ vocabulário __

## 7. Homework da semana
Siga **o seu plano** desta semana. Além dele:
**Dia 1 — Plano.** Passe o plano a limpo e cole na parede ou no celular.
**Dia 2 — Speaking.** Grave seu plano em 1 minuto, sem ler.
**Dia 3 — Vocabulário.** Revise as palavras das aulas 1–7. Separe 10 que ainda esquece e faça frases.
**Dia 4 — Listening.** Ouça 3 anúncios ou mensagens curtas em inglês (ex.: avisos de aeroporto, universidade) e anote o quê/quando/o que fazer.
**Dia 5 — Writing.** Escreva um e-mail de 80 palavras a um tutor pedindo uma sessão de ajuda.
**Dia 6 — Pronúncia.** Repita as frases da tarefa de Listen and Repeat 5 vezes cada, gravando a última.
**Dia 7 — Registro.** Anote quantos minutos estudou em cada dia e faça o quiz abaixo.
