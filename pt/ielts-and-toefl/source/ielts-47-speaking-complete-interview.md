## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** **simulado completo** do Speaking (11–14 min), com a progressão real: Part 1 (pessoal e curto) → Part 2 (fala longa) → Part 3 (abstrato e desenvolvido). Depois, avaliação pelos **4 critérios**.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** ajustar o tamanho e o estilo da resposta para cada parte e saber seus pontos fortes e fracos por critério.
**Tarefa verificável:** completar entrevista oral simulada de 11–14 minutos.

## 1. Preparação (3 min)
- Aquecimento de voz: *Red lorry, yellow lorry* (3x) · *She sells sea shells* (3x)
- 30 s: *What did you do this morning?*
**Lembretes:** Part 1 → A+E (2–3 frases) · Part 2 → notas em 1 min, fale até 2 min · Part 3 → O-R-E + nuance (3–5 frases)

## 2. Os 4 critérios (2 min)
- **Fluency and Coherence:** continuidade, ideias ligadas, pouca hesitação
- **Lexical Resource:** variedade e precisão de vocabulário, paráfrase, expressões idiomáticas naturais
- **Grammatical Range and Accuracy:** estruturas variadas (condicionais, perfeitos, relativas) com poucos erros
- **Pronunciation:** clareza, tônica, ritmo, entonação

## 3. Simulado completo (14 min)
**Professor:** faça o papel do examinador, com tom neutro, sem corrigir durante a prova. Cronometre.
### Part 1 (4–5 min)
> *Good afternoon. Can you tell me your full name? … Thank you. Now, let's talk about where you live.*
> 1. Do you live in a house or an apartment? 2. What do you like about the area where you live? 3. Is there anything you would like to change about it?
> *Let's talk about music.* 4. What kind of music do you enjoy? 5. Did you learn to play a musical instrument as a child? 6. Do you prefer listening to music alone or with other people?
> *Now let's talk about mornings.* 7. Are you a morning person? 8. What do you usually do in the morning before work or study?
### Part 2 (3–4 min)
> *Now I'm going to give you a topic, and I'd like you to talk about it for one to two minutes. Before you talk, you'll have one minute to think. You can make notes.*
> **Describe a piece of advice you received that was very useful.** You should say: who gave you the advice · what the advice was · when you received it · and explain why it was useful.
> *(1 min de preparação → até 2 min de fala)*
> **Follow-up:** *Do you often give advice to other people?*
### Part 3 (4–5 min)
> *We've been talking about advice. Let's discuss this topic in more general terms.*
> 1. Who do young people usually ask for advice in your country — parents, friends or the internet? Why?
> 2. Is advice from older people more valuable than advice from people of the same age?
> 3. Why do some people find it difficult to accept advice?
> 4. Do you think professional advisers, such as career coaches, are becoming more important? Why?
> 5. How might the way people get advice change in the future?

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Regrave a Part 2** com 1 min de notas e 2 min de fala.
**Dia 2 — Part 3.** Regrave as 5 perguntas, com O-R-E e nuance.
**Dia 3 — Critério mais fraco.** 20 min de treino específico (ex.: pronúncia → sombra; gramática → condicionais).
**Dia 4 — Simulado novo** (Parts 1–3) com temas: *transport, a book you read, reading in society*.
**Dia 5 — Autoavaliação** do dia 4 com os 4 critérios.
**Dia 6 — Vocabulário.** 15 expressões naturais para usar (*it's not my cup of tea, I'd say, to some extent, in the long run, off the top of my head…*).
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
