## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** fazer as **três tarefas de Reading** em sequência (*Complete the Words*, *Read in Daily Life*, *Read an Academic Passage*), trocando de estratégia rapidamente e controlando o tempo.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** saber quanto tempo gasta por item e onde perde tempo.
**Tarefa verificável:** concluir um conjunto misto e analisar tempo por item.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- Which Reading task is the easiest for you? Which is the hardest?
- What do you do when you don't know an answer? Do you skip it or keep trying?
- How do you feel when there's a timer?
**Troque e fale:**
> When I don't know an answer, I **make my best guess and move on**.
> → eliminate wrong options first → look for a keyword → check the sentence before and after
**Revisão da aula 15:** dê um exemplo de inferência boa e uma ruim.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
Linguagem para falar sobre desempenho:
- **time management** — gestão de tempo · **pace** — ritmo · **on track** — dentro do tempo
- **skip** — pular · **come back to** — voltar a · **guess** — chutar
- **eliminate** — eliminar · **distractor** — alternativa-armadilha
- **careless mistake** — erro por desatenção · **run out of time** — ficar sem tempo
- **get stuck (on)** — travar (em) · **spend (time) on** — gastar (tempo) em
**Chunks para análise:**
- *I spent too much time on…*
- *I got stuck on question…*
- *I made a careless mistake because…*
- *Next time, I'll…*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **pace / place / space** · **skip / ship / sheep** · **guess / guest / get**
- **eliminate** = e-LIM-i-nate · **distractor** = dis-TRAC-tor
- Ligação: *ran out of time* → *ra-nou-tof-time* · *got stuck on it* → *got-stu-ko-nit*
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> I got stuck on it, so I made a guess and moved on.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Mude a estratégia conforme a tarefa
- **Complete the Words:** leia a frase inteira, decida a classe, complete. **Não volte** muitas vezes.
- **Read in Daily Life:** leia título + perguntas, depois **procure** números, datas e palavras perigosas.
- **Academic Passage:** leia a **primeira frase de cada parágrafo** (mapa), depois responda indo ao trecho.
### Regra de ouro do tempo
Se travar em um item, **elimine o que puder, chute e siga em frente**. Um item difícil vale o mesmo que um fácil.
### Modelo de análise (fale junto com o professor)
> I finished the set in 14 minutes. I spent too much time on the academic passage, especially on question 4, because I reread the whole text. I made a careless mistake in Complete the Words: I forgot the plural “-s” in “clouds”. Next time, I'll read the first sentence of each paragraph before the questions.

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)

> I spent too much time on **the academic passage** because **I reread the whole text**.
> → the notice / I read every word → question 2 / I didn't know a word → Complete the Words / I wasn't sure about spelling
> Next time, I'll **read the questions first**.
> → set a time limit per item → skip and come back → underline keywords

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: conjunto misto (15 min)
**O professor anota o tempo de cada parte. O aluno não para o cronômetro.**
### Parte 1 — Complete the Words (meta: 3 min)
> The Amazon rainforest produces a large por___ (1) of its own rain. Trees release wat___ (2) vapor through their leaves, and this vapor forms clo___ (3) that later fall as rain. Scien___ (4) worry that if too many trees are cut do___ (5), this cycle could weaken, and parts of the forest might gradu___ (6) turn into savanna.
**Tempo:** ____ **Acertos:** __/6

### Parte 2 — Read in Daily Life (meta: 4 min)
> **Subject:** Lost & Found – Student Center
> Hello everyone,
> The Lost & Found office has moved from the Student Center basement to Room 104 on the first floor. Items found on campus are kept for 30 days; after that, unclaimed electronics are recycled and clothing is donated to local charities. To claim an item, you must describe it and show your student ID. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. On Fridays, the office closes at noon.
> — Student Center Staff
1. Where is the Lost & Found now? 2. What happens to unclaimed clothing? 3. What do you need to claim an item? 4. Can you claim something on Friday at 2 p.m.?
**Tempo:** ____ **Acertos:** __/4

### Parte 3 — Academic Passage (meta: 6 min)
> (1) For centuries, people believed that the Great Wall of China was a single, continuous wall. In fact, it is a collection of many walls built by different dynasties over almost two thousand years.
> (2) The earliest sections were made of compressed earth and wood. Later, during the Ming dynasty (1368–1644), builders used bricks and stone, which is why the Ming sections are the best preserved today.
> (3) The wall's main purpose was defense, but it also served other functions. Watchtowers allowed soldiers to send signals using smoke or fire, and gates along the wall helped officials control trade and collect taxes.
> (4) Today, the wall faces threats from erosion and tourism. Some sections have disappeared completely, while others are being restored.
1. What is the main idea of paragraph 1? 2. Why are Ming sections better preserved? 3. Besides defense, what were two functions of the wall? 4. In paragraph 4, “threats” is closest to: a) dangers b) visitors c) repairs. 5. What can be inferred about the earliest sections?
**Tempo:** ____ **Acertos:** __/5

- Tempo total: ____ · Parte mais lenta: ____ · Item em que travei: ____ · Erro por desatenção: ____

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Complete the Words.** Crie e complete um parágrafo de 10 lacunas (de um artigo). Meta: 3 minutos.
**Dia 2 — Daily Life.** Leia 2 e-mails ou avisos reais em inglês e responda 3 perguntas sobre cada (meta: 2 min por texto).
**Dia 3 — Academic.** Leia um texto de 4 parágrafos e escreva a ideia principal de cada um em 5 minutos.
**Dia 4 — Análise.** Escreva 5 frases sobre seu desempenho desta semana (*I spent… / I got stuck… / Next time…*).
**Dia 5 — Speaking.** Grave 1 minuto explicando o texto da Muralha da China.
**Dia 6 — Conjunto misto.** Refaça as 3 partes da tarefa cronometrada sem olhar as respostas anteriores. Compare o tempo.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
