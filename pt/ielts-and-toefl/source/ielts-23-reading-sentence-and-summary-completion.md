## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** completar **frases** e **resumos** com palavras **do texto** (ou de uma **lista**, em algumas versões). A resposta precisa caber **gramaticalmente** e respeitar o **limite de palavras**.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** encontrar a parte do texto que corresponde ao resumo e copiar a palavra certa, na forma certa.
**Tarefa verificável:** preencher oito lacunas preservando forma e sentido.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Complete oralmente com uma palavra que **caiba** na gramática:
- *The museum attracts thousands of ___ every year.* · *The bridge was ___ in 1890.* · *The new law had a ___ effect on traffic.* · *Scientists are trying to ___ the problem.*
**Revisão da aula 22:** que palavras sinalizam *a prediction*?

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Tipos de summary completion
- **Palavras do texto:** *Choose NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS from the passage.* → copie **exatamente** (ortografia!).
- **Lista de palavras (A–H):** escolha da caixa; a lista tem **sinônimos** do texto e distratores.
### Checagem gramatical da lacuna
- **artigo (a/an/the) + ___** → substantivo · **___ + substantivo** → adjetivo · **to ___** → verbo base
- **was/were ___** → particípio (*built, introduced*) ou adjetivo
- **concordância:** *a number of ___* → plural
### Palavras de resumos acadêmicos
- **initially** · **subsequently** · **eventually** · **gradually** · **despite** · **due to** · **resulting in** · **in order to**

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **initially** = i-NI-shal-ly · **subsequently** = SUB-se-quent-ly · **eventually** = e-VEN-tchu-al-ly
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Initially the plan failed, but it was eventually successful due to public support.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Estratégia
1. **Leia o resumo inteiro** primeiro — entenda a história.
2. Encontre **onde no texto** o resumo começa (âncora: nome, data). Resumos geralmente cobrem **uma parte** do texto, em ordem.
3. Para cada lacuna: **classe da palavra** + **sentido** → procure no trecho.
4. **Copie exatamente** e confira o limite.
### Modelo
> **Texto:** *The first bicycles, introduced in the early 1800s, had no pedals; riders pushed themselves along with their feet. Pedals were added in the 1860s, but these machines were extremely uncomfortable and were nicknamed “boneshakers”.*
> **Resumo:** *Early bicycles were moved by riders using their (1) ___. Later models with pedals were very (2) ___ and earned the name “boneshakers”.*
→ (1) **feet** (*with their feet*) · (2) **uncomfortable** (adjetivo depois de *very*)

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: justificando a palavra
> The answer is **“uncomfortable”** because the gap needs **an adjective** after **“very”**, and the text says **“extremely uncomfortable”**.
> → “feet” / a plural noun / “their” / “with their feet” → “introduced” / a past participle / “were” / “introduced in the early 1800s”
### 4.2 Resumo com lista
> **Texto:** *Ants communicate mainly through chemicals called pheromones. When an ant finds food, it leaves a trail of pheromones on its way back to the nest. Other ants follow this trail, and as more ants use it, the signal becomes stronger. If the food runs out, the ants stop reinforcing the trail, and the chemical gradually disappears.*
> **Resumo:** *Ants rely on (1) ___ signals. A successful ant marks a (2) ___ to the food. The more ants use it, the more (3) ___ it becomes. When there is no more food, the signal (4) ___ fades.*
> **Lista:** A path · B visual · C chemical · D quickly · E powerful · F slowly · G nest · H weak

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: 8 lacunas (15 min)
**Instrução:** *Complete the summary. Choose NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS from the passage for each answer.*
> **The Story of the Potato**
> The potato was first domesticated in the Andes of South America around 8,000 years ago. Spanish explorers brought it to Europe in the late sixteenth century, but at first many Europeans were suspicious of it, believing it caused disease. Some even thought it was only suitable as animal feed.
> Attitudes changed gradually. In France, the scientist Antoine-Augustin Parmentier promoted the potato after surviving on it as a prisoner of war. He organised dinners where every dish contained potatoes and even placed guards around a potato field to make local people believe the crop was valuable.
> By the nineteenth century, the potato had become a staple food in much of northern Europe, because it produced more calories per hectare than grain. However, this dependence had a terrible consequence in Ireland. When a plant disease destroyed the crop in the 1840s, about one million people died, and another million emigrated.
> **Summary:**
> Potatoes were first grown in the (1) ______ thousands of years ago. When they arrived in Europe, people were (2) ______ of them, and some considered them fit only for (3) ______. In France, Parmentier, who had eaten potatoes as a (4) ______, worked to change their image. He used (5) ______ to make a field seem valuable. Later, potatoes became a (6) ______ in northern Europe because they gave more (7) ______ per hectare than grain. In Ireland, a (8) ______ destroyed the crop in the 1840s, with tragic results.
**Acertos:** __/8
**Fala (4 min):** explique a história da batata em 1 minuto usando *initially, subsequently, eventually, due to*.
