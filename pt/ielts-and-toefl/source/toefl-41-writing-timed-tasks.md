## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** fazer as **3 tarefas de Writing** em sequência — *Build a Sentence*, *Write an Email*, *Write for an Academic Discussion* — controlando o tempo de **planejar, escrever e revisar**.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** distribuir seu tempo e não deixar nenhuma tarefa incompleta.
**Tarefa verificável:** concluir três tarefas de escrita com cronômetro.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- Do you usually finish writing tasks on time? Where do you lose time?
- Do you plan before writing? Do you revise after?
**Troque e fale:**
> I usually spend too much time **planning**, so I don't have time to **revise**.
> → writing the first sentence / finish → thinking of perfect words / check grammar → counting words / write the conclusion
**Revisão da aula 40:** diga 4 cortes de concisão (*due to the fact that → because…*).

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Divisão de tempo (sugestão para treino; confirme os tempos oficiais no site da ETS)
- **Build a Sentence:** ~30–40 s por item — leia a fala, ache o verbo, monte, confira.
- **Write an Email (≈7 min):** 1 min planejar (checklist dos pontos) · 5 min escrever · 1 min revisar.
- **Academic Discussion (≈10 min):** 1–2 min ler e planejar · 7 min escrever · 1–2 min revisar.
### Checklist de revisão de 60 segundos
- **S**ujeito em todas as frases? · **V**erbos no tempo certo (-s, -ed)? · **A**rtigos (a/an/the)? · **P**ontos do enunciado cobertos? · **O**rtografia de palavras-chave?
→ Lembre: **S-V-A-P-O**
### Expressões de planejamento
- **outline** — esboço · **draft** — rascunho · **proofread** — revisar (erros) · **word count** — número de palavras

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **proofread** = PROOF-read · **outline** = OUT-line · **draft / graph / craft**
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> I'll outline for one minute, write for seven, and proofread for two.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Erros de tempo mais comuns
1. **Planejar demais** → escreva o plano em palavras-chave, não frases.
2. **Começar sem plano** → texto confuso, ideias repetidas.
3. **Não revisar** → erros simples (*he go, a extension*) que tiram pontos.
4. **Ficar preso em um item de Build a Sentence** → chute educado e siga.
### Modelo de plano rápido (Email)
> Prompt: *Write to your landlord: the heater is broken. Explain the problem, say what you've done, ask for a repair date.*
> Plano (30 s): *heater broken since Mon · tried reset + checked power · cold, exam week · repair before Fri? · thanks*
### Modelo de plano rápido (Discussion)
> *Pos: yes, ban phones in class · conn: agree w/ Lia (distraction) + add · R1: memory — handwriting notes · Ex: my exp. · Counter: emergencies → phones at front desk*

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Planos-relâmpago (1 minuto cada, oral)
O professor lê o enunciado; o aluno fala o plano em palavras-chave.
1. *Email: You can't attend a job interview at the scheduled time. Write to the recruiter: apologize, explain, suggest two new times.*
2. *Discussion: Should universities replace textbooks with free online materials?* (Colega A: *saves money*; Colega B: *online materials are lower quality*.)
3. *Email: Your neighbor's dog barks every night. Write a polite message.*
### 4.2 Revisão de 60 segundos (S-V-A-P-O)
Encontre os erros em 60 s:
> *Dear Mr. Silva, I am writing because heater is broken since Monday. I tryed to reset it, but it don't work. Could you send someone to repair? Thank you, Bruno*

## 5. Tarefas cronometradas (15 min — sem pausa entre elas)
### Parte 1 — Build a Sentence (3 min, 5 itens)
1. **“Is the seminar today?”** → *was / it / to / moved / Thursday*
2. **“I need help with my essay.”** → *you / tried / the writing center / have / ?*
3. **“Where's the meeting?”** → *know / don't / I / is / where / it*
4. **“Why are you so tired?”** → *I / up / until / stayed / studying / 2 a.m.*
5. **“Can I borrow your notes?”** → *as / I'll / soon / them / as / send / get / I / home*
### Parte 2 — Email (5 min, 80–120 palavras)
*You booked a study room at the library for your group, but when you arrived, another group was using it. Write to the library: describe what happened, explain why it was a problem, and ask what they can do.*
> ______
### Parte 3 — Academic Discussion (7 min, 100+ palavras)
> **Professor Lima:** *Should universities require students to take at least one online course before graduating?*
> **Rita:** *Yes, because many jobs now involve online work and communication.*
> **Omar:** *No. Some students learn much better face to face, and they shouldn't be forced.*
> ______
**Registro de tempo:** P1 ____ P2 ____ P3 ____ · Revisou? P2 ☐ P3 ☐

## 6. Feedback (3 min)

- [ ] Revisou com S-V-A-P-O?
- [ ] Onde perdeu mais tempo? ______

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**. Use cronômetro sempre.
**Dia 1 — Build a Sentence.** Crie e resolva 10 itens (3 min).
**Dia 2 — Email.** *Write to a professor asking to change your presentation date.* 7 min.
**Dia 3 — Discussion.** *Should public transport be free for students?* 10 min.
**Dia 4 — Revisão S-V-A-P-O.** Revise os textos dos dias 2–3 em 60 s cada. Anote os erros que encontrou.
**Dia 5 — Speaking.** Grave 45 s respondendo oralmente à pergunta do Professor Lima.
**Dia 6 — Bloco completo.** Refaça as 3 partes (15 min) com temas novos (*Email: lost item on the bus; Discussion: should homework be graded?*).
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
