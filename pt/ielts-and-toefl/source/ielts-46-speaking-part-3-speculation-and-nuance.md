## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** perguntas de Part 3 sobre o **futuro** e situações **hipotéticas** (*What will… be like? What would happen if…?*). Para band 7+, mostre **nuance**: incerteza, ressalvas e consequências.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** especular sobre o futuro e defender posições com ressalvas (*it depends, to some extent, unless…*).
**Tarefa verificável:** defender posições com ressalvas e contrapontos.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Diga o quanto você tem certeza (100%, 70%, 30%):
- *In 20 years, people will work from home.* · *Cars will fly.* · *Everyone will speak English.* · *Cash will disappear.*
**Frases:** *It's bound to… (certeza) · It's likely that… · There's a chance that… · I doubt that… · It's highly unlikely that…*
**Revisão da aula 45:** responda O-R-E: *What makes a good teacher?*

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Grau de certeza (do mais forte ao mais fraco)
- *will definitely · is bound to · will probably · is likely to · may/might/could · probably won't · is unlikely to · definitely won't*
### Hipóteses
- **2ª condicional:** *If governments banned cars, cities would be quieter.*
- *Suppose / Imagine that… · In that case, … · That would mean…*
### Nuance e ressalvas
- *It depends on… · To some extent, … · Up to a point, … · That's true for…, but not for… · Unless…, … · Provided that… · It's not that simple, because…*
### Consequências
- *This could lead to… · One possible consequence is… · In the long run, … · As a knock-on effect, …*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- *bound to* → *BOUN-tə* · *likely to* → *LIKE-ly-tə* · *in the long run* → *in-thə-LONG-RUN*
- Entonação de ressalva: *It's TRUE… up to a POINT.*
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> It's likely that more people will work remotely, but it depends on the job, and in the long run it might change our cities.

:::quiz vocab
? mc|vocabulary|Which expresses the strongest certainty?
*A) is bound to || Correto.

C) is unlikely to || Negativo.
? w|grammar|If governments ______ cars, cities would be quieter. (ban — 2ª condicional)

= If governments banned cars, …
? w|vocabulary|It ______ on the type of job. (depende)
> depends
= It depends on the type of job.
? mc|vocabulary|“Provided that” means…
*A) on condition that || Correto.
B) because || Não.
? w|vocabulary|In the long ______, this could save money. (prazo)
> run
= In the long run, …
:::

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Por que nuance aumenta a nota
Respostas absolutas (*Everyone will…*) soam simplistas. Respostas com **ressalvas** mostram pensamento crítico e gramática mais variada (modais, condicionais).
### Modelo
> **Q:** *Do you think people will still read printed books in the future?*
> **A:** *I think they probably will, at least to some extent. Digital books are convenient, but many people still enjoy the feeling of holding a real book, and printed books don't need batteries. Having said that, I suspect the number of printed books will fall, especially in education, where tablets are cheaper in the long run. So printed books are unlikely to disappear completely, but they might become more of a luxury item.*
> **Q:** *What would happen if public transport were free?*
> *Well, in that case, I imagine more people would leave their cars at home, which could reduce traffic and pollution. However, it depends on the quality of the service. If buses were free but slow and crowded, people probably wouldn't switch. Also, someone has to pay, so taxes might go up, which not everyone would accept.*

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: graus de certeza
> In the future, **cash** **is likely to** **disappear**, **but** **older people may still use it**.
> → robots / are bound to / do more jobs / humans will still be needed in care → cities / will probably / become greener / it depends on investment → English / might / remain the global language / Chinese could become more important
### 4.2 Hipóteses (2ª condicional) — 30 s cada
1. *What would happen if everyone worked four days a week?* 2. *What if children didn't go to school until age 10?* 3. *What would happen if social media were banned for under-16s?*
### 4.3 Adicione uma ressalva
Transforme em resposta com nuance: *“Technology makes people lonely.”* → *To some extent… but…* / *It depends on…*

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: debate de Part 3 (15 min)
**Tema: The future of cities and transport.** O professor faz o papel de examinador e **desafia** a opinião do aluno com follow-ups (*But don't you think…? What about…?*).
1. How do you think cities will change in the next 30 years?
2. Would it be a good idea to ban cars from city centres?
3. What would happen if most people worked from home?
4. Do you think self-driving cars will become common? What problems might they cause?
5. Should governments make flying more expensive to protect the environment?
6. Is it possible for a city to grow and still be environmentally friendly?
**Professor anota:** modais de certeza __ · condicionais __ · ressalvas __ · consequências __ · respondeu ao desafio sem travar? ☐
**Rodada rápida (3 min):** o professor escolhe 2 perguntas e pede ao aluno para **defender o lado oposto**.

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**. Grave 3 respostas por dia (45 s).
**Dia 1 — Futuro.** *Will people still go to offices? Will cash disappear? Will we live longer?*
**Dia 2 — Hipóteses.** *What if there were no exams? What if everyone had to retire at 50? What if the internet stopped for a week?*
**Dia 3 — Ressalvas.** Responda 3 perguntas sempre com *It depends on… / To some extent…*
**Dia 4 — Lado oposto.** Grave 2 respostas defendendo opiniões que não são suas.
**Dia 5 — Vocabulário.** Frases com *bound to, likely to, provided that, unless, knock-on effect, in the long run*.
**Dia 6 — Escute.** Ouça as gravações e conte modais e condicionais. Regrave a mais simples.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
