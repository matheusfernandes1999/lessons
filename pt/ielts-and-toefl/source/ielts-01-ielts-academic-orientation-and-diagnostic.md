## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** conhecer o **IELTS Academic**: as 4 seções, o tempo de cada uma e **como a nota (band 0–9) é dada**.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** explicar em inglês o formato da prova e dizer qual é sua meta de band em cada habilidade.
**Tarefa verificável:** realizar diagnóstico breve e estabelecer metas de band por habilidade.
**Como usar este documento:** professor e aluno editam juntos durante a aula. Acrescente exemplos, corrija frases e anote dúvidas aqui.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- Why do you need the IELTS? (university, immigration, work…)
- What band score do you need? For which institution?
- When are you planning to take the test?
- Which skill do you think is your strongest?
**Frases úteis:**
- *I need the IELTS because I'm applying to a university in the UK / Australia / Canada.*
- *I need an overall band of 6.5, with no less than 6 in each skill.*
- *I'm planning to take the test in…*
**Troque e fale:**
> I need **an overall 7** for **a master's in Australia**.
> → a 6.5 / a nursing program in Canada → a 6 / a work visa → a 7.5 / a PhD in the UK

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
- **band (score)** — nota de 0 a 9 · **overall band** — nota geral (média das 4)
- **section / part** — seção / parte · **task** — tarefa
- **criteria** (sing. *criterion*) — critérios · **descriptor** — descrição de cada nível
- **requirement** — exigência · **minimum** — mínimo
- **paper-based / computer-delivered** — no papel / no computador
- **candidate** — candidato · **examiner** — examinador
- **fluency** — fluência · **accuracy** — precisão · **range** — variedade
**Chunks:**
- *I'm aiming for…* (minha meta é…) · *I'm stronger at… than…* · *I find… challenging.*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **criteria / criterion** — crai-TI-ri-a · **examiner** — ig-ZAM-i-ner · **candidate** — CAN-di-date
- **band / bend / bond** · **aim / I'm** · **range / rage / rain**
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> I'm aiming for an overall band of seven, with at least six point five in writing.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### O IELTS Academic em uma olhada (confira detalhes no site oficial)
**Listening — ~30 min · 4 partes · 40 perguntas**
- Parte 1: conversa do dia a dia (ex.: reserva, matrícula)
- Parte 2: monólogo do dia a dia (ex.: guia turístico, aviso)
- Parte 3: conversa acadêmica (alunos e/ou professor)
- Parte 4: palestra acadêmica
**Reading — 60 min · 3 textos longos · 40 perguntas** — tipos: True/False/Not Given, Matching Headings, completar frases, múltipla escolha…
**Writing — 60 min · 2 tarefas**
- Task 1 (≈20 min, mín. **150 palavras**): descrever gráfico, tabela, processo ou mapa
- Task 2 (≈40 min, mín. **250 palavras**): ensaio argumentativo — **vale mais** que a Task 1
**Speaking — 11–14 min · entrevista com examinador**
- Part 1: perguntas sobre você · Part 2: fala de até 2 min com cartão (1 min de preparo) · Part 3: discussão abstrata
### Como a nota é dada
- Listening e Reading: número de acertos (de 40) convertido em band.
- Writing: 4 critérios — **Task Achievement/Response, Coherence and Cohesion, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range and Accuracy**.
- Speaking: 4 critérios — **Fluency and Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range and Accuracy, Pronunciation**.
- **Overall** = média das 4 bandas (arredondada para a meia banda mais próxima).
### Modelo — fale junto com o professor
> I'm taking the IELTS Academic because I want to do a master's degree in Ireland. I need an overall band of 6.5, with no less than 6.0 in each skill. I think reading is my strongest skill, because I read a lot for work. I find writing challenging, especially Task 2, because I need to organize my ideas quickly. My main goal for this course is to reach 6.5 in writing.

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale
> I find **writing** challenging, especially **Task 2**, because **I need to organize my ideas quickly**.
> → listening / Part 4 / the lecture is long and fast → speaking / Part 2 / I have to talk for two minutes → reading / True/False/Not Given / “Not Given” is confusing
### 4.2 Qual seção/parte é essa?
1. You describe a bar chart about energy use in 150 words.
2. You hear a tour guide talking about a museum.
3. You talk for two minutes about a book you like.
4. You read a long text and decide if statements are True, False or Not Given.
5. You hear two students and a tutor discussing a project.
6. You write an essay giving your opinion in 250 words.
### 4.3 Calcule o overall (oral)
- Listening 7.0 · Reading 6.5 · Writing 6.0 · Speaking 6.5 → média = 6.5 → **overall 6.5**
- Listening 6.0 · Reading 6.5 · Writing 5.5 · Speaking 6.0 → média = 6.0 → overall ____
- Listening 7.5 · Reading 7.0 · Writing 6.5 · Speaking 7.0 → média = 7.0 → overall ____

## 5. Diagnóstico rápido (15 min)
### A. Listening — 3 min (🎧 professor lê uma vez; aluno completa)
> “Good morning, City Language School. … Yes, the evening course starts on the fourteenth of March. Classes are on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 6:30 to 8:30. The course costs 320 pounds, and you can pay in two parts. Could I have your surname, please? … Thank you — and how do you spell that? … R-O-D-R-I-G-U-E-S. Perfect.”
1. Start date: ______ 2. Days: ______ 3. Time: ______ 4. Price: £______ 5. Surname: ______
**Acertos:** __/5
### B. Reading — 3 min (True / False / Not Given)
> Many cities around the world have introduced bike-sharing schemes. In Paris, the Vélib' system was launched in 2007 and quickly became one of the largest in Europe. Users can pick up a bicycle at one station and return it at another. Studies suggest that such schemes reduce short car trips, although their effect on overall traffic is still debated.
6. The Vélib' system started in 2007. 7. Users must return bikes to the same station. 8. Vélib' is the cheapest system in Europe. 9. Experts agree that bike-sharing greatly reduces total traffic.
**Acertos:** __/4
### C. Speaking — 4 min
- Part 1 (20 s cada): *Where do you live? · Do you like your neighborhood? Why?*
- Part 2 (1 min de preparo + 1 min de fala): *Describe a place in your city that you like to visit.*
**Professor (band estimada 4–7 por critério):** Fluência __ Vocabulário __ Gramática __ Pronúncia __
### D. Writing — 5 min
Escreva 5–6 frases: *Some people think that cities should spend more money on public transport. Do you agree?*

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Formato.** Sem olhar, escreva o formato das 4 seções (partes, tempo, nº de perguntas). Confira no site oficial do IELTS.
**Dia 2 — Vocabulário.** Faça frases sobre você com as palavras-chave (*band, aiming for, challenging, requirement…*).
**Dia 3 — Sombra.** Leia o modelo da seção 3 três vezes e grave a sua versão com seus dados reais.
**Dia 4 — Listening.** Ouça um vídeo oficial de amostra do IELTS Listening (Part 1) e anote 5 informações (nomes, números, datas).
**Dia 5 — Speaking.** Grave 1 a 2 minutos: *Describe a place in your city that you like to visit.*
**Dia 6 — Writing.** Reescreva o parágrafo da tarefa D com 100 palavras: posição + 2 razões.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
