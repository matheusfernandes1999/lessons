## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** o ensaio de **discussão** (*Discuss both views and give your own opinion*): apresentar **as duas visões de forma justa** e deixar **sua opinião clara** — na introdução e na conclusão.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** escrever um ensaio equilibrado em 4 parágrafos, com linguagem para relatar opiniões de outros.
**Tarefa verificável:** produzir estrutura balanceada com conclusão.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Apresente os dois lados de cada tema (1 frase cada) e depois a sua opinião:
- *Should children learn coding at primary school?* · *Is it better to rent or buy a home?*
**Troque e fale:**
> **Some people argue that** **children should learn coding early**, **while others believe** **they should focus on reading and maths**. **Personally, I think** **both are possible**.
> → renting is smarter / buying is a better investment / it depends on the city → exams are the best way to assess students / projects are fairer / a mix works best

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Relatar a opinião dos outros (sem ser sua)
- *Some people argue/claim/believe that… · Supporters of this view point out that… · Those who disagree maintain that… · It is often argued that… · Critics suggest that… · Advocates of… emphasise that…*
### Mostrar sua opinião
- *Personally, I believe… · In my view… · I am inclined to agree with… · I would argue that… · Having considered both sides, I…*
### Concessão (equilíbrio)
- *Although this argument has some merit, … · While it is true that…, … · Admittedly, …*
### Estrutura recomendada (4 parágrafos)
1. **Intro:** tema + “this essay will discuss both views” + **sua opinião**
2. **Body 1:** visão A (com razão + exemplo)
3. **Body 2:** visão B — **geralmente a que você apoia** (com razão + exemplo)
4. **Conclusão:** resumo + sua opinião reforçada

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **advocate** (n) = AD-vo-cate · **emphasise** = EM-pha-sise · **inclined** = in-CLINED · **merit** = MER-it
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Although this argument has some merit, I am inclined to agree with those who emphasise the long-term benefits.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Modelo completo (≈270 palavras)
> **Enunciado:** *Some people believe that university students should study whatever they like. Others believe they should only be allowed to study subjects that will be useful in the future, such as science and technology. Discuss both views and give your opinion.*
> *(Intro) The question of what university students should be allowed to study is widely debated. While some argue that degree choices should be limited to useful fields like science and technology, I believe students should be free to choose their subject, as long as they are well informed about job prospects.*
> *(Body 1) Those who support limiting choices point out that economies need skilled professionals in areas such as engineering, medicine and computing. If governments fund university places, they argue, the money should go to subjects that create jobs and drive innovation. For example, several countries offer scholarships only in STEM fields in order to address shortages of qualified workers.*
> *(Body 2) On the other hand, advocates of free choice emphasise that motivation is essential for success. Students who study a subject they enjoy are more likely to complete their degree and perform well. Moreover, fields such as history, languages and the arts develop critical thinking and communication skills, which employers in every sector value. It is also difficult to predict which skills will be useful in twenty years.*
> *(Conclusion) In conclusion, although directing students towards science and technology may meet short-term economic needs, I believe that allowing free choice, supported by clear career guidance, produces more motivated graduates and a more flexible workforce.*
Leia com o professor e marque: visão A · visão B · opinião (onde aparece?) · concessão.

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: relatando visões
> **Those who support** **homework** **point out that** **it helps students practise**. **However, critics maintain that** **it causes stress**.
> → online learning / it is flexible / it reduces social interaction → city life / there are more jobs / it is expensive and stressful → tourism / it creates jobs / it damages local culture

2. *Some believe that zoos are cruel; others think they help protect endangered species. Discuss both views and give your opinion.*

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Linguagem.** Escreva 10 frases relatando opiniões de outros com verbos diferentes.
**Dia 2 — Planos.** Faça planos para 3 enunciados de discussão oficiais.
**Dia 3 — Introduções.** Escreva as introduções dos 3 planos (com opinião).
**Dia 4 — Speaking.** Grave 2 minutos discutindo os dois lados de *countryside vs. city for children*.
**Dia 5 — Ensaio completo** (discussão) em 40 minutos.
**Dia 6 — Autoavaliação.** Avalie o ensaio do dia 5 com o checklist da seção 6.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
