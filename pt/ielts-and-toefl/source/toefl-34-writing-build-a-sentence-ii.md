## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** montar frases **complexas**: perguntas indiretas (*Do you know where…*), orações relativas (*the book that…*), conectores (*although, because, unless*) e modificadores (*the new science building*).
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** montar frases de nível B1–B2 com relações lógicas claras e ordem natural.
**Tarefa verificável:** montar dez frases com relações lógicas claras.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Transforme a pergunta direta em indireta, falando:
- *Where is the library?* → *Do you know where the library is?*
- *What time does the class start?* → *Could you tell me…?*
- *Did she submit the essay?* → *I wonder if…*
**Revisão da aula 33:** monte em voz alta: *ever / you / been / have / to Canada / ?*

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### 1. Perguntas indiretas — ordem de AFIRMAÇÃO depois da palavra interrogativa
- *Do you know **where the library is**?* (não *where is the library*)
- *Could you tell me **what time the class starts**?* (sem *does*)
- *I wonder **if/whether she submitted** the essay.*
### 2. Orações relativas
- **who** (pessoas) · **which** (coisas) · **that** (pessoas/coisas) · **where** (lugares) · **whose** (de quem)
- *The professor **who teaches biology** is from Chile.*
- *The book **(that) I borrowed** is overdue.* (pode omitir *that* quando ele é objeto)
### 3. Conectores
- **although / even though** (embora) · **because / since** (porque) · **unless** (a menos que) · **so that** (para que) · **while** (enquanto/ao passo que) · **as soon as** (assim que)
### 4. Ordem dos adjetivos/modificadores
- opinião → tamanho → idade → cor → origem → material → tipo + substantivo
- *a **beautiful old brick** building* · *the **new science** building* · *a **useful online** tool*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- Pergunta direta ↗/↘ × indireta (entonação de afirmação no fim):
- *Where IS it?* × *Do you know where it IS?*
- **whether / weather** · **whose / who's** (iguais!) · **although / all though**
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Do you know whether the professor who teaches statistics has office hours today?

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Estratégia para frases longas
1. Encontre a **oração principal** (sujeito + verbo principal).
2. Encontre o **conector** ou **pronome relativo**: ele começa a segunda parte.
3. Na pergunta indireta: **começo de pergunta** (*Do you know / Could you tell me*) + **wh-** + **sujeito + verbo**.
### Modelo 1
**Fala:** *“I need to talk to Dr. Silva.”*
**Blocos:** *know / office / do / her / where / is / you / ?*
→ **Do you know where her office is?**
### Modelo 2
**Fala:** *“Why didn't you go to the talk?”*
**Blocos:** *interested / although / I / was / too tired / I / was*
→ **Although I was interested, I was too tired.** (ou *I was too tired, although I was interested.*)

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: perguntas indiretas
> Do you know **where the lab is**?
> → when the exam starts → who is giving the lecture → if the library is open → how much the textbook costs → whether we need to bring a calculator

### 4.2 Junte as frases (oral)
1. I have a friend. She studies medicine. (*who*)
2. This is the café. We met there. (*where*)
3. I'll send the file. I get home. (*as soon as*)
4. The test was hard. I passed. (*although*)
5. You won't pass. You study more. (*unless*)
6. She took notes. She could review later. (*so that*)

### 4.3 Monte
1. tell / me / could / the bus / leaves / when / you / ?
2. the student / whose / laptop / was stolen / reported / it / police / to the
3. even though / it / was raining / we / walked / to campus

:::quiz practice
? w|grammar|I have a friend ______ studies medicine. (who/which)
> who
= I have a friend who studies medicine.
? w|grammar|This is the café ______ we met. (lugar)
> where
= This is the café where we met.
? mc|grammar|Join: The test was hard. I passed.
*A) Although the test was hard, I passed. || Correto: contraste.
B) Because the test was hard, I passed. || Relação lógica errada.
C) Unless the test was hard, I passed. || Sem sentido.
? mc|grammar|Put in order: tell / me / could / the bus / leaves / when / you / ?
A) Could you tell me when does the bus leave? || Não use “does” na indireta.
*B) Could you tell me when the bus leaves? || Correto.
C) Could you tell me when leaves the bus? || Ordem errada.
? w|grammar|She took notes so ______ she could review later.
> that
= She took notes so that she could review later.
? mc|grammar|Which is correct?
*A) The student whose laptop was stolen reported it to the police. || Correto.
B) The student who's laptop was stolen reported it to the police. || Who's = who is.
C) The student which laptop was stolen reported it. || Which não indica posse.
:::

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: 10 frases (15 min)
**1 minuto por item.** Escreva a frase. Depois leia em voz alta.
1. **Fala:** “I need a quiet place to study.” → *know / do / the study rooms / you / are / where / ?*
2. **Fala:** “Did you like the course?” → *it / was / although / difficult / I / loved / it*
3. **Fala:** “Who is that man?” → *the professor / he's / gave / who / yesterday's lecture*
4. **Fala:** “When will you send the report?” → *as / as / it / I'll / soon / finish / I / send / it*
5. **Fala:** “Can I park here?” → *not / you / a permit / unless / have*
6. **Fala:** “Why are you recording the lecture?” → *so / I / can / that / it / later / listen to / again*
7. **Fala:** “I'm looking for a book.” → *the one / is / it / recommended / that / the professor / ?*
8. **Fala:** “Where did you grow up?” → *in a small town / where / everybody / knows / everybody*
9. **Fala:** “Is the new building open?” → *wonder / I / it / if / opens / next week*
10. **Fala:** “What did the advisor say?” → *said / she / should / that / I / take / fewer courses*
**Acertos:** __/10
**Fala (3 min):** o professor lê as falas, o aluno responde lendo suas frases com entonação natural.

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Indiretas.** Transforme 10 perguntas diretas em indiretas (*Do you know…, Could you tell me…, I wonder…*).
**Dia 2 — Relativas.** Escreva 8 frases sobre pessoas e lugares da sua vida com *who, which, where, whose*.
**Dia 3 — Conectores.** Escreva 2 frases com cada conector: *although, unless, so that, as soon as, while*.
**Dia 4 — Embaralhe.** Embaralhe 8 frases do dia 2/3 e reorganize no dia seguinte.
**Dia 5 — Speaking.** Grave 1 minuto sobre sua cidade usando 3 orações relativas e 2 conectores.
**Dia 6 — Writing.** Escreva um parágrafo de 80 palavras sobre um professor que você admira, com 2 relativas e 2 conectores.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
