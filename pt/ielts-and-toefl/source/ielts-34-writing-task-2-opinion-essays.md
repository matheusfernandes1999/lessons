## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** o ensaio de **opinião** (*To what extent do you agree or disagree?*): introdução com tese e **parágrafos argumentativos** bem construídos — frase-tópico, explicação, exemplo, ligação com a tese.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** escrever introdução + 2 parágrafos de corpo que defendem sua posição de forma lógica.
**Tarefa verificável:** redigir introdução e dois parágrafos argumentativos.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Diga **concordo/discordo + 1 razão** (30 s cada):
- *Children should not have smartphones before age 14.* · *Working from home is better than working in an office.* · *Museums should be free.*
**Troque e fale:**
> **The main reason** I agree is that **children are easily distracted**, **which affects their concentration at school**.
> → work from home / people save time commuting / they have more time for family → museums free / culture should be available to everyone / it improves education
**Revisão da aula 33:** diga os 5 tipos de enunciado de Task 2.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Estrutura do parágrafo: **PEEL**
- **P**oint — frase-tópico (a ideia do parágrafo)
- **E**xplain — por quê? como?
- **E**xample — caso concreto (país, situação, pesquisa geral — **sem inventar estatísticas exatas**)
- **L**ink — volta à tese
### Frases-tópico
- *The main reason why… is that… · Firstly, … · Another important argument is that… · A further point to consider is…*
### Explicação
- *This is because… · As a result, … · This means that… · Consequently, …*
### Exemplo
- *For example, … · For instance, in countries such as… · A clear example of this is… · This can be seen in…*
### Link
- *Therefore, … · For this reason, … · This clearly shows that…*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **consequently** = CON-se-quent-ly · **further** = FUR-ther · **therefore** = THERE-fore
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> The main reason is that free museums attract more visitors; for this reason, they benefit the whole community.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Introdução em 2 frases
1. **Paráfrase do tema** (não copie): *It is sometimes argued that…*
2. **Tese:** *I completely/partly agree/disagree because…* (pode antecipar as 2 razões)
### Modelo
> **Enunciado:** *Some people think that children should not be allowed to use smartphones until they are teenagers. To what extent do you agree or disagree?*
> **Introdução:** *It is often suggested that young children should be kept away from smartphones until their teenage years. I largely agree with this view, as early smartphone use can harm both children's concentration and their social development.*
> **Body 1 (PEEL):** *(P) The main reason why I support this idea is that smartphones can seriously reduce children's ability to concentrate. (E) Apps and games are designed to give instant rewards, which makes slower activities, such as reading or homework, seem boring in comparison. (E) For instance, many primary school teachers report that pupils who spend hours on devices find it difficult to focus during lessons. (L) Therefore, limiting access to smartphones would help children develop the attention they need to learn.*
> **Body 2:** *(P) A further argument is that face-to-face play is essential for developing social skills. …*

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: PEEL oral
O professor dá o *Point*; o aluno fala **Explain + Example + Link**:
> 1. *Working from home saves employees a lot of time.* 2. *Free museums make culture accessible to everyone.* 3. *Learning to cook at school improves young people's health.*
### 4.2 Conserte o parágrafo
> *Smartphones are bad. Children use them a lot. My cousin has a phone. Also, phones are expensive. And they can be dangerous. So children shouldn't have phones.*
Problemas: Point vago · sem explicação · exemplo fraco · várias ideias misturadas · link fraco. Reescreva com **uma** ideia central.
### 4.3 Paráfrase do enunciado
1. *Some people believe that university students should pay the full cost of their studies.*
2. *Many people think that the government should ban fast food advertising.*

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Paráfrase.** Escreva introduções para 4 enunciados de opinião oficiais.
**Dia 2 — PEEL.** Escreva 3 parágrafos PEEL sobre temas diferentes.
**Dia 3 — Exemplos.** Para 6 argumentos, escreva um exemplo geral plausível (país, situação, tipo de pessoa).
**Dia 4 — Conclusão.** Escreva conclusões de 2 frases para os ensaios dos dias 1–2 (retome a tese sem repetir palavras).
**Dia 5 — Speaking.** Grave 1 minuto defendendo sua opinião sobre museus gratuitos (estrutura PEEL oral).
**Dia 6 — Ensaio completo** de opinião em 40 minutos.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
