## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** **Lexical Resource** (vocabulário) no IELTS não é usar palavras difíceis: é usar **combinações naturais** (*collocations*) e **parafrasear** (dizer a mesma coisa com outras palavras).
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** reformular frases sem mudar o sentido usando sinônimos, mudança de classe de palavra e collocations.
**Tarefa verificável:** reformular oito afirmações com sentido preservado.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Diga de outra forma (o professor ajuda):
- *I'm very tired.* → *I'm exhausted.* / *I'm worn out.*
- *The test was very hard.* → ______
- *A lot of people use phones.* → ______
- *Cities are getting bigger.* → ______
**Revisão da aula 2:** corrija: *The number of cars have increased since 2010.*

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Collocations (palavras que andam juntas)
- **make:** a decision · progress · a mistake · an effort · a difference
- **do:** research · homework · damage · business
- **take:** action · responsibility · a break · part in · advantage of
- **have:** an impact on · an effect on · access to
- **pay:** attention to · a visit
- **adjetivo + substantivo:** *heavy traffic* (não *strong traffic*) · *strong evidence* · *high price* (não *expensive price*) · *major problem* · *serious concern* · *rapid growth* · *wide range*
- **verbo + advérbio:** *increase significantly* · *vary widely* · *depend heavily on* · *strongly believe*
### Paráfrase — 3 técnicas
1. **Sinônimo:** *important → crucial/essential*
2. **Mudar a classe da palavra:** *Cities are growing rapidly → The rapid growth of cities…*
3. **Mudar a estrutura:** *Many people think… → It is widely believed that…*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita (collocations como um bloco)
- *make a DIFference* · *take ACtion* · *pay atTENtion to* · *have an IMpact on* · *HEAvy TRAFfic*
- **impact** (n) = IM-pact · **significantly** = sig-NIF-i-cant-ly
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Governments should take action, because heavy traffic has a serious impact on health.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Por que parafrasear?
- **Writing:** a primeira frase do ensaio deve **reformular o enunciado** (não copiar).
- **Listening/Reading:** as respostas aparecem com **outras palavras** no áudio/texto.
- **Speaking:** evita repetir a palavra da pergunta.
### Cuidado: sinônimo nem sempre é igual!
- *big problem* → *major problem* ✔ · *large problem* ✘ (pouco natural)
- *Children* → *kids* (informal demais para Writing Task 2) → *young people* ✔
### Modelo — enunciado e paráfrase
> **Enunciado:** *Some people think that the government should spend more money on public transport.*
> **Paráfrase:** *It is often argued that authorities ought to invest more in public transportation systems.*
- people think → *it is often argued* · government → *authorities* · spend money on → *invest in* · public transport → *public transportation systems*

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: collocations
> We need to **make a decision**.
> → make progress / take responsibility / pay attention / take action / make an effort
> This has **a serious impact on** health.
> → a positive effect on / a major influence on / a significant effect on
### 4.2 Sinônimo ou não? (diga se a troca funciona)
1. *a big city* → *a large city*
2. *a big mistake* → *a large mistake*
3. *rapid growth* → *fast growth*
4. *strong coffee* → *powerful coffee*
5. *increase significantly* → *increase considerably*
### 4.3 Paráfrase com mudança de classe
1. *Prices increased dramatically.* → *There was a dramatic…*
2. *Tourism has developed quickly.* → *The quick…*
3. *People are concerned about pollution.* → *There is growing…*

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: 8 paráfrases (15 min)
### A. Reformule — 8 min
Use pelo menos 2 técnicas em cada (sinônimo, classe de palavra, estrutura). O sentido **não pode mudar**.
1. Many young people spend too much time on social media.
2. The government should build more hospitals.
3. Unemployment increased sharply in 2009.
4. Some people believe that homework is not useful.
5. Air pollution is a big problem in large cities.
6. Technology has changed the way we communicate.
7. It is important for children to do sports.
8. Most tourists visit the city in summer.
### B. Leia e justifique — 4 min
O aluno lê as paráfrases. O professor pergunta: *Is the meaning the same? Is the collocation natural?*
### C. Fala — 3 min
Responda em 30 s usando 3 collocations: *What can individuals do to reduce pollution?*

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Collocations.** Escreva uma frase com cada collocation de *make/do/take/have/pay* (20 frases).
**Dia 2 — Adjetivo + substantivo.** Procure 10 collocations com *problem, effect, growth, evidence, impact* (dicionário de collocations online, ex.: Oxford Collocations).
**Dia 3 — Paráfrase.** Reescreva 5 enunciados de IELTS Task 2 (encontre exemplos oficiais) com 2 técnicas cada.
**Dia 4 — Sombra.** Leia a frase-modelo e as 8 paráfrases em voz alta. Grave.
**Dia 5 — Speaking.** Grave 1 minuto: *How has technology changed communication?* Use 4 collocations.
**Dia 6 — Writing.** Escreva 80 palavras sobre poluição usando 5 collocations e 1 paráfrase do enunciado.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
