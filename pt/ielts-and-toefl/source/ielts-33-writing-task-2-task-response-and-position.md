## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** **Task Response**: responder **exatamente** o que o enunciado pede, com uma **posição clara** do início ao fim. Muitos ensaios perdem nota por fugir do tema ou responder só metade da pergunta.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** identificar o tipo de enunciado, o tema exato e escrever uma tese clara + plano.
**Tarefa verificável:** elaborar plano de ensaio com posição inequívoca.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Responda com posição clara em 1 frase:
- *Should university education be free?* · *Is social media good for teenagers?* · *Should cars be banned from city centres?*
**Troque e fale:**
> I **strongly believe** that **university should be free**, **because** **education benefits the whole society**.
> → I partly agree / social media can help teenagers / it connects them, but it can also harm their sleep → I disagree / cars should be banned / many workers depend on them

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Tipos de enunciado (e o que cada um pede)
- **To what extent do you agree or disagree?** → sua opinião (concorda totalmente / parcialmente / discorda)
- **Discuss both views and give your own opinion.** → os dois lados + **sua** opinião
- **What are the causes/problems… and what solutions can you suggest?** → 2 partes: causas/problemas + soluções
- **Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?** → comparar e **decidir** qual pesa mais
- **Two-part question:** *Why is this happening? Is it a positive or negative development?* → responder **as duas**
### Linguagem de tese (posição)
- *I completely agree that… · I partly agree… · I firmly disagree with the view that… · In my opinion, the advantages clearly outweigh… · This essay will argue that…*
### Palavras do enunciado que limitam o tema
- *young people · in developed countries · in the workplace · governments · individuals · only · always · should*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **extent** = ex-TENT · **outweigh** = out-WEIGH · **firmly** = FIRM-ly · **thesis** = THE-sis
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> To a large extent, I agree that governments should invest more in public transport.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Os 3 passos antes de escrever (5 minutos)
1. **Sublinhe** o tema + as palavras que limitam (*governments, in cities, only*).
2. **Identifique** o tipo de enunciado e o que ele exige.
3. **Escreva a tese** (1 frase) e o **plano** (2 parágrafos de corpo com 1 ideia central cada).
### Estrutura padrão (≥250 palavras, ~40 min)
- **Introdução** (2 frases): paráfrase do tema + tese.
- **Body 1** e **Body 2:** frase-tópico + explicação + exemplo.
- **Conclusão** (1–2 frases): retome a tese com outras palavras.
### Modelo
> **Enunciado:** *Some people believe that the best way to reduce traffic in cities is to make public transport free. To what extent do you agree or disagree?*
> **Tema:** reduzir tráfego · **Limite:** *the best way* (a melhor maneira!) · **Tipo:** opinião
> **Tese:** *While free public transport would help to reduce congestion, I do not believe it is the best solution on its own; it must be combined with better services and limits on car use.*
> **Plano:** Body 1 — free transport helps (cost barrier, e.g., Tallinn) · Body 2 — not enough alone (quality, coverage, car habits) → needs other measures
> **Conclusão:** partial agreement restated.

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: teses
> **I completely agree** that **children should learn a second language at primary school**.
> → I partly agree / homework should be banned → I disagree / online classes are as effective as face-to-face ones → The advantages outweigh the disadvantages / living in a big city
### 4.2 Identifique o tipo e o que falta
1. *Many people move to cities for work. What problems does this cause, and what can be done?*
2. *Some say that sport at school is a waste of time. Others think it is essential. Discuss both views and give your opinion.*
3. *More people are working from home. Is this a positive or negative development?*
4. *Tourism brings money but also damages the environment. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?*
### 4.3 Tese clara × vaga
a) *There are many different opinions about this topic.* b) *I firmly believe that the benefits of tourism outweigh its environmental costs, provided that governments regulate it.*

## Gabarito do professor
- **4.2:** 1 problem–solution (problemas + soluções) · 2 discussion (dois lados + opinião) · 3 positive/negative (avaliar) · 4 advantages/disadvantages (decidir qual pesa mais).
- **5 (exemplos de tese):** A — *Although space exploration has led to valuable technologies, I believe that most public funding should go to urgent problems such as poverty and climate change.* B — *This trend is mainly caused by economic independence and later marriage, and I believe it is largely positive, although it can increase loneliness among older people.*
