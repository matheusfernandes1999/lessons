## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** a **Part 3**: 2–4 pessoas (estudantes e/ou tutor) discutem um trabalho acadêmico. As perguntas pedem **opiniões, concordância/discordância e decisões finais**.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** identificar quem concorda, quem discorda e o que o grupo **decide** no final.
**Tarefa verificável:** identificar opiniões e decisões em grupo.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- Do you like group work? Why or why not?
- How do you usually decide things in a group: vote? discussion? the leader decides?
**Troque e fale:**
> I see your point, but **I think we should focus on one country**.
> → I'm not sure about that / it might be too broad → That's a good idea / let's do it → I agree up to a point / we also need data
**Revisão da aula 14:** dê 2 paráfrases de *disappointing*.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Concordar
- *Exactly. · Absolutely. · That's a good point. · I couldn't agree more. · Fair enough.*
### Discordar (educadamente)
- *I'm not so sure. · I see what you mean, but… · I take your point, but… · Do you really think so? · I'm not convinced.*
### Concordar parcialmente
- *I agree up to a point. · That's true, but… · Maybe, although…*
### Decidir
- *So, shall we go with…? · Let's settle on… · OK, that's decided then. · We'll leave that for now. · Let's stick with…*
### Vocabulário de trabalhos acadêmicos
- **assignment · presentation · questionnaire · survey · interview · data · findings · literature review · deadline · draft · tutor · feedback · reference**

### Pronúncia: entonação que mostra opinião
- Concordância forte ↘: *ABsolutely.* · Dúvida ↗↘: *Do you REALLY think so?* · Parcial: *I agree… up to a POINT.*
- **convinced / convenient · settle / subtle**
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> I take your point, but I'm not convinced — let's stick with the survey.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Como acompanhar uma discussão
1. **Antes:** leia as perguntas e anote **os nomes** dos falantes.
2. **Durante:** observe as **reações** (*Exactly / I'm not sure*). A opinião de uma pessoa pode **mudar** depois do argumento da outra.
3. **Decisão final:** geralmente no fim do trecho: *So, let's…* / *OK, we'll…*
### Modelo
🎧 > **Tom:** I think we should use interviews for our project. **Sara:** Interviews take ages, though. A questionnaire would reach more people. **Tom:** True, but the answers would be less detailed. **Sara:** Hmm, fair enough. What if we do a short questionnaire first and then interview five people? **Tom:** That's a great idea. Let's go with that.
- Tom: entrevistas → aceita a mistura · Sara: questionário → propõe mistura · **Decisão: questionário + 5 entrevistas**

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: reagindo a ideias
O professor propõe; o aluno reage (concorda, discorda ou parcial) **com uma razão**:
> “Let's do our presentation on social media.” · “We should meet every day.” · “We don't need a literature review.” · “Let's divide the work equally.” · “We should start with the conclusion.”
### 4.2 Quem pensa o quê?
🎧 > **Tutor:** So, how's the research on the four-day week going? **Ravi:** Good. I think the main benefit is mental health. **Jo:** I'd say it's productivity, actually — companies care about that more. **Tutor:** Interesting. Ravi, do you agree? **Ravi:** Well, Jo has a point; companies won't change without productivity gains. But for workers, it's mental health. **Tutor:** So maybe your presentation could show both perspectives? **Jo:** That works. Ravi can do the workers' side, and I'll do the companies'.
1. What does Ravi think is the main benefit? 2. What does Jo think? 3. What does the tutor suggest? 4. Who will present the companies' perspective?

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada (15 min)
**Contexto:** dois estudantes, Kate e Ben, e a tutora, Dr. Hughes, discutem um projeto sobre desperdício de comida no campus.
**Perguntas (leia antes, 1 min):**
1. *What do Kate and Ben disagree about at first?* A) the topic B) the method C) the deadline
2. *Why does Ben prefer to observe the cafeteria?* A) It's cheaper. B) People don't always tell the truth in surveys. C) The tutor told him to.
3. *What does Dr. Hughes say about their sample?* A) It's too small. B) It's too large. C) It's fine.
4. *What do they finally decide to do?* A) observation only B) survey only C) both
5. *What will Kate do first?* A) contact the cafeteria manager B) write the questionnaire C) read previous studies
🎧
> **Kate:** So, for our project on food waste, I think a survey of students is the obvious choice.
> **Ben:** I'm not so sure. People say they don't waste food, but then they throw half their lunch away. I'd rather observe what actually happens in the cafeteria.
> **Kate:** I see what you mean, but observation takes so much time.
> **Dr. Hughes:** Both methods have value. But you mentioned surveying thirty students — that won't tell you much about a campus of eight thousand.
> **Kate:** We could aim for two hundred online.
> **Dr. Hughes:** Much better. And Ben's point about honesty is important.
> **Ben:** What if we do both? A survey to see what people think, and two days of observation to compare.
> **Kate:** OK, I'm convinced. It'll make the results more interesting.
> **Dr. Hughes:** Good. Remember you'll need permission to observe in the cafeteria.
> **Kate:** Right, I'll email the manager today, before we do anything else. Ben, could you start on the questions?
> **Ben:** Sure.
**Acertos:** __/5
**Fala (4 min):** relate a discussão em 1 minuto: quem pensava o quê e o que decidiram.

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Expressões.** Escreva 3 frases para cada grupo (concordar, discordar, parcial, decidir).
**Dia 2 — Listening oficial.** Faça uma Part 3 oficial e anote a opinião de cada falante.
**Dia 3 — Sombra.** Leia o diálogo de Kate, Ben e Dr. Hughes (três vozes). Grave.
**Dia 4 — Speaking.** Grave um mini debate consigo mesmo(a): 2 opiniões sobre *online vs. face-to-face classes* e uma decisão.
**Dia 5 — Relato.** Escreva 80 palavras relatando uma reunião de grupo real ou imaginária (quem disse o quê, decisão).
**Dia 6 — Listening.** Assista a um debate curto (YouTube) e anote 5 expressões de concordância/discordância.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
