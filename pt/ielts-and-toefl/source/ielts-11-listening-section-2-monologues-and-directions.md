## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** a **Part 2**: uma pessoa fala sozinha (guia, funcionário, organizador) sobre um lugar ou evento. Muitas vezes há um **mapa ou planta** para rotular.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** seguir direções em inglês e localizar lugares em um mapa a partir do áudio.
**Tarefa verificável:** completar um mapa e responder perguntas.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- How do you get from your house to the nearest supermarket? Give directions.
- Describe your living room: what's next to the sofa? What's opposite the window?
**Frases úteis:** *Go straight ahead · Turn left/right · It's on your left · It's next to/opposite…*
**Revisão da aula 10:** soletre: *Gallagher, Quayside*.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Preposições e expressões de lugar
- **next to / beside** — ao lado · **opposite / across from** — em frente (do outro lado)
- **in front of / behind** — na frente / atrás · **between… and…** — entre
- **in the corner (of)** — no canto · **at the end of** — no fim de
- **to the left/right of** · **on the left/right-hand side**
- **north / south / east / west** · **in the north-east corner**
- **just past / just before** — logo depois / logo antes · **beyond** — além de
- **adjacent to** — adjacente · **overlooking** — com vista para
### Verbos de direção
- **go straight on / ahead** · **turn left/right** · **take the first/second turning** · **go past** · **cross** · **follow the path** · **head towards**
### Lugares
- **entrance / exit · reception · car park · footpath · bridge · fountain · pond · playground · picnic area · gift shop · toilets · lift (UK) / elevator (US)**

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **opposite** = OP-po-site · **adjacent** = ad-JA-cent · **beyond** = be-YOND
- **left / lift · path / pass · pond / pound · past / passed** (iguais!)
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Go past the fountain, and the gift shop is opposite the pond, just beyond the bridge.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Estratégia para mapas
1. **Antes do áudio (30 s):** encontre o **ponto de partida** (*You are here*, entrada) e os **pontos de referência** já rotulados.
2. **Oriente-se:** onde é norte? O que está à esquerda/direita do ponto de partida?
3. **Siga com o dedo** enquanto ouve: o falante quase sempre descreve **na ordem das perguntas**.
4. **Cuidado** com mudanças: *“The café used to be here, but it has moved to…”*
### Modelo
> **Mapa:** Entrada (sul) → caminho para o norte → lago no centro → ponte a leste do lago.
🎧 > “As you come in through the main entrance, follow the path straight ahead. The visitor centre is the first building on your left. Keep going until you reach the lake. The café is on the far side of the lake, opposite the bridge — you can't miss it.”
→ Visitor centre: **à esquerda logo após a entrada** · Café: **do outro lado do lago, em frente à ponte**

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: dando direções
> The **café** is **next to** the **gift shop**.
> → toilets / behind / reception → playground / opposite / picnic area → car park / at the end of / main road → museum / to the right of / fountain
### 4.2 Desenhe enquanto ouve (aluno desenha uma planta simples)
🎧 > “The new sports centre has a simple layout. When you enter, reception is directly in front of you. The changing rooms are to the left of reception. The swimming pool takes up the whole right-hand side of the building. Behind reception, there's a large gym, and the café is in the far left corner, next to the changing rooms.”
Depois, o aluno descreve o desenho de volta para o professor.
### 4.3 Monólogo com perguntas
🎧 > “Welcome to the summer festival. Before you start exploring, a few practical points. The first-aid tent is beside the main stage. If you lose a child, please go to the information point, which is at the north gate — not the south gate where you came in. Food stalls are along the river, and drinking water is free at all of them. The last bus back to the city leaves at 11:30, from the car park.”
1. Where is the first-aid tent? 2. Where is the information point? 3. Where is water available? 4. What time is the last bus?

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: mapa + perguntas (15 min)
**Professor:** desenhe (ou cole) este mapa simples no documento antes do áudio:
> **Riverside Park** — Main entrance (sul). Caminho principal vai para o norte. **Lago** no centro. **Rio** no lado leste (direita). Locais rotulados com letras: **A** (logo à esquerda da entrada), **B** (à direita da entrada), **C** (oeste do lago), **D** (norte do lago), **E** (ao lado do rio, perto da ponte), **F** (canto noroeste).
Rotule: 1 Café ___ · 2 Toilets ___ · 3 Children's playground ___ · 4 Boat hire ___ · 5 Rose garden ___
🎧 Áudio:
> “Good morning and welcome to Riverside Park. I'll give you a quick overview of the layout. As you come through the main entrance, the building immediately on your right houses the toilets. Straight ahead is the lake, which is the heart of the park. On the western side of the lake you'll find the children's playground — it's very popular, so it can get busy at weekends. If you'd like to take a boat out, boat hire is by the river, next to the bridge on the east side. Our famous rose garden is in the far north-west corner, beyond the old oak trees. And finally, the café — it used to be by the entrance, on the left, but that building is now our ticket office. The café has moved to the north side of the lake, where there's more space and a lovely view.”
6. What is the building on the left of the entrance now? 7. When can the playground get busy?
**Acertos:** __/7
**Fala (4 min):** o aluno explica como ir da entrada até o rose garden passando pelo café.
