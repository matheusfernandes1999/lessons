## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** a tarefa *Write an Email*: você recebe uma situação e **alguns pontos que precisa incluir**. O e-mail precisa ter **abertura, contexto, conteúdo (os pontos pedidos) e fechamento**, com tom adequado.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** escrever um e-mail claro de 100–150 palavras pedindo informações.
**Tarefa verificável:** redigir e-mail solicitando informações pertinentes.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- Who do you write emails to in your daily life?
- How do you start and end a formal email in Portuguese? And in English?
- Have you ever received a confusing email? Why was it confusing?
**Revisão da aula 34:** transforme em pergunta indireta: *When is the deadline?* → *Could you tell me…?*

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Abertura
- **Formal:** *Dear Professor Smith, · Dear Dr. Lee, · Dear Admissions Office, · To whom it may concern,* (quando não sabe o nome)
- **Semi-formal:** *Hello Ms. Garcia, · Hi Professor Lee,*
### Contexto (1ª frase — quem é você e por que escreve)
- *My name is… and I'm a second-year student in…*
- *I'm writing to ask about / to inquire about…*
- *I'm interested in… and I'd like some information about…*
### Pedido de informação
- *Could you please tell me…?* · *I'd like to know whether…* · *I was also wondering if…* · *Would it be possible to…?*
### Fechamento
- *Thank you in advance for your help.* · *I look forward to hearing from you.* · *Please let me know if you need any further information.*
- *Best regards, · Kind regards, · Sincerely,* + nome
### Erros comuns de brasileiros
- ✘ *Dear Mr. John* → ✔ *Dear Mr. Smith* (sobrenome!) · ✘ *I want to know* (direto demais) → ✔ *I'd like to know* · ✘ *Waiting your answer* → ✔ *I look forward to hearing from you.*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **inquire** = in-QUIRE · **regards** = re-GARDS · **sincerely** = sin-CERE-ly · **advance** = ad-VANCE
- **hearing / hiring** · **further / farther / father**
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Thank you in advance, and I look forward to hearing from you.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### A estrutura de 4 partes
1. **Saudação** adequada ao destinatário.
2. **Contexto** (1–2 frases): quem você é + por que escreve.
3. **Corpo**: um parágrafo curto para **cada ponto pedido** no enunciado. Use perguntas educadas.
4. **Fechamento** + assinatura.
### Leia o enunciado como um checklist
> *You are interested in a summer research program at your university. Write an email to the program coordinator. In your email: (1) introduce yourself, (2) explain why you are interested, (3) ask about requirements and dates.*
✔ Cada item vira uma parte do e-mail. **Esquecer um item custa pontos.**
### Modelo
> Dear Dr. Martins,
> My name is Camila Rocha, and I'm a second-year biology student. I'm writing to ask about the summer research program in the Ecology Lab.
> I'm very interested in the program because I'd like to gain hands-on experience with field research. Last semester, I took Professor Lima's ecology course, and I really enjoyed the lab sessions.
> Could you please tell me what the requirements are to apply? I'd also like to know when the program starts and whether students need to stay on campus during the summer.
> Thank you in advance for your help. I look forward to hearing from you.
> Best regards,
> Camila Rocha
(≈ 120 palavras) O aluno lê em voz alta como se ditasse o e-mail.

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: frases de contexto
> My name is **Pedro Alves**, and I'm a **first-year engineering student**. I'm writing to **ask about the robotics club**.
> → a third-year economics student / inquire about internship opportunities → a graduate student in history / request access to the archives → an exchange student from Brazil / ask about housing options

### 4.2 Transforme em pergunta educada (oral e escrito)
1. What are the requirements? → *Could you please tell me…*
2. Is there a fee? → *I'd like to know whether…*
3. When does it start? → *I was also wondering…*
4. Can I apply late? → *Would it be possible to…*

### 4.3 Encontre os problemas neste e-mail
> Hi John,
> I want to know about the course of English. How much is it? When it starts? Waiting your answer.
> Bye, Lucas
