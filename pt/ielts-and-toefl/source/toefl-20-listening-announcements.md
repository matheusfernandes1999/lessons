## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** *Listen to an Announcement*: avisos falados no campus (mudanças de horário, eventos, regras). A prova pergunta **o que muda, quando, quem é afetado e o que fazer**.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** ouvir um anúncio uma vez e anotar as 4 informações essenciais.
**Tarefa verificável:** registrar quatro informações essenciais de dois anúncios.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- Where do you usually hear announcements? (airport, train station, school, supermarket…)
- Have you ever missed an important announcement? What happened?
**Frases úteis:** *They announced that…* · *I didn't catch the…* · *Apparently,…*
**Troque e fale:**
> They announced that **the flight was delayed**.
> → the gate had changed / the store was closing in 10 minutes / the meeting was moved to Friday
*(atenção: discurso indireto → passado: is → was, has → had)*
**Revisão da aula 19:** relate um diálogo da última aula com P/S/N.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
Palavras de anúncios:
- **Attention, students/passengers** — atenção
- **due to** — devido a · **as of / starting** — a partir de
- **reschedule / postpone / cancel** — remarcar / adiar / cancelar
- **relocate / move to** — transferir para
- **reminder** — lembrete · **registration** — inscrição
- **be required to** — ser obrigado a · **be encouraged to** — ser incentivado a
- **upcoming** — próximo (evento) · **ongoing** — em andamento
- **shuttle** — ônibus circular · **venue** — local do evento
- **in case of** — em caso de · **for more information, visit…**
### Números e datas (onde os erros acontecem)
- **thirteen / thirty** · **fourteen / forty** · **fifteen / fifty** — nos “-teen” a tônica é no final: thir-TEEN; nos “-ty”, no começo: THIR-ty.
- Datas: *March 3rd* (third) · *the 21st* (twenty-first) · *2:15* (two fifteen / a quarter past two)

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
O professor fala um número de cada par; o aluno escreve qual ouviu:
- **13 / 30 · 14 / 40 · 15 / 50 · 16 / 60 · 17 / 70 · 18 / 80 · 19 / 90**
- **Room 214 / Room 240** · **at 4:15 / at 4:50** · **$13 / $30**
Frase:
> The meeting is in Room 14 on the fourth floor at 4:40, not 4:14.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### O formato de quase todo anúncio
1. **Abertura:** *Attention, students…* (quem é afetado)
2. **Mudança/evento:** o quê + quando + onde
3. **Motivo:** *due to / because of*
4. **Instrução:** *Please… / You are required to… / Make sure…*
5. **Mais informação:** site, e-mail, escritório
### Como anotar (grade de 4)
**O QUÊ | QUANDO | QUEM | FAZER** — use abreviações: *mtg (meeting), Fri, rm 204, →*
### Modelo
🎧 > Attention, all residents of Oak Hall. Due to a water pipe repair, there will be no hot water on Tuesday from 8 a.m. to 2 p.m. Residents who need to shower are encouraged to use the facilities in the Recreation Center, which will open one hour earlier, at 6 a.m. We apologize for the inconvenience.
- **O quê:** sem água quente (conserto) · **Quando:** ter, 8h–14h · **Quem:** moradores Oak Hall · **Fazer:** usar o Rec Center (abre 6h)

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: relatando anúncios
> They announced that **there will be no hot water on Tuesday**, so residents **should use the Recreation Center**.
> → the career fair has moved to the gym / students should check the new map → the bookstore will close early on Friday / students should buy books before Thursday → the bus will not run on Sunday / students should use the city bus

### 4.2 Ditado de números (o professor lê, o aluno escreve)
> Room 316 · 4:45 p.m. · $18.50 · March 13th · Building 40 · 1,250 students · 15 minutes · the 30th of May

### 4.3 Anúncio curto
🎧 > Good morning, everyone. Just a reminder that the deadline to drop a class without a grade is this Friday, October 13. After that, dropped classes will appear on your transcript as a “W.” If you're considering dropping a class, please talk to your advisor first. Forms are available online.
O QUÊ ______ · QUANDO ______ · QUEM ______ · FAZER ______

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: 2 anúncios (15 min)
🎧 Cada anúncio **uma vez**. O aluno preenche a grade de 4 enquanto ouve. Depois relata oralmente em 45 segundos.
### Anúncio A
> Attention, students. This year's Career Fair, originally scheduled for Wednesday, November 14, in the Student Center, has been moved to Thursday, November 15, and will take place in the Sports Arena. The change is due to the large number of companies — more than 80 have registered. The fair runs from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Students are encouraged to bring several copies of their résumé, and those who want a professional photo for their LinkedIn profile can get one for free at the entrance. A free shuttle will run from the library every 15 minutes.
O QUÊ ______ · QUANDO ______ · ONDE ______ · FAZER ______
### Anúncio B
> Good afternoon. This is a message from the IT department. As of next Monday, all students will be required to use two-step verification to access their university email. To set it up, download the authentication app and follow the instructions on the IT website. Students who have not completed the setup by Sunday at midnight will be temporarily locked out of their accounts. If you need help, the IT Help Desk on the second floor of the library will be open extended hours this week, until 9 p.m.
O QUÊ ______ · QUANDO ______ · QUEM ______ · FAZER ______
**Informações corretas:** A __/4 · B __/4

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Números.** Peça a alguém (ou use um gerador de áudio) para ditar 20 números com -teen/-ty. Anote.
**Dia 2 — Vocabulário.** Escreva um anúncio de 5 frases usando *due to, as of, required, encouraged, venue*.
**Dia 3 — Sombra.** Leia o Anúncio A em voz alta 3 vezes como um locutor. Grave a última.
**Dia 4 — Listening real.** Procure no YouTube “airport announcements” ou “university announcements”. Preencha a grade de 4 para 3 anúncios.
**Dia 5 — Speaking.** Grave 45 s relatando o Anúncio B para um colega que não ouviu.
**Dia 6 — Writing.** Escreva um e-mail curto para um amigo contando a mudança da Career Fair.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
