## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** tarefas de **completar** (formulário, tabela, notas, frases). O segredo é **prever** o tipo de palavra que falta e **respeitar o limite** (*NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS*, *ONE WORD ONLY*).
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** antes de ouvir, dizer se a lacuna pede substantivo, adjetivo, número ou verbo — e escrever a resposta com a forma e a grafia corretas.
**Tarefa verificável:** preencher oito itens com grafia e limite corretos.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
O professor lê frases com uma lacuna (“bip”); o aluno diz **que tipo de palavra** falta:
- *The tour includes a visit to the old ___.* (substantivo)
- *The museum is ___ on Mondays.* (adjetivo: closed/open)
- *Tickets cost £___.* (número)
- *Visitors should ___ their bags at the entrance.* (verbo)
**Revisão da aula 11:** dê direções da entrada da sua casa até a cozinha.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Instruções de limite (leia com atenção!)
- **ONE WORD ONLY** → 1 palavra
- **ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER** → 1 palavra, ou 1 número, ou 1 palavra + 1 número
- **NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS** → 1 ou 2 palavras
- **NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER** → até 2 palavras + 1 número
**Contam como palavra:** *a, the, of* · **Hífen** conta como 1 palavra (*part-time*) · **Números** escritos em algarismos contam como número.
### Pistas gramaticais nas lacunas
- **a / an ___** → substantivo singular (e *an* → começa com som de vogal)
- **many / several ___** → substantivo plural (**-s!**)
- **very / quite ___** → adjetivo
- **to ___ / should ___** → verbo na forma base
- **£ / $ / % ___** → número
### Vocabulário de palestras/notas
- **aim · method · result · cause · effect · stage · factor · feature · benefit · drawback · sample · equipment**

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita (plurais que somem!)
- **benefit / benefits · stage / stages** (/ɪz/) · **result / results** · **method / methods** (/dz/)
- Fala rápida: *a lot of samples* → *ə-LO-tə-SAM-plz*
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> The main benefits of the method are its low cost and its simple equipment.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Estratégia em 4 passos
1. **Leia o título** da tabela/notas: qual é o tema?
2. **Circule a pista** antes de cada lacuna (*a, several, very, £*).
3. **Preveja** o tipo e, se possível, o sentido (*a type of food? a day?*).
4. **Confira** depois: limite de palavras, plural, ortografia.
### A resposta costuma vir com sinônimo na pergunta
> Notas: *Main problem: lack of ___.* 🎧 *“The biggest issue was that we didn't have enough **funding**.”* → **funding**
### Modelo — tabela
> | Tour | Starts at | Price | Highlight |
> | Morning walk | 9:30 | £8 | the old ___ (1) |
> | Boat trip | ___ (2) | £15 | view of the ___ (3) |
🎧 > “The morning walk starts at half past nine and costs eight pounds — the best part is definitely the old **market**. The boat trip leaves at **quarter to eleven**, costs fifteen pounds, and gives you a wonderful view of the **castle**.”
→ (1) **market** (2) **10:45** (3) **castle**

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Preveja (sem áudio) — tipo de palavra e um exemplo possível
1. *The workshop is suitable for ___ students.* 2. *Participants must bring their own ___.* 3. *The course lasts ___ weeks.* 4. *The final session focuses on ___ skills.* 5. *A ___ will be given at the end.* 6. *Most participants found it very ___.*
### 4.2 Agora ouça e complete (NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER)
🎧 > “Our photography workshop is ideal for beginner students — no experience needed. You'll need to bring your own camera; phones aren't allowed in the sessions, I'm afraid. The course runs for six weeks, and in the last session we concentrate on editing skills. Everyone who finishes gets a certificate. Last year, almost all the participants said they found it extremely useful.”
### 4.3 Troque e fale: confirme a forma
> The answer is **“cameras”**, plural, because **the gap comes after “several”**.
> → “camera” / singular / “their own” + item → “editing” / noun modifier / “___ skills” → “useful” / adjective / “very”

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: notas de palestra (15 min)
**Instrução:** *Complete the notes. Write NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER.*
> **Urban Beekeeping**
> - Became popular in cities about (1) ______ years ago
> - Main reason: concern about falling (2) ______
> - Hives often placed on (3) ______
> - City bees produce honey with more (4) ______ because of the variety of plants
> - Problem: too many hives → not enough (5) ______ for all bees
> - Some cities now require beekeepers to get a (6) ______
> - Beginners advised to take a (7) ______ course first
> - Cost of basic equipment: around £(8) ______
🎧 > “Today I'm going to talk about urban beekeeping, which has really taken off in cities over the last fifteen years or so. Why? Mainly because people became worried about the decline in bee populations — you've probably seen the headlines. In cities, hives are usually kept on rooftops, where they're safe from people and pets. Interestingly, honey from city bees often has more flavour than countryside honey, because bees in cities visit a huge variety of plants in gardens and parks. However, there's a downside. In some areas, there are now so many hives that there isn't enough food — that is, flowers — for all the bees, and wild bees suffer as a result. That's why some cities have started asking beekeepers to get a licence. If you're thinking of starting, I'd strongly recommend doing a short training course first. As for costs, you can buy the basic equipment for about two hundred and fifty pounds.”
**Acertos:** __/8
**Fala (4 min):** usando só as notas, explique a palestra em 1 minuto.
