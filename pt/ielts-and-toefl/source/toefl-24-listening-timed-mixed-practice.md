## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** fazer os **4 tipos de Listening** em sequência — *Choose a Response*, *Conversation*, *Announcement*, *Academic Talk* — e entender **por que** você erra.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** classificar seus erros (não ouvi / não entendi a palavra / caí na armadilha / perdi a concentração) e escolher o treino certo para cada um.
**Tarefa verificável:** concluir bloco misto e descrever causas dos erros.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- Which listening task is the hardest for you now? Why?
- What helps you concentrate when you listen?
- What do you do when you miss a word?
**Troque e fale:**
> I missed the answer because **the speaker talked too fast**.
> → I didn't know the word “…” → I chose an option with repeated words → I was still thinking about the previous question → I didn't catch the number
**Revisão da aula 23:** transforme em frase: *temp ↑ b/c CO2 ↑; e.g. Arctic ice ↓ 40%*.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
Vocabulário de análise de erros:
- **catch** — pegar/entender (na escuta) — *I didn't catch the date.*
- **mishear** — ouvir errado — *I misheard “fifteen” as “fifty”.*
- **lose focus / concentration** — perder a concentração
- **fall for (a trap)** — cair (numa armadilha)
- **second-guess** — duvidar de si e trocar a resposta
- **overthink** — pensar demais
- **gist** — ideia geral — *I got the gist, but not the details.*
- **accent** — sotaque · **speed** — velocidade · **background noise** — barulho de fundo
**Chunks de análise:**
- *I got the gist, but I missed…*
- *I fell for the trap because…*
- *I second-guessed myself and changed a correct answer.*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **catch / cash / cats** · **gist / just / jest** · **focus / folks / fox**
- Fala rápida: *I didn't catch it* → *I-din-CA-chit* · *What did he say?* → *whaddee-SAY*
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> I got the gist, but I didn't catch the date.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Os 4 tipos de erro e o treino certo
- **Som** (não reconheci a palavra que eu conheço): treino de sons parecidos, ditado, sombra.
- **Vocabulário** (não conheço a palavra): listas temáticas, revisão espaçada.
- **Armadilha** (escolhi eco/exagero/função errada): treino de eliminação e paráfrase.
- **Atenção/tempo** (perdi o foco, fiquei preso na pergunta anterior): treino de “soltar” — responder e seguir.
### Modelo de análise (fale junto)
> In the conversation, I missed question 2 because I misheard the room number — that's a sound problem. In the lecture, I fell for a trap: the option repeated the word “energy,” but it didn't answer the question. Next week, I'll do number dictations and practice eliminating options with repeated words.

## 5. Bloco misto cronometrado (15 min)
**Regras:** 🎧 tudo **uma vez só**. O aluno responde no papel e não pede repetição.
### Parte 1 — Choose a Response (4 itens)
1. “Would you like me to save you a seat?” — a) That'd be great, thanks. b) I saved money. c) The seat is red.
2. “Why don't you take the bus instead?” — a) Because I like it. b) Good idea, it's cheaper. c) The bus was late.
3. “I can't believe the exam was moved again.” — a) Me neither. That's so frustrating. b) I believe in exams. c) It moved to the left.
4. “Have you decided on a topic for your project?” — a) I'm leaning towards renewable energy. b) The project is big. c) I decided yesterday to eat.
### Parte 2 — Conversation
> **Student:** Hi, I signed up for the Spanish conversation club, but I haven't received any information.
> **Coordinator:** Let me check. Ah — your email address has a typo, so our messages bounced back.
> **Student:** Oh no. Did I miss anything?
> **Coordinator:** Just the first meeting, but it was mostly introductions. The next one is Thursday at 4:30 in the Language Center, Room 12.
> **Student:** Great. Should I bring anything?
> **Coordinator:** Not really — maybe a notebook. I'll fix your email now so you get the materials.
5. Why didn't the student receive information? 6. Where and when is the next meeting? 7. What will the coordinator do?
### Parte 3 — Announcement
> Attention, students. The main parking lot will be closed for resurfacing from Monday, March 4, through Friday, March 8. During this period, students with parking permits may use the West Lot, and a free shuttle will run from the West Lot to the main campus every 10 minutes from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Please note that cars left in the main lot on Monday morning will be towed.
8. How long will the lot be closed? 9. What happens to cars left in the main lot?
### Parte 4 — Academic Talk
> Let's talk about why we yawn. For a long time, people believed we yawn because we need more oxygen. However, experiments in the 1980s showed that breathing extra oxygen didn't reduce yawning. A more recent theory is that yawning cools the brain. Studies found that people yawn more when the air is slightly cooler than body temperature, and less in very hot weather. As for why yawning is contagious — you probably yawned just hearing about it — some researchers link it to empathy, since people tend to “catch” yawns more from friends and family than from strangers.
10. Which theory was rejected? 11. What evidence supports the brain-cooling theory? 12. Why does the professor mention friends and family?
**Acertos:** __/12

**Dia 5 — Sombra.** Leia a palestra sobre bocejo em voz alta 2 vezes. Grave a segunda.
**Dia 6 — Speaking.** Grave 1 minuto: *Describe your listening progress and your plan for next week.*
