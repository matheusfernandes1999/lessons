## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** a **Part 2**: você recebe um **cartão** (cue card) com um tema e 3–4 tópicos, tem **1 minuto para preparar notas** e fala **até 2 minutos** sem interrupção. Depois, o examinador pode fazer 1–2 perguntas curtas.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** usar o minuto de preparação com eficiência e falar 1,5–2 minutos com estrutura clara.
**Tarefa verificável:** falar até dois minutos a partir de cue card.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- How long can you speak in English without stopping? 30 seconds? 1 minute?
**Desafio crescente:** fale sobre *your favourite food* por 30 s → depois sobre *a place you like* por 60 s → depois *a person you admire* por 90 s.
**Revisão da aula 42:** responda: *Do you like birthdays?* (girando, se necessário)

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Estrutura de uma fala de 2 minutos
- **Abertura (10 s):** *I'd like to talk about… · I'm going to describe…*
- **Tópicos do cartão (80–90 s):** siga a ordem: *As for (where)… · The reason why… · What I remember most is…*
- **Explicação final (“and explain…”) (20–30 s):** *The main reason this is special to me is… · Looking back, …*
- **Fechamento (5 s):** *So, that's why… · That's basically it.*
### Transições entre tópicos
- *Moving on to… · As for… · In terms of… · Another thing I should mention is… · What's more…*
### Notas de 1 minuto (palavras, não frases!)
- 3–4 palavras-chave por tópico · símbolos (→, +, !) · 1 detalhe sensorial (cor, som, cheiro) · 1 sentimento

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- *I'd like to talk about* → *aid-LAIK-tə-TALK-ə-BOUT* · *As for* → *az-FOR* · *Looking back* → *LOOK-ing-BACK*
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> I'd like to talk about a trip I took to Chile, and as for why I remember it, it was my first time abroad.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Cue card de exemplo
> **Describe a place you visited that you really enjoyed.** You should say: *where it was · when you went there · what you did there* · and explain why you enjoyed it.
### Notas (1 minuto)
> *Ouro Preto, MG · 2022, July, w/ uni friends · walked hills, churches, gold mine, pão de queijo! · why: history + friends + cold nights, fireplace*
### Fala modelo (≈1 min 50 s)
> *I'd like to talk about Ouro Preto, a historic town in Minas Gerais, in the south-east of Brazil. I went there in July 2022 with three friends from university, right after our exams.*
> *As for what we did, we spent most of the time walking up and down the steep cobbled streets, which was exhausting but beautiful. We visited several baroque churches — some of them are covered in gold inside — and we also went down into an old gold mine, which was a bit scary because it was dark and narrow. In the evenings, we ate a lot of local food, especially pão de queijo and feijão tropeiro.*
> *The main reason I enjoyed it so much was the combination of history and good company. I'd studied the colonial period at school, but seeing the buildings in person made it feel real. Also, it was really cold at night, so we sat around a fireplace in our guesthouse, talking for hours. Looking back, it was one of the best trips I've ever had, and I'd love to go back one day.*

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Notas em 1 minuto (cronômetro!)
O professor dá um cartão; o aluno faz notas em 60 s e **só depois** fala 1 minuto.
> **Describe a useful object you own.** You should say: what it is · how long you have had it · how you use it · and explain why it is useful.
### 4.2 Troque e fale: transições
> **I'd like to talk about** **my bike**. **As for** **how long I've had it**, **I bought it** **three years ago**.
> → my laptop / how I use it / mainly for work and films → my grandmother's ring / where it came from / she gave it to me when I graduated
### 4.3 Expanda um tópico
Cada tópico do cartão deve ter **2–3 frases**. Pratique com *“how you use it”*: o que · quando · um exemplo · um sentimento.

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: 2 long turns (15 min)
**Rodada 1 (4 min):** 1 min de notas + até 2 min de fala + 1 pergunta curta do professor.
> **Describe a teacher who had a positive influence on you.** You should say: who this teacher was · what subject he or she taught · what this teacher was like · and explain why he or she influenced you.
**Professor (rodada 1):** tempo falado ____ · cobriu os 4 pontos? ☐☐☐☐ · pausas longas __
**Feedback (2 min)**
**Rodada 2 (4 min):**
> **Describe a skill you would like to learn in the future.** You should say: what the skill is · why you want to learn it · how you would learn it · and explain how it would help you.
**Professor (rodada 2):** tempo ____ · pontos ☐☐☐☐ · pausas __ · *(atenção: este cartão exige futuro/condicional: would, I'd like to, I'm planning to)*
**Perguntas de seguimento (1 min):** *Do you think it will be difficult to learn? · Do you know anyone who has this skill?*

## 6. Feedback (3 min)
- [ ] Usou bem o minuto de notas (palavras-chave)?
- [ ] Falou 1,5–2 minutos?
- [ ] Cobriu todos os pontos, inclusive o “explain”?
- [ ] Tempos verbais adequados ao cartão (passado na 1, futuro/condicional na 2)?
**Ajuste:** ______

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**. Use cronômetro (1 min notas + 2 min fala) e grave.
**Dia 1 — Cartão: a person.** *Describe a friend you have known for a long time.*
**Dia 2 — Cartão: a place.** *Describe a park or garden you like to visit.*
**Dia 3 — Cartão: an object.** *Describe a gift you gave someone.*
**Dia 4 — Cartão: an event.** *Describe a celebration you enjoyed.*
**Dia 5 — Escute.** Ouça as 4 gravações. Qual teve melhor estrutura? Qual foi mais curta?
**Dia 6 — Regrave** a mais fraca, com notas melhores.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
