## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** a **Part 3** (4–5 min): perguntas **abstratas** ligadas ao tema da Part 2 — sobre a sociedade, as pessoas em geral, comparações entre passado e presente. Não é mais sobre **você**, é sobre **o mundo**.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** responder perguntas abstratas com opinião + razão + exemplo/comparação em 30–45 segundos.
**Tarefa verificável:** responder seis questões abstratas com justificativa.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Transforme a pergunta pessoal em geral:
- *Do you like reading?* (Part 1) → *Why do fewer young people read books today?* (Part 3)
- *Describe a teacher you liked.* (Part 2) → *What makes a good teacher?* (Part 3)
- *Do you use social media?* → ______
**Revisão da aula 44:** conte em 30 s um momento em que você ficou surpreso (passado contínuo + perfeito).

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Generalizar
- *In general, … · Generally speaking, … · On the whole, … · Most people tend to… · In my country, people usually…*
### Opinião
- *I'd say that… · From my point of view, … · It seems to me that… · I strongly believe…*
### Comparar (passado × presente, grupos)
- *Compared to the past, … · Nowadays, … whereas in the past… · Young people tend to…, while older people…*
### Razão e exemplo
- *This is mainly because… · One reason is that… · Take… for example · A good example of this is…*
### Estrutura útil: O-R-E (Opinion – Reason – Example)
- + opcional: **contraste** (*Having said that, …*)

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- *Generally speaking* → *JEN-ral-ly-SPEAK-ing* · *Having said that* → *HA-ving-SED-that* · *whereas* → *wear-AZ*
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Generally speaking, young people read less than in the past, mainly because they spend more time online. Having said that, audiobooks are becoming popular.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Respostas mais longas que na Part 1
Part 3 exige **desenvolvimento**: 3–5 frases, ideias conectadas, vocabulário mais abstrato.
### Modelo
> **Q:** *Why do you think fewer young people read books today?*
> **A:** *I'd say it's mainly because of the competition from smartphones and streaming. Young people have so many forms of entertainment that are faster and more interactive than a book. For example, a teenager can watch a whole series in one weekend, which gives instant satisfaction. Having said that, I don't think reading is disappearing — it's just changing. Many young people now read on screens or listen to audiobooks while commuting.*
> **Q:** *What makes a good teacher?*
> *From my point of view, the most important quality is the ability to motivate students. Knowledge is essential, of course, but a teacher who is passionate can make even a difficult subject interesting. Take my old history teacher, for instance — she used to bring objects to class and tell stories, and suddenly everyone wanted to learn. So I'd say enthusiasm and good communication matter more than anything else.*

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: comparação passado × presente
> **Compared to the past**, **people travel much more**, **mainly because** **flights are cheaper**.
> → families are smaller / women have more career opportunities → people move more often / jobs are less stable → children play outside less / cities are less safe and screens are more attractive
### 4.2 O-R-E rápido (30 s cada)
1. *Is it better to study alone or in groups?* 2. *Why do some people prefer to live in the countryside?* 3. *Should children have homework every day?* 4. *How has technology changed the way people shop?*
### 4.3 Vocabulário abstrato
Troque palavras simples por mais precisas na sua resposta: *good → beneficial · bad → harmful · big → significant · problem → issue · change → transformation · people → individuals/society*

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: 6 perguntas (15 min)
**Tema: Work and careers.** O professor faz as perguntas como examinador (30–45 s cada, com follow-ups curtos: *Why? Can you give an example?*).
1. What kinds of jobs are most popular with young people in your country?
2. Do you think it's better to have one job for life or to change jobs often?
3. How has technology changed the way people work?
4. Should people be paid according to how useful their job is to society?
5. Why do some people choose to work for themselves?
6. Do you think the four-day working week will become common in the future?
**Professor anota:** O-R-E completo ☐☐☐☐☐☐ · vocabulário abstrato __ · comparações __ · gramática (1–9) __

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**. Grave 3–4 respostas por dia (45 s cada).
**Dia 1 — Education.** *What makes a good school? Should university be free? Is online learning the future?*
**Dia 2 — Technology.** *Has technology made people less social? Should children have phones? Will robots replace workers?*
**Dia 3 — Environment.** *Who is responsible for protecting the environment? Why don't people recycle more?*
**Dia 4 — Cities.** *Why do people move to cities? What problems do big cities have?*
**Dia 5 — Escute.** Ouça e marque O-R-E. Regrave as duas piores.
**Dia 6 — Vocabulário.** Lista de 20 palavras abstratas (society, issue, impact, generation, sustainable…) com frases.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
