## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** a tarefa *Read in Daily Life* com **avisos, horários e instruções**: encontrar rápido datas, mudanças, regras e exceções.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** ler um aviso em 1 minuto e responder perguntas de detalhe, de regra e de inferência simples.
**Tarefa verificável:** responder seis perguntas sobre um aviso do campus.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- What kind of notices do you see at work or at school?
- Do you read instructions before using something new?
- Tell me about a time you missed information in a notice or email.
**Frases úteis:** *I didn't notice that…* · *The sign said…* · *It turned out that…*
**Revisão da aula 10:** explique o que significam os prefixos *un-, re-, mis-* com um exemplo cada.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
Palavras típicas de avisos:
- **effective (date)** — em vigor a partir de — *Effective March 1, the gym closes at 9.*
- **until further notice** — até novo aviso
- **temporarily** — temporariamente · **permanently** — permanentemente
- **closed for maintenance** — fechado para manutenção
- **eligible** — que tem direito — *Only full-time students are eligible.*
- **mandatory / optional** — obrigatório / opcional
- **in advance** — com antecedência · **no later than** — no máximo até
- **refund** — reembolso · **fee** — taxa · **free of charge** — gratuito
- **residents** — moradores · **visitors** — visitantes
- **except / excluding** — exceto · **including** — incluindo
- **sign up (for)** — inscrever-se · **cancel** — cancelar
**Pequenas palavras perigosas:** *only, all, except, unless, before, after, at least, up to*.

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **fee / free / fear** — fee /fiː/, free /friː/, fear /fɪər/
- **sign / sing / sin** — sign /saɪn/ (g mudo)
- **eligible** = E-li-gi-ble · **mandatory** = MAN-da-to-ry · **maintenance** = MAIN-te-nance
- **refund** (n) = RE-fund · **refund** (v) = re-FUND
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Only eligible students can sign up, and the fee is refundable.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Como ler um aviso em 3 passos
1. **Título e primeira linha** (10 s): sobre o quê é e para quem.
2. **Leia as perguntas** e sublinhe a palavra-chave de cada uma.
3. **Procure** (scanning) números, dias, horas e as palavras perigosas (*only, except, unless*). A resposta certa costuma ser uma **paráfrase** do aviso.
### Modelo
> **Fitness Center: Winter Break Hours**
> From December 20 to January 5, the Fitness Center will open from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. on weekdays and will be closed on weekends. The pool will be closed for maintenance during the entire break. Students staying on campus can use the gym free of charge; faculty and staff must pay a $10 weekly fee. Regular hours (6 a.m.–11 p.m.) resume on January 6.
**Raciocínio em voz alta:**
- *Can I swim on December 28?* → No: the pool is closed for the **entire break**.
- *Who pays?* → faculty and staff ($10/week). Students = free.
- *Inference: What can you do on Saturday, December 21?* → Nothing at the Fitness Center — it's **closed on weekends**.

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: respondendo com evidência
> According to the notice, **the pool is closed** because **it says “closed for maintenance during the entire break.”**
> → students don't pay / it says “free of charge” → regular hours start on January 6 / it says “resume on January 6” → the gym closes at 4 / it says “10 a.m. to 4 p.m.”

### 4.2 Paráfrase: ligue o aviso à resposta
1. “Walk-ins are not accepted.” 2. “Bring a valid photo ID.” 3. “Seating is limited.” 4. “Late submissions will not be graded.” 5. “The event has been postponed.”
a) It will happen later. b) You need an appointment. c) You must show identification. d) Not many people can attend. e) Work after the deadline gets zero/no grade.

### 4.3 Mini aviso 2 (2 min)
> **Shuttle Bus Update.** Due to road construction on Main Street, the campus shuttle will use Oak Avenue starting Monday. The stop in front of the Science Building will be temporarily moved to the parking lot behind it. Buses will run every 20 minutes instead of every 15. We apologize for the inconvenience.
- Why is the route changing? · Where is the new stop? · Will buses be more or less frequent?

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada (15 min)
### A. Leia e responda — 6 min (1 min de leitura + 5 min para as perguntas)
> **Study Abroad Information Session**
> The Office of Global Programs invites all second- and third-year students to an information session about next year's exchange programs. The session will take place on Wednesday, April 10, at 5 p.m. in Room 302 of the Humanities Building. Attendance is mandatory for students who plan to apply; students who cannot attend must watch the recorded session online and submit a short confirmation form no later than April 15. Representatives from partner universities in Canada, Japan, and Germany will answer questions. Light refreshments will be provided. Please note that first-year students are not eligible to apply this year but are welcome to attend.
1. Who is invited to the session?
2. What must applicants do if they cannot attend?
3. What is the deadline for the confirmation form?
4. Which countries will be represented?
5. Can a first-year student apply for an exchange program this year?
6. Can a first-year student go to the session?
**Acertos:** __/6

### B. Evidência oral — 5 min
Para cada resposta, diga em inglês **a frase do aviso** que prova a resposta: *“I know because it says…”*

### C. Reescreva o aviso — 4 min
Explique o aviso para um amigo em 4 frases simples (paráfrase), sem copiar as frases do texto.

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Vocabulário.** Escreva um mini aviso (4 frases) usando 6 palavras da aula.
**Dia 2 — Leitura real.** Leia a página de avisos (*news / announcements*) de uma universidade estrangeira. Escolha 2 avisos e escreva o quê/quando/quem/o que fazer.
**Dia 3 — Paráfrase.** Reescreva 6 frases de avisos com outras palavras (*mandatory → you must*).
**Dia 4 — Sombra.** Leia o aviso do Fitness Center em voz alta 3 vezes, como se fosse um anúncio. Grave a última.
**Dia 5 — Speaking.** Grave 1 minuto explicando o aviso do Study Abroad para um amigo.
**Dia 6 — Perguntas.** Crie 4 perguntas de múltipla escolha sobre o aviso do ônibus (com 1 resposta certa e 2 armadilhas).
