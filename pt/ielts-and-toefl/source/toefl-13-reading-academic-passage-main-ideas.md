## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
Palavras para falar sobre textos:
- **topic** — assunto · **main idea** — ideia principal · **purpose** — objetivo do autor
- **passage** — trecho/texto · **paragraph** — parágrafo
- **describe / explain / compare / contrast / argue / propose** — verbos do autor
- **cause / effect** — causa / efeito · **advantage / drawback** — vantagem / desvantagem
- **summary / summarize** — resumo / resumir
- **overall** — de modo geral · **in short** — em resumo
Palavras acadêmicas comuns em passagens:
- **species** — espécie · **evidence** — evidência · **theory** — teoria
- **process** — processo · **factor** — fator · **population** — população

### Pronúncia: verbos acadêmicos
Ouça, compare e repita (sílaba forte em maiúsculas):
- **des-CRIBE / ex-PLAIN / com-PARE / con-TRAST (v) / AR-gue / pro-POSE**
- **CON-trast (n) × con-TRAST (v)** — **SUM-ma-ry × SUM-ma-rize**
- **e-VI-dence**? Não! **EV-i-dence** · **the-O-ry**? Não! **THE-o-ry**
Frase: > The author compares two theories and explains the evidence for each.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Tópico × ideia principal
- **Tópico** (palavras): *urban heat islands*
- **Ideia principal** (frase completa): *Cities are hotter than rural areas because of buildings and asphalt, but trees and reflective roofs can reduce the effect.*
### Onde está a ideia principal?
- Geralmente na **1ª frase do parágrafo** (frase-tópico) ou no **último parágrafo**.
- Palavras como *however*, *in fact*, *therefore* muitas vezes introduzem a ideia mais importante.
### Organizações comuns
- **Problema → causa → solução** · **Comparação** (A × B) · **Processo** (etapas) · **Teoria → evidência → crítica**
### Modelo
> (1) Cities are often several degrees warmer than the surrounding countryside, a phenomenon known as the urban heat island effect. (2) This happens mainly because materials such as asphalt and concrete absorb heat during the day and release it slowly at night. (3) In addition, cities have fewer trees, which normally cool the air through shade and evaporation. (4) To reduce the effect, some cities are planting trees and painting roofs white so that they reflect sunlight instead of absorbing it.
**Tópico:** urban heat islands · **Organização:** problema → causas → soluções
**Resumo em 3 frases:** *Cities are warmer than rural areas. This is because hard materials store heat and there are few trees. Some cities are fighting the problem with trees and white roofs.*
O aluno lê o resumo junto com o professor (mesmo ritmo).

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)

> In paragraph 1, the author **describes a problem**.

> Many people believe that multitasking makes them more productive. However, research shows that the brain cannot focus on two complex tasks at the same time. Instead, it switches quickly between them, and each switch costs time and attention. As a result, people who multitask often make more mistakes and take longer to finish their work.

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada (15 min)
### A. Leia — 4 min
> **The Return of the Wolves**
> (1) In 1995, wolves were reintroduced to Yellowstone National Park in the United States after an absence of about seventy years. Their return produced changes that scientists had not fully expected.
> (2) Without wolves, the elk population had grown very large. The elk ate young trees along rivers, especially willows and aspens, preventing forests from recovering. When wolves returned, they reduced the number of elk and, perhaps more importantly, changed their behavior: elk avoided open valleys where they could be easily attacked.
> (3) As a result, some trees began to grow again near rivers. The new vegetation provided food and material for beavers, whose dams created ponds that benefited fish, birds and insects. Scientists call this chain of effects a “trophic cascade.”
> (4) However, not all researchers agree on how strong this effect was. Some argue that changes in climate and the presence of other predators, such as bears, also influenced the recovery. Even so, the Yellowstone case is often used to show how one species can affect an entire ecosystem.

### B. Responda oralmente — 4 min
1. What is the topic?
2. What is the main idea of paragraph 2? Of paragraph 3? Of paragraph 4?
3. How is the passage organized?
