## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** tarefas de **matching**: ligar itens (pessoas, lugares, opções) a categorias (opiniões, características). O desafio é acompanhar **quem disse o quê** e as **paráfrases** das categorias.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** ouvir várias opiniões e associar cada item à categoria certa, mesmo com sinônimos e mudanças de ideia.
**Tarefa verificável:** resolver exercício de matching sem confundir falantes.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Dê sua opinião em 1 frase usando palavras de avaliação:
- *your city's public transport · online shopping · your last holiday · the IELTS so far*
**Frases úteis:** *It's well organised · It's overrated · It's good value for money · It's a bit disappointing · It's surprisingly…*
**Revisão da aula 13:** cite 2 tipos de distrator de múltipla escolha.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Categorias típicas e suas paráfrases
- **good value** ↔ *worth the money · reasonably priced · you get a lot for what you pay*
- **too crowded** ↔ *packed · hard to find a seat · queues everywhere*
- **well organised** ↔ *everything ran smoothly · clearly planned*
- **disappointing** ↔ *not as good as I expected · let me down · overrated*
- **convenient** ↔ *easy to get to · right on the doorstep · saves time*
- **suitable for families** ↔ *great for kids · children loved it*
- **needs improvement** ↔ *could be better · there's room for improvement*
### Marcadores de falante
- *As for… · When it comes to… · Moving on to… · The second option… · My colleague thinks…, but I…*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **overrated** = o-ver-RAT-ed · **organised** = OR-ga-nised · **convenient** = con-VEE-ni-ent
- **queue / cue / Q** (iguais!) · **value / volume**
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> As for the museum, it was overrated, but the market was good value for money.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Estratégia
1. **Leia as categorias (A–F)** e pense em 1–2 paráfrases para cada.
2. **Itens vêm na ordem do áudio**; categorias, **não**.
3. Uma categoria pode **sobrar** (ou ser usada mais de uma vez — leia a instrução!).
4. **Espere a opinião final:** *“At first I thought it was crowded, but actually…”*
### Modelo
> **Categorias:** A good value · B too crowded · C well organised · D disappointing
> **Itens:** 1 the food festival · 2 the boat tour
🎧 > “The food festival was packed — honestly, you couldn't move. The boat tour, on the other hand, I expected a lot from it after all the reviews, but it was really nothing special.”
→ 1 **B** (*packed, couldn't move*) · 2 **D** (*expected a lot… nothing special*)

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: avaliações com paráfrase
> **The museum** was **overrated** — I mean, **it wasn't as good as I expected**.
> → the hotel / good value / we got a lot for what we paid → the concert / well organised / everything ran smoothly → the beach / too crowded / there was no space for our towels
### 4.2 Matching curto
**Categorias:** A convenient · B suitable for families · C needs improvement · D good value
🎧 > “Let me tell you about the three campsites we tried. Sunny Fields was cheap, and the facilities were great for the price. Lakeside was a bit expensive, but our kids absolutely loved the playground and the activities. And Pine Hill… well, the location was handy, right next to the station, but the showers were old and the staff weren't very helpful — they really need to sort that out.”
1 Sunny Fields ___ 2 Lakeside ___ 3 Pine Hill ___

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada (15 min)
**Instrução:** *What does each student say about the following modules? Choose FIVE answers from the list (A–G). Some letters will not be used.*
> **A** useful for future jobs · **B** too theoretical · **C** heavy workload · **D** inspiring lecturer · **E** poorly organised · **F** easier than expected · **G** good for meeting people
> **Modules:** 1 Statistics · 2 Urban History · 3 Marketing · 4 Environmental Law · 5 Creative Writing
🎧 Conversa (professor lê com duas vozes):
> **Lena:** So, which modules would you recommend for next year?
> **Omar:** Well, Statistics — everyone says it's terrifying, but honestly, I found it fine. Once you get the basics, it's quite manageable.
> **Lena:** Oh, good. What about Urban History?
> **Omar:** Professor Carter teaches it, and she's amazing. She made me want to become a historian! Every lecture felt like a story.
> **Lena:** And Marketing? My brother said it's useful for getting a job.
> **Omar:** Maybe, but it was a mess this year. Rooms changed every week, and half the time the slides weren't online.
> **Lena:** Oh no. And Environmental Law?
> **Omar:** Interesting topic, but the reading list is enormous. We had two essays and a presentation every month — I barely slept.
> **Lena:** Hmm. Creative Writing?
> **Omar:** I loved it. It's all workshops in small groups, so I made loads of friends there — some of my best friends now.
**Acertos:** __/5
**Justificativa (4 min):** para cada resposta, diga a paráfrase que ouviu. Por que **A** e **B** não foram usadas?
**Fala (3 min):** *Which subject did you enjoy most at school? Why?* (45 s, use 3 palavras de avaliação)
