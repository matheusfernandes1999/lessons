## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** **diagramas de processo** (como algo é feito ou como um ciclo natural funciona). Sem números! A pontuação vem de **sequência clara**, **voz passiva** e **conectores de etapa**.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** descrever um processo com overview (nº de etapas, início e fim) e todas as fases em ordem.
**Tarefa verificável:** produzir resposta com overview e sequência lógica.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Sequência
- *First / Firstly / To begin with / At the first stage* · *Next / Then / After that / Subsequently* · *Once… / After… / When…* · *At the same time / Meanwhile* · *Finally / At the final stage / Eventually*
### Voz passiva (processos fabricados)
- *is collected · are transported · is heated · are mixed · is poured · are packed · is delivered*
- Presente simples passivo: **is/are + particípio**
### Voz ativa (ciclos naturais)
- *The water evaporates · the larvae hatch · the salmon migrate*
### Vocabulário de processos
- **raw materials** · **stage / step / phase** · **transported / conveyed** · **heated / cooled** · **crushed / ground** · **filtered** · **stored** · **distributed** · **by-product** — subproduto · **cycle**

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita (particípios)
- **ground** (de grind) · **poured** /pɔːd/ · **heated** /ɪd/ · **packed** /t/ · **transported** /ɪd/ · **stored** /d/
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Once the beans are roasted, they are ground, packed and distributed to shops.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Estrutura de um processo
1. **Introdução:** paráfrase (*The diagram illustrates how… is produced / the stages in…*).
2. **Overview:** nº de etapas + início e fim (*Overall, there are eight stages, beginning with… and ending with…*).
3. **Body 1:** primeira metade das etapas. **Body 2:** segunda metade.
4. **Não pule etapas** e use os **termos do diagrama**.
### Modelo
> **Diagrama — Production of bricks:** 1 clay dug from the ground → 2 clay put through a sieve → 3 mixed with sand and water → 4 shaped in moulds → 5 dried for 24–48 hours → 6 heated in a kiln → 7 cooled → 8 packed and delivered
> **Overview:** *Overall, brick production involves eight stages, beginning with the extraction of clay and ending with the delivery of the finished bricks.*
> **Body 1:** *First, clay **is dug** from the ground and **put through** a sieve to remove large particles. **It is then mixed** with sand and water and **shaped** into bricks using moulds.*
> **Body 2:** *After that, the bricks **are left to dry** for 24 to 48 hours **before being heated** in a kiln. **Once they have cooled**, they **are packed** and **delivered** to customers.*

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: ativa → passiva
> Workers **collect** the fruit. → The fruit **is collected**.
> 1. Machines wash the fruit. 2. A machine cuts the fruit into pieces. 3. Workers add sugar. 4. They heat the mixture. 5. They pour the jam into jars. 6. Trucks deliver the jars.
### 4.2 Varie os conectores
Reescreva 4 frases curtas com conectores **diferentes** e uma estrutura com *before being / after being / once*:
> *The bricks are dried. The bricks are heated.* → *After being dried, the bricks are heated.*
### 4.3 Ciclo natural (ativa) — fale
> **Water cycle:** sun heats water → water evaporates → vapour rises and condenses into clouds → rain falls → water flows into rivers and the sea
