## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** múltipla escolha no Listening: **todas as opções costumam ser mencionadas**, mas só uma responde à pergunta. A certa vem quase sempre em **paráfrase**.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** eliminar distratores e explicar **por que** cada opção errada está errada.
**Tarefa verificável:** resolver oito itens justificando exclusão de opções.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
O professor lê; o aluno diz a paráfrase:
- *“It's not cheap.”* → *It's expensive.* · *“I'm not keen on it.”* → ______ · *“It was packed.”* → ______ · *“We can't afford it.”* → ______ · *“It's a bit of a trek.”* → ______
**Revisão da aula 12:** o que significa *NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER*?

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Os 4 distratores clássicos
1. **Mencionado, mas negado:** *“Some people think it's about cost, but actually it's about time.”*
2. **Mencionado, mas no passado/futuro:** *“We used to… / We might… later.”*
3. **Opinião de outra pessoa:** *“My tutor suggested X, but I prefer Y.”*
4. **Verdadeiro, mas não responde à pergunta:** fala de outra coisa.
### Paráfrases frequentes
- *expensive* ↔ *costly / not cheap / pricey* · *crowded* ↔ *packed / busy*
- *far* ↔ *a long way / a trek* · *difficult* ↔ *tricky / challenging*
- *main reason* ↔ *the key factor / what really matters*
- *disappointed* ↔ *let down / not what I expected*
### Palavras de contraste (sinal de que a resposta vem depois!)
**but · however · actually · in fact · although · the thing is · what surprised me was**

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- Ênfase de contraste: *It WASN'T the price, it was the LOCATION.*
- **actually** (AC-tchu-a-li) · **tricky / trick / treat**
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Some people think it's the price, but actually what really matters is the location.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Estratégia
1. **Leia a pergunta** e sublinhe a palavra-chave (*main reason, first, prefer, disappointed*).
2. **Parafraseie as opções** mentalmente.
3. **Ouça a ideia inteira**, não a primeira palavra igual.
4. **Elimine** com motivo: negada? de outra pessoa? passado? não responde?
### Modelo
> *Why did the student choose this university?* A) its reputation B) its location C) the cost
🎧 > “Everyone assumes I chose it for its reputation — and it is well known, of course. And it's not cheap, so it wasn't the price! The thing is, it's only twenty minutes from my parents' house, and that was what really decided it.”
- A ✘ mencionada, mas é o que “everyone assumes” · C ✘ negada (*not cheap… wasn't the price*) · **B ✔** (*twenty minutes from my parents' house* = location)

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: justificando
> It's not **A**, because **that's what other people think**. It's not **C**, because **she says it wasn't the price**. So the answer is **B**.
Pratique com: *it was in the past · he mentions it but rejects it · it doesn't answer the question*
### 4.2 Três itens
🎧 1. *What was the problem with the first hotel?* A) It was noisy. B) It was far from the centre. C) It was expensive.
> “The first hotel was actually quite reasonable, and it was right in the centre. But the street was full of bars, so we hardly slept at all.”
🎧 2. *What does the woman want to do next year?* A) travel B) study C) work
> “I thought about travelling for a while, but I've decided I really need to save money, so I'm going to get a full-time job — maybe I'll do a master's later.”
🎧 3. *What did the speaker find most surprising about the city?* A) the weather B) the food C) the people
> “I expected the rain — everyone warns you about that. The food was good, as I'd heard. What I didn't expect was how friendly everyone was — strangers would just start chatting to you.”

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: 8 itens (15 min)
🎧 **Professor:** leia a conversa uma vez. Dê 1 minuto para o aluno ler as perguntas antes.
**Contexto:** dois estudantes, Mia e Josh, falam sobre um projeto de pesquisa de campo.
1. *Why did they choose the river as their study site?* A) It's close to the university. B) Their tutor recommended it. C) It has a lot of wildlife.
2. *What was the main problem on the first visit?* A) bad weather B) broken equipment C) getting lost
3. *What does Josh think about the data so far?* A) It's very reliable. B) The sample is too small. C) It contradicts previous studies.
4. *What will they do next week?* A) visit the river again B) meet their tutor C) start writing the report
🎧
> **Mia:** So, how do you think the project's going?
> **Josh:** Not bad. I'm glad we went for the river. I know Dr. Lewis suggested the woodland, but the river's only ten minutes from campus by bike.
> **Mia:** True, and it's not like the woodland has more animals anyway.
> **Josh:** Exactly. The first visit was a bit of a disaster, though.
> **Mia:** Because of the rain?
> **Josh:** Well, the rain didn't help, but we'd have been fine if the water-testing kit hadn't stopped working after twenty minutes.
> **Mia:** Right. And we didn't get lost at all, thanks to my map!
> **Josh:** Ha, yes. As for the results, I'm a bit worried. They look consistent, but we've only got samples from three days. That's not enough to say anything for sure.
> **Mia:** Do they agree with what other studies found?
> **Josh:** More or less, yes. So, next week — should we go back to the river?
> **Mia:** I think we should see Dr. Lewis first and ask how many more samples we need. Then we can plan more visits.
> **Josh:** OK, I'll email her to set up a meeting. The report can wait till we've got more data.
**Acertos:** __/4
**Justificativa oral (5 min):** para cada item, explique por que as 2 opções erradas estão erradas.
**Mini fala (3 min):** *Describe a project you worked on with someone. What problems did you have?* (45 s)

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Paráfrases.** Crie 15 pares de paráfrase (*expensive = costly*). Teste-se.
**Dia 2 — Listening oficial.** Faça uma Part 3 oficial (múltipla escolha) e justifique cada opção errada por escrito.
**Dia 3 — Distratores.** Escreva 3 mini diálogos com os 4 tipos de distrator.
**Dia 4 — Sombra.** Leia o diálogo de Mia e Josh em voz alta (duas vozes). Grave.
**Dia 5 — Speaking.** Grave 1 minuto: *Describe a time something was different from what you expected.* Use *what I didn't expect was…*
**Dia 6 — Listening.** Ouça 5 min de um podcast; anote 5 vezes em que alguém disse *but/actually* e o que mudou.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
