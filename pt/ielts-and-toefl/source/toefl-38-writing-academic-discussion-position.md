## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** a tarefa *Write for an Academic Discussion*: um professor faz uma pergunta em um fórum online, dois colegas respondem, e **você contribui** com sua opinião (cerca de 100 palavras ou mais, em cerca de 10 minutos).
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** tomar uma posição clara, conectar-se com os colegas e desenvolver **um argumento principal**.
**Tarefa verificável:** produzir resposta focada com argumento principal.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Diga sua posição em 1 frase + 1 razão:
- Should university students be required to take a foreign language?
- Is it better to have one long vacation or several short ones?
- Should museums be free?
**Revisão da aula 37:** diga as partes de um e-mail de resolução de problema.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Posição
- *In my view,…* · *I believe that…* · *I'm convinced that…* · *I tend to agree with… that…*
### Conectar-se com os colegas (essencial!)
- *I agree with Maria that…, but I'd add that…*
- *Although Paulo makes a good point about…, I think…*
- *Unlike Paulo, I believe…*
- *Building on Maria's idea,…*
### Argumento principal
- *The main reason is that…* · *This matters because…* · *As a result,…*
### Exemplo
- *For example,…* · *In my country,…* · *A study I read showed…* · *When I…*
### Vocabulário de debate
- **perspective** · **argument** · **benefit / drawback** · **outweigh** — superar (em peso) · **overlook** — não levar em conta · **crucial** · **contribute**

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **argument** = AR-gu-ment · **perspective** = per-SPEC-tive · **crucial** = CRU-cial · **outweigh** = out-WEIGH
- **view / few / vie** · **weigh / way** (iguais!)
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> I agree with Maria that the benefits outweigh the drawbacks, but I'd add one crucial point.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Estrutura em 4 movimentos (≈ 110–140 palavras)
1. **Posição** (1 frase) — responda a pergunta do professor.
2. **Conexão** (1 frase) — mencione um colega (concordando ou discordando).
3. **Argumento principal + explicação** (2–3 frases) — algo **novo**, não a mesma ideia do colega.
4. **Exemplo** (1–2 frases) + frase de fechamento opcional.
### Modelo
> **Professor Reyes:** *Many universities require students to take courses outside their major. Should these general education requirements be kept, or should students focus only on their main area? Why?*
> **Maria:** *I think they should be kept. Courses in other areas help students think in different ways.*
> **Paulo:** *I disagree. University is expensive, and students should spend their time and money on courses that prepare them for their careers.*
> **Resposta modelo:**
> In my view, general education requirements should be kept. I understand Paulo's concern about cost, but I think he overlooks how much employers value broad skills. Today, most jobs require people to communicate clearly and work with colleagues from other fields, and courses outside your major develop exactly these abilities. For example, my cousin studied engineering, and she says that a required public speaking course was more useful in her first job than some technical classes. So, rather than being a waste of money, these courses are an investment in students' future careers.
(≈ 105 palavras) O aluno lê em voz alta e marca os 4 movimentos.

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: conexão com colegas
> I agree with **Maria** that **these courses broaden our thinking**, but I'd add that **they also help us find new interests**.
> → Paulo / university is expensive / students could take fewer but better courses
> Although **Paulo** makes a good point about **cost**, I believe **the long-term benefits are greater**.
> → Maria / creativity / students need more practical skills

### 4.2 Argumento novo × repetição
Para cada post de colega, crie oralmente um argumento **diferente** que apoie a mesma posição:
1. Maria: *General education helps students think in different ways.* → Novo argumento a favor: ______
2. Paulo: *Students should save money and time.* → Novo argumento contra: ______

### 4.3 Posições rápidas (1 frase de posição + 1 de conexão)
Tema: *Should first-year students be required to live on campus?*
- Colega A (a favor): *It helps them make friends.*
- Colega B (contra): *It's too expensive for many families.*
→ Sua posição + conexão: ______

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Frases de conexão.** Escreva 6 frases conectando-se a colegas (concordar, discordar, acrescentar).
**Dia 2 — Banco de argumentos.** Para 4 temas (carros no centro, uniforme escolar, aulas online, trabalho remoto), escreva 2 argumentos a favor e 2 contra.
**Dia 3 — Escrita 1.** *Should universities require attendance in classes?* (Crie 2 posts de colegas e responda.) 10 min.
**Dia 4 — Speaking.** Grave 45 s respondendo oralmente à pergunta do Professor Adams.
**Dia 5 — Escrita 2.** *Is it better for students to work part-time while studying?* 10 min.
**Dia 6 — Revisão.** Revise as respostas dos dias 3 e 5 com o checklist (seção 6). Conte palavras.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.

## Gabarito do professor
- **4.2 (exemplos):** 1 a favor: *They help students choose a better major if they discover a new interest.* 2 contra: *Students could take these courses online for free if they want.*
- **5 (exemplo):** *I partly agree with Kenji, but I think a full ban is too extreme. Laura is right that many people depend on cars, but there is another problem: small shops in the center may lose customers who come from far away. A better solution would be to charge drivers a fee to enter the center and use the money to improve buses. For example, London introduced a congestion charge, and traffic in the center fell while businesses continued to operate. In this way, cities can reduce pollution without excluding anyone.*
