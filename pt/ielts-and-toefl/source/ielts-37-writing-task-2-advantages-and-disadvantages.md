## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** ensaios de **vantagens e desvantagens** — especialmente *Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?*, que exige **pesar** e **decidir**. E evitar **generalizações** (*everyone, always, never*).
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** fazer um plano equilibrado e escrever um parágrafo que compara vantagens e desvantagens com linguagem cautelosa.
**Tarefa verificável:** produzir plano e parágrafo comparativo.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Uma vantagem e uma desvantagem de:
- *living abroad · online shopping · having a car in a big city · working while studying*
**Troque e fale:**
> **One clear advantage of** **living abroad** is that **you learn a new language quickly**. **However, a significant drawback is** **feeling homesick**.
> → online shopping / it saves time / you can't try products on → a car / freedom to travel / high costs and traffic
**Revisão da aula 36:** dê uma solução específica para o desperdício de comida.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Vantagens
- *advantage · benefit · merit · upside · a major advantage is… · … is beneficial because… · one of the main benefits of… is…*
### Desvantagens
- *disadvantage · drawback · downside · a major drawback is… · … can be detrimental to… (prejudicial)*
### Pesar e decidir
- *the advantages outweigh the disadvantages · the benefits are greater than the costs · on balance, … · overall, … · the drawbacks are relatively minor compared with…*
### Evitar generalizações (hedging)
- *everyone* → *many people* · *always* → *often / in many cases* · *will* → *may / can / is likely to* · *all young people* → *a large number of young people*
- *tend to · it could be argued that · to some extent*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **detrimental** = de-tri-MEN-tal · **downside** = DOWN-side · **on balance** = on-BAL-ance · **likely** = LIKE-ly
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> On balance, the benefits clearly outweigh the drawbacks, although some people may find it difficult at first.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Dois tipos de enunciado
- *Discuss the advantages and disadvantages.* → apresente os dois (opinião é opcional, mas uma conclusão equilibrada ajuda).
- *Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?* → **decida** e diga na introdução: *I believe the advantages clearly outweigh…*
### Estrutura (outweigh)
1. **Intro:** tema + decisão.
2. **Body 1:** o lado **mais fraco** (com concessão): *Admittedly, there are some drawbacks…*
3. **Body 2:** o lado **mais forte** (sua posição), com mais desenvolvimento.
4. **Conclusão:** *On balance, …*
### Modelo — parágrafo comparativo
> **Enunciado:** *More and more people are choosing to work from home. Do the advantages of this outweigh the disadvantages?*
> *Admittedly, working from home has some drawbacks. Employees **may** feel isolated without daily contact with colleagues, and **some** find it difficult to separate their work and personal lives. **However**, these problems are **relatively minor compared with** the benefits. **Most importantly**, remote workers save the time and money they would spend commuting, which **can** reduce stress and give them more time for their families. **In many cases**, they **also tend to** be more productive because they face fewer interruptions. **On balance, therefore**, the advantages **clearly outweigh** the disadvantages.*

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: hedging
> **Everyone** prefers online shopping. → **Many people** prefer online shopping.
> 1. Young people never read books. 2. Tourism always damages the environment. 3. Online courses will replace universities. 4. All workers are more productive at home.
### 4.2 Plano “outweigh” (oral)
*Some families choose to have their children educated at home rather than at school. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?*
→ Decisão · Body 1 (lado mais fraco + concessão) · Body 2 (lado mais forte) · conclusão
### 4.3 Vantagem → explicação → exemplo
1. *Public transport is cheaper than driving.* 2. *Learning online is flexible.* 3. *Living in a small town is safer.*
