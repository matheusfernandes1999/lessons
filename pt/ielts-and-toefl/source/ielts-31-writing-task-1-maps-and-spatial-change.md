## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** **mapas** na Task 1: geralmente dois mapas do **mesmo lugar em épocas diferentes** (ou um plano futuro). Você descreve **o que mudou, o que foi construído/removido** e **onde**.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** comparar dois mapas usando linguagem de posição e verbos de transformação (na passiva).
**Tarefa verificável:** comparar dois mapas usando posição e mudança.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- How has your neighbourhood changed in the last 10 years?
- What was built? What was demolished? What stayed the same?
**Troque e fale:**
> **A shopping centre** **was built** **where the old factory used to be**.
> → a park / was created / in the north of the town → the school / was extended / to the east → the cinema / was demolished / replaced by flats
**Revisão da aula 30:** descreva 3 etapas de um processo na passiva.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Verbos de mudança (passiva!)
- **construir:** *was built / was constructed / was erected* · **criar:** *was created / was developed*
- **remover:** *was demolished / was knocked down / was removed / was cleared*
- **mudar:** *was converted into / was replaced by / was turned into / was relocated to*
- **ampliar/reduzir:** *was extended / was expanded / was widened / was reduced*
- **sem mudança:** *remained unchanged / stayed the same*
- **paisagem:** *trees were cut down · a forest was cleared · farmland was replaced by housing*
### Posição
- *in the north/south-east of · in the centre · on the outskirts (periferia) · next to · opposite · alongside (ao longo de) · to the west of · across the river · near the coast*
### Overview de mapas
- *Overall, the town became more urbanised / residential / developed, with… · The most significant change was…*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **demolished** = de-MOL-ished · **converted** = con-VERT-ed · **outskirts** = OUT-skirts · **alongside** = a-long-SIDE
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> The old factory was demolished and replaced by flats, while the park remained unchanged.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Estrutura para dois mapas
1. **Introdução:** *The maps illustrate the changes that took place in… between… and…*
2. **Overview:** maior transformação + caráter geral (*more residential, less green*).
3. **Body 1:** mudanças em uma área (ex.: norte) **ou** construções novas.
4. **Body 2:** mudanças em outra área **ou** o que foi removido/mantido.
### Tempo verbal
- Passado: *was built* (mapas antigos → recentes) · Futuro: *will be built / is planned to be* (planos)
### Modelo
> **Mapas — Riverside village, 1990 e 2020:**
> 1990: farmland north of the river · small school in the centre · forest in the east · one bridge
> 2020: farmland → housing estate · school extended · forest partly cleared for a car park · second bridge to the west · sports centre next to school
> **Overview:** *Overall, the village became considerably more developed, with farmland and forest giving way to housing and new facilities.*
> **Body:** *The most dramatic change took place north of the river, where the farmland **was replaced by** a large housing estate. To link this area to the centre, a second bridge **was built** to the west of the original one…*

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: transformações
> The **farmland** **was replaced by** **a housing estate**.
> → the car park / was converted into / a park → the forest / was cleared to make way for / a road → the library / was relocated to / the town centre → the main road / was widened / to four lanes
### 4.2 Descreva a localização (oral)
Use o mapa do modelo: *Where is the sports centre? Where was the forest? Where is the new bridge?*
### 4.3 Mapa de plano futuro (futuro passivo)
> **Plano para 2030 — Hilltown:** old hospital → museum · empty land south → new hospital · pedestrian zone in the centre · bike lanes along the river
Fale 4 frases: *The old hospital **will be converted** into…*

:::quiz practice
? w|grammar|The farmland was ______ by a housing estate. (substituída)
> replaced
= The farmland was replaced by a housing estate.
? w|grammar|A second bridge was ______ to the west. (build → particípio)
> built
= A second bridge was built to the west.
? mc|grammar|For a plan for 2030, which is correct?
A) A new hospital was built. || Passado: errado para um plano.
*B) A new hospital will be built. || Correto.
? mc|grammar|Which is correct?
*A) The forest was partly cleared to make way for a car park. || Correto.
B) The forest cleared partly for car park. || Falta passiva e artigo.
? w|grammar|The old hospital will be ______ into a museum. (convertido)
> converted
= The old hospital will be converted into a museum.
? mc|comprehension|A good overview for the village maps is…
*A) The village became much more developed, with farmland and forest giving way to housing and facilities. || Correto.
B) A second bridge was built to the west in 2020. || Detalhe.
:::

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: dois mapas (15 min)
> **Maps — Westbay seafront, 2000 and 2024**
> **2000:** beach along the south · fishing harbour in the west · small car park in the centre · row of fishermen's houses in the east · sand dunes between the beach and the road · road running east–west
> **2024:** beach unchanged · fishing harbour → marina with yachts · car park → public square with cafés · fishermen's houses → hotel · sand dunes partly removed; boardwalk built along the beach · new car park north of the road · road unchanged
**Escreva (11 min):** introdução + overview + 2 parágrafos (150–180 palavras). Use pelo menos 6 verbos de mudança diferentes e 5 expressões de posição.
> ______
**Revisão (4 min):** confira passiva, preposições de lugar e se mencionou o que **não mudou**.

## Gabarito do professor
- **4.3:** The old hospital will be converted into a museum. A new hospital will be built on the empty land to the south. The centre will be turned into a pedestrian zone. Bike lanes will be created along the river.
- **5 (overview exemplo):** *Overall, the seafront was transformed from a small fishing area into a leisure destination, with most traditional features replaced by tourist facilities, although the beach and main road remained unchanged.*
