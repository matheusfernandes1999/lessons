## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** juntar informações de um **texto** e de uma **fala** sobre o mesmo tema acadêmico, mostrar como se relacionam (concordam? contradizem? complementam?) e **não inventar** o que não foi dito.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** fazer uma síntese oral de 1 minuto e escrever uma resposta de discussão usando as duas fontes.
**Tarefa verificável:** apresentar síntese oral e resposta escrita fundamentadas.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- Do you drink coffee or tea? How much per day?
- Have you heard that coffee is good or bad for your health? Where did you hear it?
- How do you know if health information is reliable?
**Frases úteis:** *I read that… · I heard that… · It's not clear whether… · The evidence suggests…*
**Revisão da aula 43:** diga 3 serviços de apoio acadêmico de uma universidade.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Relacionar fontes
- **Concordar:** *The lecture supports / confirms the reading.* · *Both sources agree that…*
- **Contradizer:** *The lecture challenges / casts doubt on the reading.* · *While the reading claims…, the speaker argues…*
- **Complementar:** *The lecture adds that… · The speaker gives an example of…*
### Não extrapolar (linguagem cautelosa)
- *may / might / could* · *suggests* (≠ *proves*) · *is linked to* (≠ *causes*) · *some studies* (≠ *all scientists*)
- **correlation vs. causation:** *A is linked to B* ≠ *A causes B*
### Vocabulário do tema
- **moderate** — moderado · **consumption** — consumo · **risk** · **heart disease** · **caffeine** · **sleep quality** · **participants** · **observational study** — estudo observacional · **controlled experiment**

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **caffeine** = caf-FEINE · **moderate** (adj) = MOD-er-ate · **consumption** = con-SUMP-tion
- **cause / course / cost** · **risk / wrist / rich**
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Moderate coffee consumption may be linked to lower risk, but that doesn't prove it causes it.

### Regra de ouro
Diga **apenas** o que as fontes dizem. Se quiser dar opinião, deixe claro: *In my view,…*
### Modelo de síntese oral (fale junto)
> The reading says that moderate coffee consumption is linked to a lower risk of heart disease. The lecture partly agrees, but it adds an important limitation: most of these studies are observational, so they show a connection, not a cause. The speaker also mentions a negative effect that the reading doesn't discuss — caffeine late in the day can reduce sleep quality.

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### Leitura (3 min)
> **Coffee and Health: A Changing View**
> For decades, coffee was considered unhealthy. Today, however, many large studies have found that people who drink three to four cups of coffee per day have a lower risk of heart disease and type 2 diabetes than people who drink none. Researchers believe that coffee contains antioxidants that may protect the body's cells. As a result, some nutrition experts now describe moderate coffee consumption as part of a healthy diet.
### Fala (🎧 professor lê uma vez, 2 min)
> So, you've read that coffee drinkers seem to be healthier. That's true in the data, but we need to be careful. Most of those studies are observational — researchers ask people how much coffee they drink and then follow their health. The problem is that coffee drinkers may be different in other ways. For example, people who are already sick often stop drinking coffee, which makes the non-drinkers look less healthy. Also, there's one effect everyone agrees on: caffeine stays in your body for several hours. A cup at 4 p.m. can still reduce sleep quality at night. So, coffee in the morning: probably fine. Coffee at night: think twice.
### 4.1 Preencha a tabela (oral com o professor)
- Ideia 1 (benefícios): leitura ______ · fala ______ · relação ______
- Ideia 2 (tipo de estudo): leitura ______ · fala ______ · relação ______
- Ideia 3 (sono): leitura ______ · fala ______ · relação ______
### 4.2 Troque e fale: relacionando
> The lecture **challenges** the reading by **explaining that most studies are observational**.
> → adds to / mentioning the effect on sleep → partly agrees with / accepting that the data show a link → gives an example / sick people stop drinking coffee

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada (15 min)
### A. Síntese oral — 4 min (1 min de preparo + 60–75 s de fala, duas tentativas)
*Summarize the reading and explain how the lecture relates to it.*
Use pelo menos: *The reading says… · The lecture partly agrees / challenges… · The speaker adds… · For example…*
### B. Resposta escrita — 9 min (110–150 palavras)
> **Professor Okafor:** *Based on our reading and lecture about coffee, should universities warn students about caffeine, for example in cafeterias? Why or why not?*
> **Jade:** *Yes. Many students drink energy drinks and coffee at night before exams, and their sleep suffers.*
> **Tom:** *No. The reading shows coffee can be healthy, so warnings would only confuse people.*
> ______
**Regra:** use **pelo menos 1 informação de cada fonte** e não invente dados.
### C. Checagem — 2 min
Sublinhe no seu texto o que veio da leitura (L) e da fala (F). Alguma frase extrapola?
