## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** anotar **pouco e bem** — só palavras-chave, números e relações (causa, contraste, exemplo), usando abreviações e símbolos. Quem tenta escrever tudo perde a próxima frase.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** ouvir 2 minutos e reconstruir a fala com no máximo 8 linhas de notas.
**Tarefa verificável:** resumir uma fala de dois minutos com até oito notas.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- Do you take notes in meetings or classes? On paper or on your phone?
- What do you write: full sentences or keywords?
- Look at your notes from a past class: can you understand them now?
**Teste rápido:** o professor lê 2 frases; o aluno anota em **5 palavras no máximo** e depois reconstrói a frase falando.
> 1. “The population of the city doubled between 1950 and 1980 because of migration from rural areas.”
> 2. “Although solar energy is cleaner, it is still more expensive than coal in many countries.”
**Revisão da aula 22:** dê um exemplo de frase de ressalva (*admittedly, that said…*).

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Símbolos úteis
- **→** causa/leva a · **←** vem de · **↑ / ↓** aumenta / diminui · **=** igual/é · **≠** diferente
- **+** e/mais · **vs** contra/comparado · **∴** portanto · **b/c** porque · **w/** com · **w/o** sem
- **e.g.** exemplo · **i.e.** isto é · **≈** aproximadamente · **#** número · **?** dúvida/hipótese
### Abreviações
- **govt** government · **pop** population · **info** information · **env** environment
- **dev** development · **prob** problem/probably · **imp** important · **ppl** people
- **yr** year · **c.** century (ex.: 19c.) · **sci** scientist · **res** research
- Corte o final: *temperature → temp*, *economy → econ*, *history → hist*

### Pronúncia: números e datas na fala
Ouça, compare e repita:
- *nineteen fifty* (1950) · *two thousand and five* / *two thousand five* (2005) · *the 1980s* = *the nineteen eighties*
- *a third* (1/3) · *half* · *a quarter* · *twenty percent*
- *double / triple* · *a tenth / a hundredth*
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Between nineteen fifty and nineteen eighty, the population almost tripled.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### O que anotar × o que ignorar
**Anote:** tópico, palavras repetidas pelo professor, números, nomes, termos definidos, relações (causa, contraste), conclusões.
**Ignore:** exemplos longos (anote só 1 palavra), histórias pessoais, repetições, palavras de função (*the, is, of*).
### Formato
Uma ideia por linha. Recuo para detalhes. Máximo **8 linhas** para 2 minutos.
### Modelo
🎧 > Today we're looking at the decline of honeybee populations. Since about 2006, beekeepers in the US have reported losing around 30% of their colonies every winter — that's roughly double the historical average. Scientists think there are three main causes. First, pesticides, especially a group called neonicotinoids, which affect the bees' ability to navigate. Second, parasites like the varroa mite. And third, the loss of wildflowers because of large single-crop farms. This matters because bees pollinate about a third of the food we eat.
**Notas:**
> Honeybee ↓ (since 2006)
> US: lose ≈30% colonies/winter = 2x avg
> 3 causes:
>   1 pesticides (neonic.) → navigation prob
>   2 parasites (varroa mite)
>   3 ↓ wildflowers b/c single-crop farms
> Imp: bees pollinate ≈1/3 food
O aluno reconstrói a fala **oralmente** só olhando as notas.

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: lendo notas em voz alta
O aluno transforma notas em frases completas:
> pop ↑ b/c migr → *The population increased because of migration.*
> 1. solar ≠ cheap; coal = cheaper
> 2. temp ↑ 1.2° since 1900
> 3. e.g. Japan: ppl > 100 yrs ↑
> 4. res: sleep → memory ↑
> 5. govt tax ↑ → smoking ↓ 20%

### 4.2 Ditado de notas (o professor lê; aluno anota em no máx. 6 palavras cada)
🎧 1. “The Industrial Revolution started in Britain in the late eighteenth century, mainly because of coal and new machines.”
🎧 2. “Unlike frogs, toads have dry skin and can live farther from water.”
🎧 3. “About seventy percent of the Earth's surface is covered by water, but only around three percent of that is fresh water.”
🎧 4. “The main reason for the change was cost; the new material was half the price.”

### 4.3 Compare as notas
O professor mostra notas “ruins” (frases completas copiadas) × “boas”. O aluno diz qual é melhor e por quê.

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada (15 min)
🎧 O professor lê a fala **uma vez** (≈2 min). O aluno anota em **no máximo 8 linhas**.
> Today I want to talk about the four-day work week. In the last few years, several countries have tested it — Iceland, the UK, Spain and Japan, among others. The idea is simple: employees work four days instead of five, usually for the same pay.
> So, what were the results? In the UK, a trial in 2022 involved about 60 companies and nearly 3,000 workers. At the end of six months, most companies decided to continue with the four-day week. Workers reported less stress and better sleep, and the number of sick days fell by about two thirds. Interestingly, company revenue stayed roughly the same — in some cases it even went up slightly.
> Why would productivity not fall? One explanation is that people waste less time. With fewer hours, meetings become shorter, and employees focus on what really matters. Another factor is motivation: workers who feel trusted tend to work harder.
> But there are limitations. The model works more easily in offices than in hospitals or factories, where someone always needs to be present. Also, most companies in the trials volunteered, so they were probably already open to change.
> So, the four-day week looks promising, but it's not a one-size-fits-all solution.
**Minhas notas (máx. 8 linhas):**
> 1 ______
> 2 ______
> 3 ______
> 4 ______
> 5 ______
> 6 ______
> 7 ______
> 8 ______
**Resumo oral (2 min):** só com as notas, explique a fala em 1 minuto.
**Perguntas (oral):** 1. How many workers were in the UK trial? 2. What happened to sick days? 3. Give two explanations for stable productivity. 4. Give two limitations.

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Símbolos.** Reescreva 8 frases de uma notícia em formato de notas (máx. 6 palavras cada).
**Dia 2 — Palestra.** Assista a um TED-Ed de 4 min e faça notas em 8 linhas.
**Dia 3 — Reconstrução.** No dia seguinte, sem rever o vídeo, grave 1 minuto explicando o conteúdo só pelas notas.
**Dia 4 — Números.** Ouça um podcast de notícias (5 min) e anote só os números e o que eles significam.
**Dia 5 — Sombra.** Leia a fala da semana de 4 dias em voz alta, gravando o 2º parágrafo.
**Dia 6 — Writing.** Transforme suas notas da tarefa cronometrada em um parágrafo de 80–100 palavras.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
