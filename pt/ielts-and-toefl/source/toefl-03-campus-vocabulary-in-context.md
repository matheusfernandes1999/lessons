## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** entender e usar o vocabulário de matrícula, biblioteca e serviços da universidade — ele aparece muito em *Read in Daily Life*, *Listen to a Conversation* e *Listen to an Announcement*.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** explicar com suas palavras 5 situações comuns em um campus (problema + o que fazer).
**Tarefa verificável:** explicar cinco situações comuns em um campus.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- Did you study at a university? What was the campus like?
- What services does a university usually have?
- Have you ever had a problem with registration, a document or a deadline? What happened?
**Frases úteis:**
- *My university had* a big library and a cafeteria.
- *I had a problem with* my registration.
- *I had to* go to the office and fill out a form.
**Revisão da aula 2:** fale 3 frases completas sobre sua rotina, uma com *There is/There are*.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Matrícula (registration)
- **enroll / register (for)** — matricular-se — *I need to register for two more courses.*
- **registrar's office** — secretaria acadêmica — *Go to the registrar's office to get your transcript.*
- **transcript** — histórico escolar — *The university asked for my transcript.*
- **add / drop a course** — incluir / trancar disciplina — *You can drop a course until week three.*
- **tuition (fees)** — mensalidade/taxas — *Tuition is paid every semester.*
- **financial aid** — auxílio financeiro — *She applied for financial aid.*
- **advisor** — orientador acadêmico — *Talk to your advisor before choosing courses.*
### Biblioteca e serviços
- **check out / borrow** — pegar emprestado — *You can check out five books.*
- **return** — devolver — *Return the books by Friday.*
- **overdue** — atrasado (devolução) — *This book is overdue, so you have to pay a fine.*
- **fine** — multa — *The fine is one dollar per day.*
- **reserve** — reservar — *Reserve a study room online.*
- **dorm (residence hall)** — alojamento estudantil — *I live in a dorm with two roommates.*
- **meal plan** — plano de refeições — *My meal plan includes lunch and dinner.*
- **student ID** — carteirinha — *Show your student ID at the gym.*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **fine / find / fight** — fine termina em /n/, find em /nd/.
- **dorm / door / dome** — o “r” de dorm é suave, sem som de “h”.
- **fee / fill / feel** — fee é longo /iː/, fill é curto /ɪ/, feel tem /iːl/.
- **due / do / dew** — *due* e *dew* soam iguais em inglês americano: /duː/.
- **advisor** = ad-VI-sor (tônica no meio) — **register** = RE-gis-ter (tônica no começo)
Frase para repetir 3 vezes:
> Return your overdue books or you will have to pay a fine.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Como explicar uma situação do campus
Use sempre 3 partes: **situação → problema → solução/próximo passo**.
- **Situação:** *A student wants to take a biology course.*
- **Problema:** *but the course is full.*
- **Solução:** *so she should talk to her advisor or join the waitlist.*
Conectores simples: **but** (mas), **so** (então), **because** (porque), **should** (deveria).

### Modelo: aviso da biblioteca
Leia junto e depois fale junto com o professor.
> **Library Notice — Fall Semester**
> Students can check out up to five books for three weeks. If you need a book for longer, you can renew it online once. Overdue books have a fine of 50 cents per day. Study rooms must be reserved at least one day in advance, and you need your student ID to enter after 8 p.m.
**Perguntas (oral):**
1. How many books can students check out?
2. How can you keep a book for more time?
3. What happens if a book is overdue?
4. When do you need your student ID?

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale
> I need to **register for a course**.
> → return two books → pay my tuition → talk to my advisor → reserve a study room → get my transcript
> If you **return the book late**, you **pay a fine**.
> → lose your student ID / need to get a new one → miss the deadline / can't register → don't have a meal plan / pay for every meal

### 4.2 Combine a situação com a solução
1. Maria lost her student ID.
2. Tom's course is full.
3. Ana can't pay tuition this semester.
4. Leo needs a quiet place to study with his group.
5. Sara wants to keep a library book for two more weeks.
a) She should renew it online.
b) He should reserve a study room.
c) She should go to the student services office and request a new one.
d) She should apply for financial aid.
e) He should join the waitlist or talk to his advisor.

### 4.3 Mini diálogo — leia em dupla (professor e aluno), depois troquem os papéis
> **Student:** Hi, I'd like to drop my chemistry course.
> **Officer:** Sure. Do you have the form signed by your advisor?
> **Student:** Not yet. Do I really need the signature?
> **Officer:** Yes, and the deadline is this Friday.
> **Student:** OK, I'll talk to my advisor today. Thanks!
Agora faça de novo trocando: *chemistry → statistics*, *this Friday → tomorrow*, *advisor → department coordinator*.

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada (15 min)
### A. Explique 5 situações — 8 min (cerca de 1 min e meio cada)
Para cada cartão, fale em 3 partes: **situação → problema → solução**. O professor cronometra.
1. A new student wants to live on campus, but all dorms are full.
2. A student has three overdue books and doesn't have money for the fine.
3. A student wants to change her major and doesn't know the process.
4. A student's tuition payment didn't go through and registration closes tomorrow.
5. An international student needs to send his transcript to another university.
**Professor anota:** 3 partes presentes? (✓/✗) 1 __ 2 __ 3 __ 4 __ 5 __

### B. Escuta rápida — 4 min
O professor lê o anúncio **uma vez**. O aluno anota apenas: **o quê, quando, o que fazer**.
> Attention, students. The registrar's office will be closed this Thursday because of a system update. If you need to add or drop a course, please do it online before Wednesday at 5 p.m. For transcript requests, the office will open again on Friday morning.
- O quê: ______
- Quando: ______
- O que fazer: ______

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Vocabulário.** Separe as 15 palavras em 2 grupos (matrícula / biblioteca e serviços). Escreva uma frase para cada.
**Dia 2 — Sombra.** Leia o aviso da biblioteca 3 vezes em voz alta, a última gravando. Atenção a *overdue*, *renew*, *reserved*.
**Dia 3 — Leitura real.** Entre no site de uma universidade americana ou canadense e leia a página da biblioteca (*Library hours / Borrowing*). Anote 5 palavras novas.
**Dia 4 — Speaking.** Grave 1 minuto explicando a situação 2 ou 4 da tarefa cronometrada, sem ler.
**Dia 5 — Diálogo.** Escreva um diálogo de 6 falas entre um aluno e a secretaria sobre *adding a course*. Leia em voz alta fazendo as duas vozes.
**Dia 6 — Writing.** Escreva 5 frases com *If you…, you…* sobre regras do campus (*If you lose your ID, you…*).
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
