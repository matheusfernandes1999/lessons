## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** a linguagem de **tendências** para gráficos de linha (e tabelas com anos): subir, cair, estabilizar, oscilar, atingir pico — com **verbos, substantivos e advérbios variados** e números precisos.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** escrever dois parágrafos descrevendo mudanças ao longo do tempo, **sem inventar causas**.
**Tarefa verificável:** redigir dois parágrafos de tendências sem inventar causas.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Descreva com gestos e palavras (o professor desenha a linha no ar ou no papel):
- ↗ (subida forte) · ↘ (queda leve) · → (estável) · ↗↘↗↘ (oscilação) · ↗ ⛰ ↘ (pico e queda)
**Troque e fale:**
> The price of coffee **rose sharply** between 2010 and 2015.
> → fell slightly / 2015 and 2018 → remained stable / 2018 and 2020 → peaked at $5 / in 2021
**Revisão da aula 27:** o que um bom overview deve conter?

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Verbos (passado para dados passados)
- **Subir:** *rise (rose), increase, grow (grew), climb, go up* · **Cair:** *fall (fell), decrease, decline, drop, go down*
- **Estável:** *remain stable/steady, stay the same, level off (estabilizar após mudança)*
- **Variar:** *fluctuate* · **Pico:** *peak at, reach a peak of* · **Mínimo:** *hit a low of, fall to a low of*
### Substantivos (estrutura alternativa)
- *a rise / an increase / growth / a fall / a decrease / a decline / a drop / a fluctuation / a peak*
- *There was a sharp rise in… · …saw a slight decline*
### Advérbios / adjetivos de intensidade
- **Grande:** *sharply/sharp, dramatically/dramatic, significantly/significant, considerably*
- **Pequeno:** *slightly/slight, marginally, gradually/gradual (lento e constante), steadily/steady*
### Preposições com números (atenção!)
- **rose to 50%** (chegou a) · **rose by 10%** (aumentou em) · **from 40% to 50%** · **stood at 40%** (estava em) · **peaked at 60%**

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **rose / rows / rise** · **fell / fall / fill** · **fluctuate** = FLUC-tchu-ate · **gradually** = GRAD-ju-al-ly
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Sales rose sharply to a peak of 80 units, then fell gradually and levelled off at 50.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Regras de ouro
1. **Agrupe**: não descreva ano por ano. Descreva **fases** (*between 2000 and 2010…*).
2. **Varie a estrutura**: verbo + advérbio (*rose sharply*) ↔ *there was* + adjetivo + substantivo (*there was a sharp rise*).
3. **Números sempre com unidade e ano.**
4. **Nunca invente causas** (*because of the crisis* ✘).
### Modelo — gráfico
> **Line graph — Visitors to a national park (thousands), 2000–2020:** 2000: 120 · 2005: 180 · 2010: 250 · 2015: 240 · 2020: 245
> **Parágrafo:** *In 2000, the park received 120,000 visitors. This figure **rose steadily** over the following decade, **reaching a peak of** 250,000 in 2010. **After that**, numbers **dipped slightly** to 240,000 in 2015, **before levelling off** at around 245,000 in 2020.*

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: verbo ↔ substantivo
> Sales **rose sharply** in 2010. → There was **a sharp rise** in sales in 2010.
> 1. Prices fell slightly in 2012. 2. The population grew steadily. 3. Unemployment decreased dramatically. 4. Exports fluctuated between 2000 and 2005.
### 4.2 to, by, from, at — complete
1. Profits rose ___ $2 million ___ $5 million. 2. The rate fell ___ 3% (diferença). 3. It peaked ___ 70%. 4. In 1990, the figure stood ___ 40%. 5. The number increased ___ 1,200 (valor final).
### 4.3 Descreva oralmente
> **Coffee consumption (cups per person/year), 2000–2020:** 2000: 300 · 2005: 310 · 2010: 380 · 2015: 450 · 2020: 440

## Gabarito do professor
- **4.1:** 1 There was a slight fall in prices in 2012. 2 There was steady growth in the population. 3 There was a dramatic decrease in unemployment. 4 There were fluctuations in exports between 2000 and 2005.
- **4.2:** 1 from… to · 2 by · 3 at · 4 at · 5 to.
- **4.3 (exemplo):** Consumption rose marginally from 300 to 310 cups between 2000 and 2005, then grew sharply, peaking at 450 in 2015, before dipping slightly to 440 in 2020.
