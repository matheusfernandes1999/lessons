## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** ensaios de **problema/causa + solução**. A nota alta vem de soluções **ligadas** aos problemas (cada solução responde a um problema específico) e **realistas**.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** organizar o ensaio em problema → solução, com linguagem de causa, efeito e proposta.
**Tarefa verificável:** redigir resposta com propostas relacionadas aos problemas.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Para cada problema, diga 1 causa e 1 solução:
- *traffic jams in big cities · obesity in children · plastic in the oceans · loneliness among elderly people*
**Troque e fale:**
> **One major cause of** **traffic jams** is **the number of private cars**. **One effective solution would be to** **improve public transport**.
> → obesity / fast food advertising / ban junk food ads for children → plastic / single-use packaging / tax plastic bags → loneliness / people living alone / create community centres
**Revisão da aula 35:** diga 3 formas de relatar a opinião de outras pessoas.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Problemas
- *a serious/major/growing problem · an issue · a challenge · a threat to · to have a negative impact on · to lead to · to result in*
### Causas
- *is caused by · is due to · stems from · a major factor is · one reason for this is*
### Soluções (modais e estruturas)
- *Governments should/could/ought to… · One possible solution is to… · An effective measure would be… · This problem could be tackled/addressed by… · If…, … would…*
- *raise awareness · impose taxes on · provide incentives for · invest in · introduce regulations · launch campaigns*
### Ligação problema → solução
- *To tackle this, … · In order to address this issue, … · This would help to reduce…*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **tackle** = TACK-le · **incentive** = in-CEN-tive · **regulation** = reg-u-LA-tion · **awareness** = a-WARE-ness
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> To tackle this issue, governments should invest in public transport and provide incentives for car sharing.

:::quiz vocab

= This problem could be tackled by raising taxes.

*A) is caused by / comes from || Correto.
B) solves || Não.
? w|vocabulary|Governments should ______ awareness of the dangers of sugar. (aumentar — r…)
> raise
= raise awareness
? mc|vocabulary|An “incentive” is…
*A) something that encourages people to do something || Correto.

? w|grammar|If governments ______ cycling, fewer people would drive. (promote — 2ª condicional)

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Duas estruturas possíveis
- **A (por blocos):** Body 1 = problemas/causas · Body 2 = soluções correspondentes
- **B (por pares):** Body 1 = problema 1 + solução 1 · Body 2 = problema 2 + solução 2
### Regras
- **Cada solução responde a um problema mencionado.**
- Soluções **específicas** (*introduce a tax on sugary drinks*) > vagas (*the government should do something*).
- Use **condicional** para mostrar efeito: *If…, this would…*
### Modelo (estrutura B)
> **Enunciado:** *In many cities, young people cannot afford to buy or rent a home. What are the causes of this problem, and what measures could be taken to solve it?*
> **Body 1:** *One major cause is the shortage of affordable housing in city centres, where most jobs are located. As demand grows, prices rise faster than salaries. To tackle this, local governments could require developers to include a percentage of low-cost flats in every new building, as some European cities already do.*
> **Body 2:** *A second factor is the growth of short-term holiday rentals, which reduce the number of homes available to residents. This issue could be addressed by limiting the number of days per year that a property can be rented to tourists. If such rules were enforced, more flats would return to the long-term market.*

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: ligando problema e solução
> **Because** **many people drive alone**, **one solution would be to** **introduce car-sharing lanes**.
> → teenagers spend hours online / schools could teach digital wellbeing → fast food is cheap / tax sugary drinks → older people live alone / fund volunteer visiting programmes
### 4.2 Específico × vago
Melhore as soluções vagas:
1. *The government should do something about pollution.*
2. *People should be more careful with water.*
3. *Schools need to help students more.*
### 4.3 Combine (problema → solução)
Problemas: 1 rising obesity · 2 traffic congestion · 3 plastic waste · 4 youth unemployment
Soluções: a) congestion charge in the centre · b) deposit-return scheme for bottles · c) apprenticeships with local companies · d) healthier school meals and sport

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada (15 min)
**Enunciado:** *In many countries, the amount of food that is wasted is increasing. What are the causes of this problem, and what solutions can you suggest?*
**Plano (2 min):** 2 causas + 2 soluções correspondentes (estrutura A ou B).
**Escreva (11 min):** introdução + 2 parágrafos de corpo + conclusão (≈230 palavras).
> ______
**Revisão (2 min):** cada solução responde a uma causa? Há pelo menos uma condicional?

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Vocabulário.** Faça frases com 10 expressões de problema/solução.
**Dia 2 — Banco de soluções.** Escreva 2 soluções específicas para 6 problemas comuns em IELTS (trânsito, obesidade, crime, poluição, desemprego, solidão).
**Dia 3 — Plano.** Faça planos para 2 enunciados oficiais de problema/solução.
**Dia 4 — Speaking.** Grave 1 minuto: *What can be done to reduce food waste?*
**Dia 5 — Ensaio completo** em 40 minutos.
**Dia 6 — Revisão.** Confira se cada solução se liga a um problema; reescreva um parágrafo.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
