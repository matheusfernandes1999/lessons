## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** falas **indiretas** — quando a pessoa pede, sugere ou pede esclarecimento **sem dizer diretamente** (*“It's a bit cold in here…”* = feche a janela).
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** perceber a intenção escondida e escolher a resposta natural, mesmo com distratores parecidos.
**Tarefa verificável:** acertar respostas em dez turnos com distratores.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
O professor diz frases indiretas. O aluno diz **o que a pessoa realmente quer** e responde.
- “It's really hot in here.” → (abrir a janela/ligar o ar) → *“Want me to open the window?”*
- “I don't have a pen…” → ______
- “I'm starving.” → ______
- “I'm not sure I understood the last part.” → ______
**Revisão da aula 17:** responda: *“Why don't we take a break?”* e *“Is the library open?”*

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Pedidos indiretos (do mais direto ao mais educado)
- *Can you…?* → *Could you…?* → *Would you mind + -ing…?* → *I was wondering if you could…* → *Do you think you could…?*
- **Atenção:** *“Would you mind closing the door?”* → para aceitar: ***“Not at all.”*** / ***“No, of course not.”*** (= não me importo = vou fazer!)
### Sugestões
- *Why don't you/we…?* · *How about + -ing?* · *Maybe you should…* · *Have you thought about…?* · *You might want to…*
### Pedidos de esclarecimento
- *What do you mean by…?* · *Sorry, did you say…?* · *Could you clarify…?* · *So you're saying that…?*
### Respostas que mostram hesitação/recusa educada
- *I'm afraid I can't.* · *I'd rather not.* · *Let me think about it.* · *That might be tricky.*

### Pronúncia: palavras que somem na fala rápida
Ouça, compare e repita (lento → rápido):
- **Would you mind** → *wudja-MIND* · **Do you think** → *dyuh-THINK* · **What do you mean** → *whaddya-MEAN*
- **Why don't we** → *why-DOAN-we* · **How about** → *how-BOUT*
Pares parecidos: **mind / mine / mild** · **rather / later / ladder**
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Would you mind if we started a bit later?

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Os 3 distratores mais comuns
1. **Eco de palavras:** repete uma palavra da fala com outro sentido. 🎧 *“Can you give me a hand with these boxes?”* → ✘ *“My hand hurts.”*
2. **Função errada:** responde como se fosse outra coisa. 🎧 *“Why don't we order pizza?”* → ✘ *“Because it's expensive.”* (responde *why*, mas era sugestão!)
3. **Literal demais:** não entende a indireta. 🎧 *“It's getting late.”* → ✘ *“Yes, it's 11 p.m.”* (a pessoa quer ir embora)
### Modelo
🎧 > “I was wondering if you had a minute to look at my résumé.”
- a) I have a minute, it's 3:01. ✘ (literal)
- b) Sure, I'm free now. Let's see it. ✔
- c) I wonder too. ✘ (eco)

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: aceitar e recusar
> **Sure, I'd be happy to.** / **I'm afraid I can't right now — maybe later?**
O professor faz pedidos; o aluno alterna aceitar e recusar com uma razão:
> Could you lend me your notes? · Would you mind switching seats? · Do you think you could cover my shift? · Can you help me move on Saturday?

### 4.2 Diga a intenção escondida e responda
🎧 1. “This box is way too heavy for me.”
🎧 2. “The deadline is tomorrow and I haven't even started.”
🎧 3. “I've never used this software before.”
🎧 4. “Sorry — the first or the second floor?”
🎧 5. “You look exhausted.”

### 4.3 Sugestão ou pergunta de motivo?
🎧 a) “Why don't you ask the professor?” b) “Why didn't you ask the professor?”
→ (a) é sugestão → responda *“Good idea, I will.”* (b) é pergunta de motivo → responda *“I didn't have time.”*
Pratique: o professor fala uma das duas versões aleatoriamente para 4 situações; o aluno responde de acordo.

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: 10 turnos (15 min)
🎧 Cada fala uma vez. Resposta em até 10 segundos. Depois, o aluno fala a resposta em voz alta.
1. “Would you mind turning the music down?” — a) Not at all, sorry! b) Yes, I like music. c) It's a new song.
2. “How about studying at my place tonight?” — a) About two hours. b) Sounds good. What time? c) I studied last night.
3. “I'm not sure what the professor meant by 'peer review'.” — a) It's when classmates check each other's work. b) The professor is sure. c) I reviewed it.
4. “You might want to leave early — there's a lot of traffic.” — a) Thanks, I'll go now. b) I want to leave traffic. c) Traffic is early.
5. “Do you think you could cover my shift on Friday?” — a) I think so too. b) I'm afraid I can't — I'm traveling. c) The shift is on Friday.
6. “So you're saying the exam is open-book?” — a) Yes, you can use your notes. b) I'm saying hello. c) The book is open.
7. “Have you thought about applying for the internship?” — a) I'm thinking about lunch. b) Actually, I sent my application yesterday. c) The internship thought.
8. “I can't hear the speaker from back here.” — a) Let's move closer. b) The speaker is loud. c) I'm here.
9. “Maybe you should talk to your advisor.” — a) My advisor talks a lot. b) That's a good idea. I'll email her. c) Maybe.
10. “I'd rather not go out tonight.” — a) OK, let's stay in then. b) Rather is a word. c) Go out now.
**Acertos:** __/10
**Rodada de produção (5 min):** o professor repete 1, 2, 5, 7 e 9. O aluno cria uma resposta diferente para cada (aceitar ↔ recusar).

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Pedidos.** Escreva o mesmo pedido de 5 formas (do mais direto ao mais educado).
**Dia 2 — Fala rápida.** Repita 10 vezes: *wudja-mind, dyuh-think, whaddya-mean, how-bout*. Grave frases completas.
**Dia 3 — Indiretas.** Escreva 8 falas indiretas e o que elas realmente significam.
**Dia 4 — Sombra.** Grave as 10 falas da tarefa com entonação natural.
**Dia 5 — Speaking.** Grave um mini diálogo (você faz os dois papéis): pedir um favor, receber recusa educada, sugerir alternativa.
**Dia 6 — Distratores.** Para 5 falas da aula, escreva 1 resposta certa + 2 distratores (eco e função errada).
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
