## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** fechar o curso com um **mini simulado final**, uma revisão relâmpago de **todas as estratégias** e um **plano individual** para os últimos dias e para o dia da prova.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** apresentar em inglês seu plano de revisão, sua rotina pré-prova e sua estratégia para cada seção.
**Tarefa verificável:** concluir simulado e formular plano individual de revisão.

## 1. Warm-up (4 min)
- How do you feel about the test now compared to lesson 1?
- What is the most useful strategy you learned?
**Troque e fale:**
> At the beginning, I **didn't know how to write an overview**, but now **I can describe any chart clearly**.
> → panicked in Part 2 / I use my one minute to make good notes → confused False and Not Given / I compare statements carefully → gave one-word answers / I always extend my answers

## 2. Revisão relâmpago das estratégias (5 min)
O professor diz a tarefa; o aluno diz a estratégia em 1 frase (em inglês):
- **Listening Part 1** → *Predict the type of answer and check spelling.*
- **Listening MC/Matching** → *Expect distractors; wait for the final answer.*
- **Listening Part 4** → *Follow the headings; don't stop if you miss one.*
- **Reading TFNG/YNNG** → *Facts vs. opinions; contradiction vs. no information.*
- **Reading Headings** → *Summarise the paragraph; ignore repeated words.*
- **Reading Completion** → *Check grammar and word limit; copy exactly.*
- **Writing Task 1** → *Paraphrase, overview without numbers, group data, no opinion.*
- **Writing Task 2** → *Answer all parts, clear position, PEEL, natural cohesion.*
- **Speaking Part 1** → *Answer + extend.* · **Part 2** → *Keywords in one minute, speak for two.* · **Part 3** → *Opinion, reason, example, nuance.*

## 3. Mini simulado final (20 min)
### Listening (4 min) — 🎧 uma vez
> “Welcome to the Science Festival. Today's main talk, on robots in medicine, will take place in the Blue Hall — not the Red Hall as printed in your programme. It starts at two forty-five. Children's workshops are in the garden tent and are free, but places are limited, so please register at the information desk by noon. The festival closes at six, and the last shuttle bus to the station leaves at six fifteen.”
1. Talk location: ______ Hall 2. Start time: ______ 3. Register for workshops by: ______ 4. Last shuttle: ______
### Reading (5 min) — True/False/Not Given
> *Sleep researchers recommend that adults get seven to nine hours of sleep per night. However, surveys in several countries show that many adults sleep less than seven hours on weekdays. Some studies link short sleep to weight gain, although the exact reasons are still being investigated. Napping for 20 minutes during the day can improve alertness, but longer naps may make people feel groggy.*
5. Adults should sleep at least seven hours. 6. Most adults in the world sleep more than nine hours. 7. Scientists fully understand why short sleep is linked to weight gain. 8. Short naps can improve alertness.
### Writing (6 min) — introdução + tese
> *In some countries, young people are encouraged to work or travel for a year between finishing school and starting university. Discuss the advantages and disadvantages for young people who decide to do this.*
### Speaking (5 min)
- Part 1: *Do you like festivals? · What festivals are popular in your country?*
- Part 2 (1 min notas + 1,5 min): *Describe a festival or celebration you enjoyed.*
- Part 3: *Why are traditional festivals important? · Will festivals change in the future?*

## 6. Homework — as duas últimas semanas
Siga o **seu plano**. Sugestão mínima:
**Dia 1 — Simulado oficial completo** (condições reais).
**Dia 2 — Correção** com registro de erros.
**Dia 3 — Ponto de atenção 1** (30 min).
**Dia 4 — Ponto de atenção 2** (30 min).
**Dia 5 — Writing** T1 + T2 (60 min).
**Dia 6 — Speaking** completo gravado + Listening de uma seção.
**Dia 7 — Descanso ativo:** algo em inglês por prazer.
**Véspera:** só revisar o checklist; dormir bem. **Good luck!**
