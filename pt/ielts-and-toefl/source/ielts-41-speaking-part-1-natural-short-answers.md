## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** a **Part 1** (4–5 min): perguntas sobre você — casa, trabalho/estudo, rotina, gostos. A resposta ideal tem **2–3 frases**: resposta direta + **expansão** (razão, detalhe ou exemplo). Nem monossílaba, nem discurso.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** responder 10 perguntas de Part 1 com naturalidade e expansão adequada.
**Tarefa verificável:** responder a dez perguntas com expansão adequada.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Responda só com 1 palavra (sim, é proposital!): *Do you work or study? · Do you like your hometown? · Do you cook?* — Agora o professor pergunta de novo e o aluno responde com **2–3 frases**. Qual soa melhor?
**Revisão da aula 40:** qual é seu plano de tempo para o Writing?

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Começos naturais (variados)
- *Yes, I do. · Actually, … · Well, … · To be honest, … · Not really. · It depends. · I'd say… · Definitely.*
### Expansões
- **Razão:** *mainly because… · the reason is that…*
- **Detalhe:** *I usually… · especially… · most of the time…*
- **Exemplo:** *for example, last weekend… · like when…*
- **Contraste:** *although… · but…*
- **Mudança no tempo:** *I used to…, but now… · When I was a child,…*
### Vocabulário de temas comuns
- **Home:** *flat/apartment · neighbourhood · cosy · spacious · noisy · convenient location*
- **Work/study:** *in my final year · major in · deal with clients · flexible hours · deadline*
- **Free time:** *unwind · hang out · binge-watch · be into (something) · keep fit*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- Contrações naturais: *I'm · I've · I'd · it's · don't · didn't* → fale contraído! (Part 1 é conversa.)
- *used to* → *YOOS-tə* · *I'd say* → *aid-SAY*
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Actually, I used to hate cooking, but now I'm really into it, especially at weekends.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Fórmula A + E (Answer + Extend)
- **A:** responda direto (1 frase)
- **E:** acrescente 1–2 frases (razão / detalhe / exemplo / contraste / passado-presente)
### Modelos
> **Q:** *Do you like your neighbourhood?*
> **A+E:** *Yes, I really do. It's quite a quiet area, but it's also close to everything — there's a supermarket and a metro station just a few minutes away.*
> **Q:** *Do you often cook?*
> **A+E:** *Not as often as I'd like, to be honest. During the week I'm too busy, so I usually just make something quick, but on Sundays I love trying new recipes.*
> **Q:** *What did you like to do when you were a child?*
> **A+E:** *I used to spend hours playing football in the street with my cousins. We didn't have a proper pitch, so we used flip-flops as goalposts!*
### Erros que tiram nota
- Responder com 1 palavra · Decorar respostas longas (soa artificial) · Repetir a pergunta inteira (*“Do I like my neighbourhood? Yes, I like my neighbourhood…”*)

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: expansões
> **Yes, I do**, **mainly because** **it's close to my work**.
> → Not really / the reason is that / it's very noisy at night → It depends / on weekdays… but at weekends… → Definitely / especially / in summer, when…
### 4.2 Pingue-pongue (resposta em 3 s)
O professor faz perguntas rápidas; o aluno responde A+E:
> *Do you work or are you a student? · What do you like about your job/course? · Do you prefer mornings or evenings? · How often do you use public transport? · Do you like rainy days? · What kind of music do you listen to? · Have you ever been to a concert? · Do you prefer to read on paper or on a screen?*
### 4.3 Troque a estrutura (passado → presente)
> *I used to…, but now…* — fale sobre: *sport · food · music · how you study*

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: 10 perguntas (15 min)
O professor faz as perguntas como examinador (sem ajudar). O aluno responde A+E (10–20 s cada).
**Topic 1 — Work or studies:** 1. Do you work or are you a student? 2. Why did you choose that job/course? 3. What's the most difficult part of it?
**Topic 2 — Hometown:** 4. Where is your hometown? 5. What do you like most about it? 6. Has it changed much in recent years?
**Topic 3 — Weekends:** 7. What do you usually do at weekends? 8. Did you do anything special last weekend? 9. Do you prefer to spend weekends at home or going out? 10. Would you like to have a three-day weekend?
**Registro (professor):**
- Respostas com expansão: __/10 · Muito curtas: __ · Longas demais: __
- Fluência (1–9) __ · Vocabulário __ · Gramática __ · Pronúncia __
**Rodada 2 (4 min):** repita 3 respostas fracas com expansão diferente.

## 6. Feedback (3 min)
- [ ] Respondeu direto e expandiu (A+E)?
- [ ] Variou os começos (*Actually, Well, To be honest…*)?
- [ ] Usou tempos variados (*used to, have changed, would like*)?
- [ ] Soou natural (contrações, ritmo)?
**Ajuste:** ______

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**. Grave sempre.
**Dia 1 — Temas.** Liste 12 temas comuns de Part 1 (home, work, food, weather, music, sport, technology, friends, shopping, transport, holidays, reading).
**Dia 2 — Vocabulário.** Para 4 temas, escreva 5 palavras/expressões úteis cada.
**Dia 3 — Gravação.** Responda 3 perguntas para cada um de 3 temas (A+E). Escute.
**Dia 4 — Variação.** Regrave mudando os começos e as expansões.
**Dia 5 — Passado/presente.** Grave 5 respostas usando *I used to…, but now…*
**Dia 6 — Simulação.** Peça a alguém (ou use uma lista) para fazer 12 perguntas aleatórias; responda sem preparação.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
