## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** **desenvolver** a resposta de discussão: cada razão precisa de **explicação** e **exemplo**; um bom texto também reconhece e responde a um **contraponto**.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** escrever 130–160 palavras com duas razões bem desenvolvidas e um contraponto.
**Tarefa verificável:** produzir resposta com duas razões desenvolvidas.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
O professor dá uma afirmação; o aluno diz **“Why?”** três vezes para si mesmo, aprofundando a razão.
> *Students should learn financial skills at school.* → Why? Because many young people get into debt. → Why? Because they don't understand interest rates. → Why? Because nobody explains how credit cards work.
Agora você: *Cities should plant more trees.*
**Revisão da aula 38:** diga os 4 movimentos da resposta de discussão.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Adicionar segunda razão
- *Another reason is that…* · *In addition,…* · *Moreover,…* · *On top of that,…* · *Equally important,…*
### Explicar (aprofundar)
- *This means that…* · *In other words,…* · *As a result,…* · *This is important because…*
### Contraponto + resposta (concessão)
- *Some people might argue that…; however,…*
- *It's true that…, but…*
- *Admittedly,…. Even so,…*
- *While I understand that…, I still believe…*
### Exemplos com credibilidade
- *For instance, in my city,…* · *A good example is…* · *According to a recent report,…* (sem inventar números exatos!)
### Vocabulário útil
- **long-term / short-term** · **in practice** · **evidence** · **consequence** · **essential** · **significantly**

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **moreover** = more-O-ver · **admittedly** = ad-MIT-ted-ly · **consequence** = CON-se-quence
- **while / well / wheel** · **argue / agree**
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Admittedly, it's expensive. Even so, the long-term benefits are significant.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Uma razão desenvolvida = R + E + Ex
- **R**azão: *Trees reduce heat.*
- **E**xplicação: *Their shade and the water they release cool the streets, so people use less air conditioning.*
- **Ex**emplo: *In my neighborhood, the street with old trees is noticeably cooler in summer.*
### Estrutura de 130–160 palavras
1. Posição + conexão com colega (1–2 frases)
2. Razão 1 + explicação + exemplo (3 frases)
3. Razão 2 + explicação (2 frases)
4. Contraponto + resposta (1–2 frases)
### Modelo
> **Professor:** *Should cities invest more money in parks and green spaces, even if this means less money for other services?*
> **Ana:** *Yes. Parks make people happier and healthier.*
> **Ben:** *No. Cities have more urgent problems, like public transportation and housing.*
> **Resposta:**
> I agree with Ana that cities should invest in green spaces, and I'd add that parks also solve practical problems. First, trees reduce heat in cities. Their shade and the water they release cool the streets, which means people use less air conditioning. For example, in my neighborhood, the street with old trees is noticeably cooler in summer. Another reason is that parks can reduce flooding, because soil and plants absorb rainwater that would otherwise run into the streets. Ben might argue that transportation and housing are more urgent. That's true, but green spaces are relatively cheap and they help with these problems too, since a cooler, safer city is better for everyone.
(≈ 130 palavras)
