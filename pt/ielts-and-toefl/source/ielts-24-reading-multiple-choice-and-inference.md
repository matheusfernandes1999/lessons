## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** múltipla escolha no Reading, incluindo perguntas de **inferência** (*What does the writer suggest…? What can be inferred…?*) e de **propósito** (*Why does the writer mention…?*).
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** escolher a opção que o texto **apoia** — nem mais, nem menos — e eliminar distratores com motivo.
**Tarefa verificável:** responder oito questões inferenciais.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
O professor lê uma situação; o aluno infere:
- *“The streets were wet and people were carrying umbrellas.”* → ______
- *“Sales of the new phone fell after reviewers mentioned battery problems.”* → ______
- *“The author describes the plan as ‘ambitious but poorly funded’.”* → What does the author think? ______
**Revisão da aula 23:** o que significa *NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS from the passage*?

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Tipos de pergunta
- **Detail:** *According to the writer, …?* (está escrito)
- **Inference:** *The writer suggests / implies that…* (está implícito, mas apoiado)
- **Purpose:** *Why does the writer mention X?* → *to illustrate / to contrast / to support / to criticise*
- **Attitude:** *What is the writer's attitude to X?* → *sceptical, supportive, neutral, critical, cautious*
### Distratores típicos
- **Exagero:** *always, all, completely, never*
- **Verdade, mas não responde** à pergunta
- **Palavra igual ao texto, sentido diferente**
- **Conhecimento geral** (verdade no mundo, mas não no texto)
### Adjetivos de atitude
- **sceptical** (cético) · **optimistic** · **cautious** (cauteloso) · **critical** · **supportive** · **ambivalent** (dividido) · **dismissive** (desdenhoso)

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **sceptical** = SCEP-ti-cal · **ambivalent** = am-BIV-a-lent · **imply / employ**
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> The writer seems sceptical, implying that the plan is ambitious but unrealistic.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Estratégia
1. Leia a **pergunta** (não as opções ainda) e localize o trecho.
2. Leia o trecho e **responda com suas palavras**.
3. Agora leia as opções e escolha a mais próxima da sua resposta.
4. **Teste a opção:** consigo apontar uma frase que a apoia? Se não, elimine.
### Modelo
> *Many cities have introduced bike-sharing schemes, hoping to reduce traffic. In practice, the results have varied. In Copenhagen, where cycling was already common, the schemes were quickly adopted. In cities with few bike lanes, however, the bicycles were often left unused, and several schemes closed within a year.*
> **Q:** *What does the writer suggest about bike-sharing schemes?*
> A) They always reduce traffic. B) Their success depends on existing conditions. C) They are more popular in Europe than in Asia. D) They are too expensive to run.
- A ✘ exagero (*always*; o texto diz *varied*) · C ✘ não mencionado · D ✘ não mencionado · **B ✔** (Copenhagen já tinha ciclismo → sucesso; sem ciclovias → fracasso)

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: propósito
> The writer mentions **Copenhagen** **to show** that **schemes work where cycling is already common**.
> → the closed schemes / illustrate / they can fail without infrastructure → a 2019 study / support / the claim that sleep helps memory → old beliefs / contrast them with / modern research
### 4.2 Qual a atitude?
1. *“The proposal, while well-intentioned, ignores the practical difficulties.”*
2. *“This remarkable discovery could transform the treatment of diabetes.”*
3. *“It remains unclear whether these benefits will last.”*
4. *“Such claims are, frankly, absurd.”*
(critical · optimistic · cautious · dismissive)

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: 8 questões (15 min)
> **The Return of the Lynx?**
> **(1)** The Eurasian lynx disappeared from Britain more than a thousand years ago, mainly because of hunting and the loss of forests. Today, some conservation groups are campaigning to bring it back to remote parts of Scotland and northern England.
> **(2)** Supporters argue that the lynx would help control the growing population of roe deer, which damage young trees and prevent forests from regenerating. They point to Switzerland, where lynx were reintroduced in the 1970s and now live alongside farmers with relatively few conflicts.
> **(3)** Sheep farmers, however, are far from convinced. They fear that lynx would attack lambs, and many feel that decisions are being made by city-based campaigners who will not have to deal with the consequences. Compensation schemes, they say, rarely cover the full cost of lost animals.
> **(4)** Scientists themselves are divided. Some studies suggest that lynx prefer woodland prey and rarely approach farms; others note that conditions in Britain, where forests are small and fragmented, may push lynx into closer contact with livestock.
> **(5)** What seems clear is that any reintroduction would need the support of local communities. Without it, the project would risk failing — not because of ecology, but because of politics.
1. Why did the lynx disappear from Britain? A) disease B) hunting and deforestation C) competition with deer D) climate change
2. Why does the writer mention Switzerland? A) to show reintroduction can work B) to criticise Swiss farmers C) to compare deer populations D) to describe lynx behaviour in mountains
3. What is the farmers' main concern? A) lynx would eat deer B) lynx would harm their animals C) forests would grow D) tourists would come
4. What does the writer suggest about the farmers' view of campaigners? A) farmers trust them B) farmers think campaigners won't suffer the effects C) campaigners live on farms D) campaigners pay compensation
5. What is the writer's view of compensation schemes? A) the writer doesn't give a personal view — it's the farmers' view B) they are generous C) they are unnecessary D) they always work
6. What does paragraph 4 show about scientists? A) they all support the project B) they disagree about the risk to livestock C) they have no data D) they oppose the project
7. Why might British conditions be different? A) forests are small and broken up B) there are no sheep C) deer are smaller D) winters are colder
8. What is the writer's main conclusion? A) the lynx should not return B) ecology is the main obstacle C) local support is essential D) Switzerland's model will fail
**Acertos:** __/8 · **Justifique 3 respostas oralmente (3 min).**
