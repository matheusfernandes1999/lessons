## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** entender **o que a pessoa quer** (intenção) e **a ideia principal**, mesmo sem entender todas as palavras.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** ouvir uma fala curta e dizer em uma frase: *“He wants to… / She is complaining about… / They are suggesting…”*
**Tarefa verificável:** identificar a mensagem central em seis falas.
**Atenção, professor:** os textos de listening estão em citações marcadas com 🎧. Leia em voz alta em ritmo natural; o aluno não deve olhar o texto antes de responder.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- When you watch a series in English, do you understand everything? What do you do when you don't?
- Is it easier to understand men or women? Fast or slow speakers? British or American accents?
- What do you do when you don't understand a word in a conversation?
**Frases úteis:**
- *I usually get the general idea, but I miss details.*
- *I get lost when people speak fast.*
- *Could you say that again, please?*
**Revisão da aula 4:** diga 3 palavras da família de *produce* e uma frase com cada.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Verbos de intenção (o que a pessoa quer fazer)
- **ask for** — pedir — *She is asking for help.*
- **suggest** — sugerir — *He suggests meeting tomorrow.*
- **complain (about)** — reclamar — *They are complaining about the noise.*
- **apologize (for)** — pedir desculpas — *He apologizes for being late.*
- **offer** — oferecer — *She offers to help.*
- **refuse / decline** — recusar — *He declines the invitation.*
- **agree / disagree** — concordar / discordar
- **remind** — lembrar alguém — *The professor reminds students about the test.*
- **warn** — avisar de um perigo/problema — *She warns him that the road is closed.*
- **check / confirm** — verificar / confirmar
- **invite** — convidar
- **thank** — agradecer
**Fórmula para responder:** *The speaker wants to* + verbo / *The speaker is* + verbo-ing.

### Pronúncia: fala conectada (como o inglês soa de verdade)
Ouça, compare e repita: primeiro devagar, depois rápido.
- **did you** → *didja*: *Didja finish the essay?*
- **want to** → *wanna*: *I wanna go.*
- **going to** → *gonna*: *It's gonna rain.*
- **could you** → *couldja*: *Couldja help me?*
- **can / can't**: *I CAN go* (can fraco: /kən/) × *I CAN'T go* (can't forte, “t” quase mudo).
O professor fala 6 frases com *can* ou *can't*; o aluno diz qual ouviu:
> I can come tomorrow. · She can't find the room. · We can meet at noon. · He can't hear you. · You can borrow it. · They can't register yet.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### A ideia principal NÃO é a primeira palavra que você entende
Três perguntas para fazer enquanto ouve:
1. **Quem** está falando e **com quem**? (aluno → professor, amigo → amigo)
2. **Qual é o problema ou o assunto?**
3. **O que a pessoa quer?** (pedir, sugerir, reclamar…)
**Cuidado com as palavras “armadilha”:** a resposta errada muitas vezes repete palavras que você ouviu. A certa costuma usar **outras palavras** (paráfrase).

### Modelo
🎧 Professor lê:
> “Hey, I was wondering if you could send me your notes from Tuesday's lecture. I had a doctor's appointment and couldn't make it.”
**Raciocínio em voz alta:**
- Quem: um aluno para um colega.
- Problema: faltou à aula de terça.
- Intenção: **pedir as anotações**.
- Resposta em uma frase: **The speaker is asking for the lecture notes because he missed class.**
Agora o aluno fala junto com o professor a frase do modelo (mesmo ritmo, mesma entonação).

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale
> The speaker is **asking for** notes.
> → suggesting a new time → complaining about the Wi-Fi → apologizing for the delay → offering a ride → reminding us about the deadline
> I was wondering if you could **send me the notes**.
> → help me with the form → lend me your charger → explain the assignment → check my email

### 4.2 Ouça e diga a intenção
🎧 Professor lê cada fala uma vez. Aluno responde com *The speaker wants to… / is…ing…*
> 1. “Honestly, the printers in this lab never work. That's the third time this week.”
> 2. “If you want, I can drive you to the airport on Saturday.”
> 3. “Maybe we should start the presentation with the graph instead of the text.”
> 4. “Just a quick note: the lab report is due before midnight, not at the end of class.”
> 5. “Thanks, but I already have plans for Friday.”
> 6. “Sorry, did you say room 204 or 214?”

### 4.3 Escolha a paráfrase
Para cada fala acima, o aluno escolhe a frase que diz **a mesma coisa com outras palavras**:
- a) The speaker turns down an invitation.
- b) The speaker is unhappy with the equipment.
- c) The speaker wants to confirm a detail.
- d) The speaker proposes a change.
- e) The speaker offers help with transportation.
- f) The speaker reminds others of a time limit.

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: 6 falas (15 min)
**Regras:** o professor lê cada fala **uma vez**. O aluno tem **20 segundos** para dizer a mensagem central em uma frase e depois escolhe a alternativa.
🎧 1.
> “I tried to register for Biology 101 online, but the system says I need a permission code. Do you know who gives those?”
a) She wants to drop Biology 101. b) She needs information about getting a permission code. c) She is complaining about the professor.
🎧 2.
> “The cafeteria will stop serving breakfast at nine thirty starting next week, so plan accordingly.”
a) Breakfast hours are changing. b) The cafeteria is closing. c) Breakfast will be free next week.
🎧 3.
> “I really appreciate you staying late to help me with the experiment. I couldn't have done it alone.”
a) He is apologizing. b) He is thanking someone. c) He is inviting someone.
🎧 4.
> “Before you submit, double-check that your name is on every page. Last semester, a few reports got lost.”
a) The speaker is giving a warning/advice. b) The speaker lost a report. c) The speaker wants to read the reports.
🎧 5.
> “Do you think you could switch shifts with me on Thursday? I have an exam that morning.”
a) He wants to take an exam on Thursday. b) He asks to change work shifts. c) He is offering to work more.
🎧 6.
> “I don't think we need another meeting. We can solve this by email.”
a) She disagrees with having a meeting. b) She wants to schedule a meeting. c) She didn't receive the email.
**Acertos:** __/6   **Frases de mensagem central corretas:** __/6

**Extra (se sobrar tempo):** o professor relê as falas e o aluno repete a fala 3 e a 5 (falar junto, mesmo ritmo).

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Vocabulário.** Escreva uma fala sua para cada verbo de intenção (12 falas). Ex.: *suggest → “Why don't we take a break?”*
**Dia 2 — Fala conectada.** Repita 10 vezes cada: *didja, wanna, gonna, couldja*. Depois grave 4 frases usando cada uma.
**Dia 3 — Listening.** Assista a 3 minutos de uma série ou vídeo sobre universidade. Pause depois de 5 falas e escreva a intenção de cada.
**Dia 4 — Sombra.** Leia em voz alta as 6 falas da tarefa cronometrada, imitando a entonação natural. Grave a última leitura.
**Dia 5 — Speaking.** Grave 45 s: *Tell me about a time you complained about something. What happened?*
**Dia 6 — Paráfrase.** Reescreva as 6 falas da prática 4.2 com outras palavras.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
