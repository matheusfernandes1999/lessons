## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** um **mini teste** com as 4 seções (versão compacta para 45 min) e **análise detalhada**: acertos por tipo de questão em Listening/Reading e estimativa por critério em Writing/Speaking.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** saber exatamente onde está cada habilidade e quais critérios precisam de mais trabalho.
**Tarefa verificável:** registrar acertos e feedback analítico por critério.
**Observação:** faça um simulado **completo e oficial** fora da aula nesta semana (≈2h45).

## 3. Reading (10 min)
> **Microplastics**
> Microplastics are pieces of plastic smaller than five millimetres. Some are manufactured at this size, such as the tiny beads once common in cosmetics, while others form when larger items break down. Scientists have found microplastics almost everywhere they have looked: in oceans, in Arctic ice, in drinking water and even in human blood.
> Several countries, including the UK and the USA, have banned microbeads in rinse-off cosmetics. However, researchers point out that these beads represent only a small share of the problem. A much larger source is synthetic clothing: every time polyester or nylon is washed, thousands of tiny fibres are released.
> The effects on human health are not yet clear. Laboratory studies suggest that high concentrations may damage cells, but the amounts people are exposed to in daily life may be far lower. Most experts agree that more research is needed before firm conclusions can be drawn.
10. Microplastics are pieces of plastic smaller than five millimetres. (T/F/NG)
11. All microplastics are manufactured at a small size. (T/F/NG)
12. The UK has banned microbeads in all cosmetic products. (T/F/NG)
13. Synthetic clothing is a major source of microplastics. (T/F/NG)
14. Microplastics have been proven to cause serious diseases in humans. (T/F/NG)
15. One washing machine company has invented a filter for fibres. (T/F/NG)
**Acertos:** __/6

## 4. Writing (12 min)
**Task 1 — overview only (3 min):**
> **Table — Average weekly hours of exercise by age group, 2010 and 2020:** 18–30: 4.5 → 5.0 · 31–50: 3.0 → 3.5 · 51–70: 2.0 → 3.0 · 70+: 1.0 → 2.0
> *Write the introduction and overview.*
**Task 2 — introduction + 1 body paragraph (9 min):**
> *Some people believe that the government should be responsible for encouraging people to do more exercise. Others think it is the responsibility of individuals. Discuss both views and give your opinion.*

## 5. Speaking (8 min)
- **Part 1 (2 min):** *Do you like sports? · How often do you exercise? · Did you play any sports at school?*
- **Part 2 (1 min notas + 2 min):** *Describe a sport or physical activity you enjoy. You should say: what it is · when you started · how often you do it · and explain why you enjoy it.*
- **Part 3 (2 min):** *Why do some people not exercise enough? · Should schools have more PE lessons?*
