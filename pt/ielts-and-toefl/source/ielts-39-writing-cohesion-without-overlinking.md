## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** **Coherence and Cohesion** — muitos alunos usam conectores **demais** (*Firstly, Moreover, Furthermore, In addition, Additionally…* em toda frase). O examinador chama isso de **mechanical/overuse**. Coesão natural vem de **referência** (*this, these, such*), **substituição**, **repetição de palavra-chave** e conectores **só quando necessários**.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** revisar um texto e trocar conectores mecânicos por ligações naturais.
**Tarefa verificável:** revisar redação removendo conectores mecânicos.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Leia em voz alta e diga o que soa estranho:
> *Firstly, cars are expensive. Moreover, they pollute. Furthermore, they cause traffic. In addition, parking is difficult. Additionally, they are dangerous.*
Agora diga a mesma ideia de forma natural (o professor ajuda).
**Revisão da aula 38:** desenvolva: *Remote work reduces traffic.* (cadeia de 2 consequências)

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Ferramentas de coesão (além dos conectores)
- **Referência:** *this, that, these, those, it, they, such + noun* → *This trend… · Such measures… · These problems…*
- **Substantivos-resumo:** *this problem / issue / trend / approach / development / measure / situation*
- **Substituição:** *one/ones, do so, the former/the latter*
- **Sinônimos** do tema (sem exagerar): *cars → vehicles → private transport*
- **Orações relativas:** *…, which means that… · …, which is why…*
### Conectores — use com função clara
- **contraste:** *however, although, while, whereas* · **causa/efeito:** *because, as a result, therefore* · **adição:** *also, in addition* (moderado) · **exemplo:** *for example, such as*
### Erros comuns
- conector com **função errada** (*Moreover* para contraste ✘)
- **On the other hand** sem *on the one hand* implícito de comparação de lados
- conector no início de **toda** frase

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **cohesion** = co-HE-sion · **coherence** = co-HER-ence · **mechanical** = me-CHAN-i-cal · **whereas** = wear-AZ
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Such measures can reduce traffic, which is why many cities are introducing them.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Antes (mecânico)
> *Firstly, many people move to cities for jobs. Moreover, cities are becoming crowded. Furthermore, housing is expensive. In addition, there is more pollution. Additionally, the government should build more houses. Moreover, it should improve public transport. In conclusion, cities have problems.*
### Depois (natural)
> *Many people move to cities in search of work, and as a result, urban areas are becoming increasingly crowded. **This growth** puts pressure on housing, pushing prices beyond what many families can afford. **It** also leads to higher levels of pollution, as more vehicles fill the streets. To address **these problems**, governments could invest in affordable housing and better public transport, **both of which** would make city life more sustainable.*
**O que mudou:** 1 conector de efeito (*as a result*) · referências (*This growth, It, these problems*) · relativa (*both of which*) · ideias conectadas pela lógica.

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: substantivos-resumo
> Many young people use social media for hours. **This habit** can affect their sleep.
> → Some cities have banned cars from the centre / This policy → Prices have risen sharply / This increase → Workers are moving abroad / This trend → Governments taxed sugary drinks / Such measures
### 4.2 Escolha o conector com a função certa
1. *Online classes are flexible. ___, students can feel isolated.* (Moreover / However)
2. *Fuel prices rose. ___, more people started cycling.* (As a result / Although)
3. *___ tourism creates jobs, it can damage the environment.* (Although / Furthermore)
4. *Some countries, ___ Norway, have high taxes on cars.* (such as / moreover)
### 4.3 Junte com relativa
1. *The city built new bike lanes. This encouraged people to cycle.* → *…, which…*
2. *Many students work part-time. This is why they have less time to study.* → *…, which is why…*

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: revisão (15 min)
### Texto para revisar (≈150 palavras)
> *Firstly, technology has changed education. Moreover, students use laptops in class. Furthermore, they can access online courses. In addition, teachers use videos. Additionally, students can study at home. On the other hand, technology has disadvantages. Moreover, students get distracted. Furthermore, some students do not have computers. In addition, this is unfair. Moreover, teachers need training. Furthermore, it is expensive. In conclusion, technology has advantages and disadvantages and the government should do something.*
**Reescreva (11 min):** mantenha as ideias, mas use no máximo **4 conectores**, pelo menos **3 referências** (*this/such/these + noun*), **1 relativa** e organize em 2 parágrafos (vantagens / desvantagens + solução).
> ______
**Compare (4 min):** leia as duas versões em voz alta. Qual soa mais natural? Conte os conectores.

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Substantivos-resumo.** Escreva 10 pares de frases ligadas por *this + noun*.
**Dia 2 — Revisão.** Pegue um ensaio seu antigo e conte os conectores. Corte metade e use referências.
**Dia 3 — Relativas.** Escreva 8 frases com *which means that / which is why / both of which*.
**Dia 4 — Leitura modelo.** Leia um artigo de jornal de qualidade e sublinhe 10 ferramentas de coesão (não conectores).
**Dia 5 — Ensaio completo** em 40 minutos, com atenção à coesão natural.
**Dia 6 — Speaking.** Grave 1 minuto sobre tecnologia na educação usando *this, such, which is why*.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
