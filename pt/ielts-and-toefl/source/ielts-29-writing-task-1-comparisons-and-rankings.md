## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** gráficos de **barras, pizza e tabelas** sem (ou com pouco) tempo: o essencial é **comparar** categorias, destacar **extremos** (maior/menor) e **agrupar** dados parecidos.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** escrever um resumo de tabela com comparativos, superlativos e expressões de proporção corretos.
**Tarefa verificável:** redigir resumo de tabela com comparações pertinentes.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Compare oralmente:
- *São Paulo and Rio (size, weather, cost of living)*
- *coffee and tea (popularity in your country)*
- *three apps you use (time spent on each)*
**Troque e fale:**
> **São Paulo** is **much bigger than** **Rio**, but **Rio** is **far more popular with tourists**.
> → Brazil / Argentina / larger / Argentina / colder → coffee / tea / more popular / tea / healthier
**Revisão da aula 28:** diga a diferença entre *rose to 50%* e *rose by 50%*.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Comparativos e superlativos
- *higher/lower than · more/less than · the highest/lowest · the largest proportion*
- **Intensificadores:** *much / far / significantly / considerably higher* · *slightly / marginally lower*
- **Igualdade:** *as high as · the same as · similar to · roughly equal*
- **Múltiplos:** *twice as many as · three times higher than · half as much as*
### Proporções (pie charts e %)
- 50% = *half* · 25% = *a quarter* · 33% = *a third* · 75% = *three quarters* · 20% = *one in five* · 10% = *one in ten*
- *the majority (>50%) · a minority · just over / just under / nearly / approximately*
### Ranking e agrupamento
- *ranked first/second · followed by · came last · X and Y had similar figures · the remaining categories*
### Contraste
- *whereas · while · in contrast · by comparison*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **considerably** = con-SID-er-a-bly · **approximately** · **majority / minority**
- **twice / twelve · half / have · third / thirty**
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Spending on housing was twice as high as spending on transport, whereas food accounted for just under a third.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Como organizar uma tabela/barras
1. **Overview:** maior × menor + padrão geral (*all categories rose / most…*).
2. **Body 1:** categorias **mais altas** ou **grupo A**.
3. **Body 2:** categorias **mais baixas** ou **grupo B**.
4. **Não liste tudo** — selecione e compare.
### Modelo
> **Table — Percentage of household spending, Country Y, 2020:** Housing 32% · Food 24% · Transport 16% · Leisure 12% · Clothing 6% · Other 10%
> **Overview:** *Overall, housing accounted for the largest share of household spending, while clothing represented the smallest.*
> **Body:** *Housing made up almost a third of expenditure (32%), **followed by** food at just under a quarter (24%). Transport ranked third, at 16%, **which was twice as high as** spending on clothing (6%). Leisure and other expenses **had similar figures**, at 12% and 10% respectively.*

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: comparando
> Spending on **housing** was **twice as high as** spending on **transport**.
> → food / clothing / four times as high as → leisure / food / half as much as → transport / leisure / slightly higher than
### 4.2 Descreva em uma frase (proporção)
1. 51% · 2. 74% · 3. 9% · 4. 33% · 5. 19% · 6. 50.5%
### 4.3 Agrupe
> **Bar chart — Hours of homework per week by country:** Finland 3 · Japan 4 · UK 5 · Brazil 5 · USA 6 · China 14
Qual é o destaque? Como agrupar os outros?
