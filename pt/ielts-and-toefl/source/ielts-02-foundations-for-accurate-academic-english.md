## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** a base de **Grammatical Range and Accuracy**: frase completa (sujeito + verbo), **concordância** e **tempos verbais** usados em Writing e Speaking.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** encontrar e corrigir os erros de base mais comuns de brasileiros em frases sobre dados, opiniões e experiências.
**Tarefa verificável:** corrigir oito frases com erros de base.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Responda com frases completas e no tempo certo:
- What do you do? (presente)
- What did you study at school/university? (passado)
- How long have you lived in your city? (presente perfeito)
- What are you going to do after the IELTS? (futuro)
**Revisão da aula 1:** diga sua meta de band e sua prioridade.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
Verbos muito usados no IELTS (Writing Task 1 e 2):
- **increase / rise** — aumentar · **decrease / fall / drop** — diminuir
- **remain** — permanecer · **reach** — atingir · **account for** — representar (percentual)
- **affect** — afetar · **cause** — causar · **lead to** — levar a · **benefit** — beneficiar
- **argue** — argumentar · **claim** — afirmar · **believe** — acreditar
Substantivos incontáveis frequentes (sem *-s*, sem *a*):
- **information · research · advice · equipment · knowledge · evidence · traffic · pollution · furniture**

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **rise / rice / raise** — *rise* /raɪz/ (som de z) × *rice* /raɪs/
- **fall / full / fool** · **remain / remind**
- Final -s: **increases** (/ɪz/) · **falls** (/z/) · **affects** (/s/)
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> The number of students rises every year, but research shows that traffic remains a problem.

*A) remain || Correto.

= The price reached a peak in 2020.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### 1. Toda frase tem sujeito + verbo
- ✘ *Is important to study.* → ✔ ***It** is important to study.*
- ✘ *Exist many problems.* → ✔ ***There are** many problems.*
- ✘ *In my city have many parks.* → ✔ ***There are** many parks in my city.*
### 2. Concordância
- Sujeito “escondido” atrás de preposição: *The number **of cars** **is** rising.* (o núcleo é *number*)
- *A number of people **are**…* (= muitos) × *The number of people **is**…* (= a quantidade)
- *Everyone/each/every* → singular · *people/police* → plural
### 3. Tempos mais usados no IELTS
- **Presente simples** — fatos gerais (Task 2): *Technology **changes** the way we work.*
- **Passado simples** — dados do passado (Task 1): *Sales **rose** in 2010.*
- **Presente perfeito** — até agora (Task 2/Speaking): *Online learning **has become** popular.*
- **Futuro / previsão** (Task 1 com projeções): *The population **is expected to** reach 10 million.*
### Modelo — fale junto
> In my country, the number of people who work from home has increased a lot since 2020. There are many advantages, but research shows that some workers feel isolated. In my opinion, companies should offer a mix of office and home days.

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: concordância e tempo
> The number of **cars** **is rising**.
> → tourists / increased (2015–2020) → students / has doubled (since 2000) → workers / is expected to fall (by 2030)
> **It is** important **to protect the environment**.
> → necessary / reduce traffic → essential / invest in education → difficult / find affordable housing
### 4.2 Conserte (oral)
1. Is very difficult find a job nowadays.
2. The number of tourists have increased.
3. People is more connected today.
4. In 2010, the sales increase a lot.
5. Since 2015, the government invested in schools.
6. Exist many solutions for this problem.

:::quiz practice
? w|grammar|______ is very difficult to find a job nowadays. (sujeito)
> It
= It is very difficult to find a job nowadays.
? w|grammar|The number of tourists ______ increased. (have/has)
> has
= The number of tourists has increased.
? mc|grammar|Which is correct?
A) People is more connected today. || People = plural.
*B) People are more connected today. || Correto.
? w|grammar|In 2010, sales ______ a lot. (increase — passado)
> increased
= In 2010, sales increased a lot.
? mc|grammar|“Since 2015, the government ______ in schools.”
A) invested || Com “since” use presente perfeito.
*B) has invested || Correto.
C) invests || Não combina com “since”.
? mc|grammar|Which is correct?
*A) There are many solutions to this problem. || Correto.
B) Exist many solutions to this problem. || Falta sujeito.
C) Have many solutions to this problem. || Use “there are”.
? mc|grammar|“A number of people ______ unhappy with the plan.”
*A) are || “A number of” = muitos (plural).
B) is || Seria com “the number of”.
:::

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: 8 frases (15 min)

2. The percentage of women in politics have risen since 1990.
3. In 2005, the factory produce 2,000 cars.
4. Is clear that technology help students.
5. Governments should give more informations to citizens.
6. Everyone in my family like football.
7. Over the last decade, tourism grew significantly in my city.
8. The police is investigating the case.
**Acertos:** __/8
### B. Explique — 4 min
Para 4 correções, diga em inglês: *“It should be… because…”*
### C. Produza — 4 min

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Verbos.** Escreva uma frase com cada verbo-chave sobre dados do seu país (invente números plausíveis só para praticar).
**Dia 2 — Incontáveis.** Escreva 9 frases, uma com cada substantivo incontável da aula.
**Dia 3 — Tempos.** Escreva 3 frases em cada tempo da seção 3.
**Dia 4 — Sombra.** Leia o modelo da seção 3 em voz alta 3 vezes. Grave sua versão sobre outro tema.
**Dia 5 — Speaking.** Grave 1 minuto: *How has your city changed in the last ten years?* (presente perfeito + passado)
**Dia 6 — Writing.** Escreva 100 palavras: *Working from home: advantages and disadvantages.* Revise S-C-T-I.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
