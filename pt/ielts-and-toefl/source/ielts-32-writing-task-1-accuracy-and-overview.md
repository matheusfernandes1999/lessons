## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** os dois problemas que mais travam a nota de Task 1: **overview fraco ou ausente** e **listar todos os números** sem selecionar. Mais: precisão de dados e gramática.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** revisar uma resposta, escrever um overview claro e selecionar os dados mais relevantes.
**Tarefa verificável:** revisar uma resposta e melhorar overview e precisão.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### O que o examinador quer (Task Achievement)
- **Overview claro** das principais tendências/diferenças.
- **Características-chave** selecionadas e ilustradas com dados.
- **Dados precisos** (números, unidades e anos corretos).
- **Sem opinião, sem causas, sem conclusão do tipo “In conclusion, I think…”**
### Expressões de seleção
- *The most noticeable/striking feature is… · By far the highest… · A notable exception is… · In contrast… · Similarly…*
### Precisão
- *approximately / around / just over / just under / roughly* (quando o número não é exato no gráfico)
- *respectively* · *the figure for X* · *the proportion of X*
### Erros comuns de precisão
- unidade errada (*%* × *millions*) · ano errado · *the number of* × *the amount of* · *increase to* × *increase by*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **noticeable** = NO-ti-cea-ble · **striking** = STRI-king · **exception** = ex-CEP-tion · **respectively** = re-SPEC-tive-ly
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> The most striking feature is that sales in Japan were by far the highest, with one notable exception in 2015.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Técnica do “marca-texto”
Antes de escrever, marque no gráfico apenas:
1. o **maior** e o **menor** · 2. a **maior mudança** · 3. **semelhanças** · 4. **exceções**. Só isso entra nos parágrafos.
### Resposta fraca × revisada
> **Dados — Internet users (% of population):** UK 2000: 27 → 2020: 95 · Brazil 2000: 3 → 2020: 81 · India 2000: 1 → 2020: 43
> **Fraca:**
> *The graph shows internet users. In 2000 the UK was 27%, Brazil was 3% and India was 1%. In 2005 the UK was 70%, Brazil was 21% and India was 2%. In 2010 the UK was 85%, Brazil 41%, India 7%. In 2015 the UK was 92%, Brazil 59%, India 27%. In 2020 the UK was 95%, Brazil 81% and India 43%. In conclusion, I think the internet is very important.*
> **Problemas:** introdução copiada · sem overview · lista ano a ano · *the UK was 27%* (impreciso) · opinião no fim.
> **Revisada:**
> *The line graph compares the proportion of people using the internet in the UK, Brazil and India between 2000 and 2020.*
> *Overall, internet use rose in all three countries, with the UK consistently recording the highest figures. The most striking growth occurred in Brazil.*
> *In 2000, just over a quarter of the UK population (27%) used the internet, compared with only 3% in Brazil and 1% in India. Over the next two decades, the UK figure climbed to almost universal access, at 95%.*
> *Brazil saw the most dramatic increase, rising from 3% to 81% by 2020. India also grew significantly, although its figure remained the lowest, reaching just under half the population (43%) at the end of the period.*

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: transformando frases imprecisas
> *The UK was 27%.* → *The figure for the UK was 27%.* / *27% of the UK population used the internet.*
> 1. Brazil was 81% in 2020. 2. Coffee was the most in 2015. 3. Cars were 55%. 4. Women were more than men.
### 4.2 Escolha o overview
> **Dados — CO₂ emissions per person (tonnes), 1990 and 2020:** USA 19 → 14 · Germany 12 → 8 · China 2 → 8 · India 0.7 → 1.8
a) *Overall, the USA had the highest emissions and India had the lowest.* b) *Overall, emissions per person fell in the two Western countries but rose in China and India, although the USA remained the highest emitter throughout.*
### 4.3 Marca-texto
Para os dados de CO₂, diga: maior, menor, maior mudança, semelhança (2020: Germany = China!).
