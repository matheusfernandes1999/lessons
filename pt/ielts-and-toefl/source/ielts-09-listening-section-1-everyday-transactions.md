## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** a **Part 1** do Listening: uma conversa do dia a dia (reserva de hotel, matrícula em curso, aluguel, consulta). Você completa um **formulário** com nomes, números, datas, preços e endereços.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** prever o tipo de informação de cada campo e pegar a resposta mesmo quando a pessoa **se corrige**.
**Tarefa verificável:** preencher dez campos a partir de diálogo.
**Professor:** leia os diálogos 🎧 com duas vozes diferentes, ritmo natural, **uma vez só**.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Faça o papel de atendente; o aluno responde com dados (inventados ou reais):
- *Can I have your full name, please?* · *How do you spell that?* · *What's your postcode?* · *What's a good contact number?* · *When would you like to come in?*
**Revisão da aula 8:** diga suas 3 intervenções prioritárias.

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Situações e palavras típicas
- **booking / reservation** · **deposit** — sinal · **fee / charge** · **discount** · **refund**
- **date of birth** · **postcode / zip code** · **address** · **contact number** · **email address**
- **single / double room** · **ground floor** · **parking space** · **pick-up time**
- **membership** · **monthly / annual** · **beginner / intermediate / advanced**
### Como as pessoas dizem números e símbolos
- **0** = *oh* ou *zero* · **double 7** = 77 · **triple 3** = 333
- **@** = *at* · **.** = *dot* · **_** = *underscore* · **-** = *hyphen/dash*
- **£15.50** = *fifteen pounds fifty* · **£1,200** = *one thousand two hundred pounds* / *twelve hundred pounds*
- **Datas:** *the third of May* / *May the third* → escreva **3 May** ou **May 3**

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **fifteen / fifty · eighteen / eighty** — tônica no fim × no começo
- **flat / flight · road / rowed · lane / line**
- Endereços: *Flat 4B, 27 Elm Road* → *flat four B, twenty-seven Elm Road*
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> My postcode is SW19 2AB, and my number is oh seven nine double four, three one eight, two two six.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Estratégia em 4 passos
1. **Leia a instrução:** *ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER* → não escreva duas palavras!
2. **Preveja** cada lacuna: nome? número? dia? preço? substantivo?
3. **Ouça a correção:** *“It's on the 14th… oh, sorry, the 15th.”* → **15th**.
4. **Ortografia:** nomes soletrados devem estar **perfeitos**; plural com *-s* conta!
### Armadilhas clássicas da Part 1
- **Correção:** *“Tuesday — no, actually, Thursday is better.”*
- **Opção rejeitada:** *“The double room is £90, but I'll take the single, which is £65.”*
- **Distração de número:** *“It used to be £40, but now it's £45.”*
### Modelo
🎧 > **Receptionist:** And what date would you like to arrive? **Guest:** The 12th of June… actually, can we make it the 13th? My flight changed. **Receptionist:** Sure. A double room is £120 a night, or there's a standard single for £85. **Guest:** Just the single, please.
→ **Arrival:** 13 June · **Room:** single · **Price:** £85

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: 10 campos (15 min)
🎧 **Professor:** leia o diálogo uma vez. Antes, dê 1 minuto para o aluno ler o formulário e prever.
**Instrução:** *Write ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER for each answer.*
> **Hillside Language School — Enrolment Form**
> Name: Ana (1) ______ · Nationality: (2) ______ · Date of birth: (3) ______ · Course: (4) ______ English · Level: (5) ______ · Start date: (6) ______ · Classes per week: (7) ______ · Fee per week: £(8) ______ · Accommodation: with a (9) ______ · Contact email: ana.(10) ______@webmail.com
🎧 Diálogo:
> **Receptionist:** Good morning, Hillside Language School. How can I help?
> **Ana:** Hi, I'd like to enrol on one of your courses.
> **Receptionist:** Lovely. Can I have your name?
> **Ana:** Ana Pereira — that's P-E-R-E-I-R-A.
> **Receptionist:** And your nationality?
> **Ana:** I'm Brazilian.
> **Receptionist:** Date of birth?
> **Ana:** The 22nd of August, 1998.
> **Receptionist:** Which course are you interested in — General English, Business English or Academic English?
> **Ana:** I was thinking of Business English, but actually I need it for university, so Academic, please.
> **Receptionist:** Fine. We'll do a test on your first day, but what level do you think you are?
> **Ana:** Probably intermediate.
> **Receptionist:** The next course starts on the 4th of September… sorry, no — the 11th. The 4th is full.
> **Ana:** The 11th is fine.
> **Receptionist:** You'll have 15 classes a week — three each morning, Monday to Friday. The fee is £240 a week, but students who book more than four weeks get a discount, so it'd be £215.
> **Ana:** I'm booking six weeks, so that's great.
> **Receptionist:** Do you need accommodation? We can offer a room in a student residence or with a host family.
> **Ana:** A family, please. I want to practise my English at home.
> **Receptionist:** Perfect. And finally, an email address?
> **Ana:** It's ana dot pereira98 at webmail dot com.
**Acertos:** __/10
**Fala (4 min):** usando o formulário, o aluno faz o papel de Ana e responde às perguntas do professor sem olhar o diálogo.

Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
