## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** *Listen to a Conversation*: diálogos entre aluno e funcionário, professor ou colega. Quase sempre seguem o roteiro **problema → opções → solução → próximo passo**.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** ouvir uma conversa de 1–2 minutos e relatar oralmente o problema, a solução e o que a pessoa vai fazer depois.
**Tarefa verificável:** relatar problema, solução e próxima etapa de dois diálogos.
**Professor:** os diálogos 🎧 podem ser lidos em voz alta mudando a voz para cada personagem.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- Tell me about a problem you had to solve recently — at work, school or home.
- Who helped you? What did you do in the end?
**Frases úteis:** *The problem was that…* · *They suggested…* · *In the end, I decided to…* · *The next step is to…*
**Troque e fale:**
> The problem was that **my internet stopped working**, so I **called the company**.
> → I lost my card / blocked it online → my flight was canceled / rebooked another one → I missed a class / asked a classmate for notes
**Revisão da aula 18:** responda: *“Would you mind helping me?”* e *“Why don't we start now?”*

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
Linguagem de conversa sobre problemas:
- **What seems to be the problem?** — Qual é o problema?
- **I'm having trouble with…** — Estou com dificuldade em…
- **It turns out (that)…** — Acontece que… / Descobri que…
- **Here's the thing:…** — A questão é a seguinte:…
- **option / alternative** — opção · **workaround** — solução alternativa
- **waive (a fee)** — dispensar (uma taxa) · **exception** — exceção
- **paperwork** — papelada · **approval** — aprovação · **sign off on** — aprovar/assinar
- **in the meantime** — enquanto isso · **get back to (someone)** — retornar (para alguém)
- **That makes sense.** — Faz sentido. · **That works for me.** — Pra mim funciona.

### Pronúncia: reações naturais
Ouça e repita com a entonação certa:
- *Oh, I see.* ↘ · *Really?* ↗ · *That makes sense.* ↘ · *Hmm, I'm not sure about that.* (hesitação)
Ouça, compare e repita: **waive / wave** (iguais!) · **trouble / travel** · **approval / approve / a proof**
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> It turns out the fee can be waived if your advisor signs off on it.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### O que anotar (só 4 linhas!)
- **P:** problema · **O:** opções sugeridas · **S:** solução escolhida · **N:** próximo passo
### Perguntas típicas da prova
*Why does the student go to the office? · What does the man suggest? · What will the woman probably do next? · Why does the professor mention X?*
### Modelo
🎧 > **Student:** Hi, I'm trying to register for Statistics 201, but the system says I haven't completed the prerequisite.
> **Advisor:** Let me check… It turns out your Statistics 101 credit from your previous college hasn't been transferred yet.
> **Student:** Oh, I sent my transcript weeks ago!
> **Advisor:** Sometimes it takes a while. Here's what we can do: I'll give you a temporary permission code so you can register now. In the meantime, contact the registrar's office to make sure the transfer is processed.
> **Student:** That works for me. I'll call them this afternoon.
- **P:** não consegue se matricular em Statistics 201 — pré-requisito não aparece.
- **O/S:** crédito ainda não transferido → código de permissão temporário.
- **N:** ligar para a secretaria (registrar) à tarde.

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: relatando a conversa
> The student **couldn't register for a class** because **her credit hadn't been transferred**. The advisor **gave her a temporary code**, and next she's going to **call the registrar's office**.
O professor dá novos dados; o aluno fala a frase completa:
> → couldn't get a refund / missed the deadline / waived the late fee / fill out a form
> → couldn't find a book / it was checked out / put it on hold / come back on Monday

### 4.2 Diálogo curto 2
🎧 > **Professor:** Hi Daniel, you wanted to talk about the paper?
> **Student:** Yes. I chose the topic of renewable energy, but there's too much information. I don't know where to start.
> **Professor:** That's common. The topic is too broad. Why don't you focus on one type of energy in one country — say, wind power in Brazil?
> **Student:** That makes sense. Should I change my outline, then?
> **Professor:** Yes. Send me a new outline by Friday, and I'll give you feedback over the weekend.
P: ______ · S: ______ · N: ______

### 4.3 Previsão: o que acontece depois?
O professor lê só a primeira fala; o aluno prevê o problema e a solução provável.
1. “Hi, I live in Maple Hall, and my roommate and I have very different schedules…”
2. “Excuse me, I bought this textbook yesterday, but it's the wrong edition.”
3. “Professor, I'm going to miss next week's exam because of a family wedding.”

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: 2 diálogos (15 min)
**Regras:** 🎧 o professor lê cada diálogo **uma vez**. O aluno anota só P/O/S/N. Depois relata oralmente (1 min) e responde às perguntas.
### Diálogo A
> **Student:** Hi, I got an email saying my library account is blocked. What's going on?
> **Librarian:** Let me see… You have a book that's three weeks overdue — *Introduction to Psychology*.
> **Student:** Oh no. I think I lent it to a friend and forgot.
> **Librarian:** Well, the fine is $15 right now. Until you return the book or pay for it, you can't borrow anything else.
> **Student:** I really need to check out two books for a paper due Monday. Is there anything I can do?
> **Librarian:** If you return the book today, I can reduce the fine to $5. And you could use the reference copies of your books here in the library in the meantime.
> **Student:** OK, I'll get the book from my friend right after class and bring it back this afternoon.
1. Why is the account blocked? 2. What happens if he returns the book today? 3. What can he do in the meantime? 4. What will he do next?
### Diálogo B
> **Student:** Hi, I'm a sophomore, and I'd like to apply for the summer research program in the biology lab.
> **Coordinator:** Great. The deadline was actually last Friday, though.
> **Student:** Oh, I didn't know. I was sick last week.
> **Coordinator:** I see. Normally we don't accept late applications, but if you bring a doctor's note, I can ask the committee to make an exception. You'd also need a recommendation letter from a professor.
> **Student:** I can ask Professor Kim — I took her genetics class. When would I need everything?
> **Coordinator:** By Wednesday at the latest. The committee meets on Thursday.
> **Student:** Perfect. I'll email Professor Kim right now.
1. What does the student want? 2. What is the problem? 3. What two documents does he need? 4. What will he do next?
**Acertos:** __/8 **Relato oral com P/S/N completos:** A ☐ B ☐

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Vocabulário.** Escreva um mini diálogo de 6 falas usando 6 expressões da aula.
**Dia 2 — Sombra.** Leia o Diálogo A em voz alta fazendo as duas vozes. Grave.
**Dia 3 — Listening real.** Assista a um vídeo de “campus tour” ou “office hours” no YouTube (3–5 min) e anote P/O/S/N de uma situação.
**Dia 4 — Relato.** Grave 1 minuto relatando o Diálogo B sem olhar.
**Dia 5 — Speaking.** Grave 1 minuto: *Describe a problem you solved with someone's help.* Use *it turns out, in the meantime, the next step*.
**Dia 6 — Writing.** Escreva um diálogo de 8 falas para a situação 4.3 n.º 2 (livro errado).
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
