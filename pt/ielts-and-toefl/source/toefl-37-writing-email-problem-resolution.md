## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** e-mails para **resolver problemas**: explicar o que aconteceu, o que você **já tentou** e o que **precisa** que a pessoa faça.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** escrever um e-mail objetivo, sem drama, com sequência clara (problema → tentativas → pedido).
**Tarefa verificável:** produzir e-mail claro sobre dificuldade com inscrição.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- Tell me about a problem you had with a company, a website or an office. What did you do?
- Is it better to call or to write an email when there's a problem? Why?
**Frases úteis:** *I tried to… but…* · *I've already…* · *It still doesn't work.* · *What I need is…*
**Revisão da aula 36:** faça um follow-up educado oral: *seu professor não respondeu sobre a carta de recomendação.*

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Descrever o problema
- *I'm having trouble + -ing…* · *I'm unable to…* · *I keep getting an error message that says…*
- *The system won't let me…* · *It seems that…* · *There appears to be a mistake in…*
### Ações já tomadas (presente perfeito e passado)
- *I've already tried + -ing…* · *I've checked…* · *I contacted… on Monday, but…* · *I followed the instructions on…*
### Pedido objetivo
- *Could you please look into this?* · *I'd appreciate it if you could…* · *Would it be possible to…?* · *What should I do next?*
### Urgência sem drama
- *The deadline is…, so I'd be grateful for a quick reply.* · *Since registration closes on…*
### Vocabulário de inscrição/sistemas
- **enrollment / registration** · **waitlist** · **prerequisite** · **error message** · **portal** · **log in** · **hold (on an account)** — bloqueio · **override** — liberação manual · **screenshot**

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **error / era / arrow** · **log in / login** · **hold / old / hole**
- **prerequisite** = pre-REQ-ui-site · **screenshot** = SCREEN-shot
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> I've already tried logging in twice, but I keep getting an error message.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Estrutura P-T-P (+ urgência)
1. **Contexto:** quem é você (nome, curso, ID se for relevante).
2. **Problema:** o que aconteceu — fatos, mensagem de erro, datas.
3. **Tentativas:** o que você já fez (mostra que você não está pedindo à toa).
4. **Pedido:** o que você precisa, de forma direta e educada.
5. **Urgência (se houver)** + agradecimento.
### Modelo
> Dear Registrar's Office,
> My name is Rafael Costa (student ID 20231458), and I'm a second-year economics major. I'm writing because I'm unable to register for ECON 310, Intermediate Microeconomics.
> When I try to add the course in the student portal, I get an error message that says “Prerequisite not met.” However, I completed ECON 210 last spring with a B+. I've already checked my transcript online, and the course appears there. I also contacted the Economics Department on Monday, and they suggested that I write to you.
> Could you please look into this and, if possible, add an override so that I can register? Since registration closes this Friday, I'd be grateful for a quick reply. I've attached a screenshot of the error.
> Thank you for your help.
> Best regards,
> Rafael Costa

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: tentativas
> I've already **checked my transcript**, but **the course doesn't appear**.
> → cleared my browser cache / the page still freezes → called the help desk / nobody answered → tried a different computer / I get the same error → paid the fee / my account still shows a hold

### 4.2 Organize o e-mail (numere as frases na ordem certa)
a) Could you please check if my payment was received?
b) I paid the housing deposit online on June 2.
c) Dear Housing Office,
d) However, my account still says “Deposit pending.”
e) My name is Lia Moura, and I've been offered a room in Oak Hall.
f) I've attached the bank receipt.
g) Best regards, Lia Moura
h) I called the office yesterday, but the line was busy.

### 4.3 Sem drama
Reescreva de forma objetiva:
> “This is a DISASTER!!! Your system is TERRIBLE and I will lose my semester because of YOU!!!”

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Frases.** Escreva 4 frases de problema, 4 de tentativas e 4 de pedido.
**Dia 2 — Ordem.** Escreva o e-mail da Lia (4.2) na ordem certa e acrescente uma frase de urgência.
**Dia 3 — E-mail 1.** *Your grade on the online portal is different from the one your professor told you in class. Write to the professor.* 12 minutos.
**Dia 4 — E-mail 2.** *You were charged twice for the meal plan. Write to Student Accounts.* 12 minutos.
**Dia 5 — Speaking.** Grave 1 minuto explicando oralmente o problema do Rafael como se estivesse ao telefone.
**Dia 6 — Revisão.** Revise os e-mails dos dias 3–4 com o checklist da seção 6.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
