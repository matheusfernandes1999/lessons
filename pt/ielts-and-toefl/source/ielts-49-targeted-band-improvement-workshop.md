## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** **oficina** para atacar as **2 lacunas prioritárias** do simulado (aula 48). O professor escolhe 2 módulos abaixo; para cada um: explicação rápida → treino → **tarefa nova equivalente** → comparação com o simulado.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** mostrar uma diferença observável (acertos, critério, tempo) nos seus 2 pontos fracos.
**Tarefa verificável:** executar novo conjunto e documentar diferenças observáveis.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- What were your two weakest areas in the mock test?
- What did you do this week to improve them?
**Troque e fale:**
> In the mock test, I lost marks in **Writing Task 2** because **my paragraphs weren't developed enough**. Today I want to **practise extending my ideas**.
> → Listening Part 1 / I made spelling mistakes / do spelling dictations → Reading / I confused False and Not Given / compare statements more carefully → Speaking Part 3 / my answers were too short / use O-R-E

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
Linguagem de progresso:
- *I've improved my… · I'm still struggling with… · Compared with the mock test, … · My score went up from… to… · I managed to… · I no longer…*
- **band descriptor** · **criterion** · **target** · **gap** — lacuna · **consistent** · **noticeable improvement**

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **noticeable** · **consistently** · **managed** /ˈmænɪdʒd/
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Compared with the mock test, I managed to write a clearer overview and my grammar was more consistent.

## 3. Módulos de oficina (escolha 2 — 12 min cada)
### Módulo A — Writing Task 1: overview e seleção
**Explicação:** overview = 2 características principais **sem números**; corpo = dados **agrupados**.
**Treino:** escreva overviews para: *(a) Unemployment rate, 4 countries, 2010→2020: A 8→5, B 6→4, C 10→12, D 5→5* · *(b) Pie: household waste — food 40%, paper 25%, plastic 20%, glass 10%, other 5%*
**Tarefa nova:** introdução + overview + 1 body para (a) em 8 min.
### Módulo B — Writing Task 2: desenvolvimento (PEEL + cadeia)
**Explicação:** Point → Explain (2 ligações: *which means… as a result…*) → Example → Link.
**Treino:** desenvolva em 4–5 frases: *Working from home reduces stress.*
**Tarefa nova:** 1 parágrafo PEEL para *Should governments tax unhealthy food?* em 8 min.
### Módulo C — Speaking Part 3: profundidade e nuance
**Explicação:** O-R-E + *Having said that / It depends on…*
**Treino:** 3 respostas de 45 s: *Why do people buy things they don't need? · Will shops disappear? · Is advertising harmful to children?*
**Tarefa nova:** 2 perguntas novas do professor; compare tamanho e estrutura com a aula 48.
### Módulo D — Speaking: gramática variada
**Explicação:** use 1 perfeito, 1 condicional, 1 relativa por resposta longa.
**Treino:** fale *Describe a city you would like to visit* com as 3 estruturas marcadas.
**Tarefa nova:** Part 2 nova (1+2 min): *Describe a time you changed your mind about something.*
### Módulo E — Listening: ortografia, números e distratores
**Treino:** ditado de 10 itens (nomes soletrados, datas, preços, códigos) + 3 falas com correção (*“Tuesday — no, Thursday”*).
**Tarefa nova:** 🎧 *“The tour leaves from Gate B… sorry, Gate D, at quarter past nine on the 21st. Tickets are £18.50 for adults; children under twelve pay half. Please bring a waterproof jacket. Our guide's surname is Fairweather — F-A-I-R-W-E-A-T-H-E-R.”* → Gate · Time · Date · Adult price · Child price · Bring · Guide
### Módulo F — Reading: F × NG
**Explicação:** False = o texto **contradiz**; NG = o texto **não fala**.
**Treino + tarefa nova:** > *The library opened in 1998 and now holds over 200,000 books. It is open every day except Sunday. Membership is free for residents, but visitors pay a small annual fee.* — 1 The library opened in the 1990s. 2 It is open seven days a week. 3 Visitors cannot borrow books. 4 It is the largest library in the city.

:::quiz practice
? mc|comprehension|Module A (a): best overview?
*A) Overall, unemployment fell in two countries, rose in one and stayed the same in another, with C having the highest rate in 2020. || Correto.
B) Overall, A was 8 and then 5. || Números/detalhe.
? w|comprehension|Module E: Gate ______ (letra)
> D
= Gate D (corrigido de B)
? w|comprehension|Module E: adult price £______ (com centavos, ex.: 10.50)
> 18.50
= £18.50
? w|comprehension|Module E: guide's surname: ______
> Fairweather
= Fairweather
? tfng|comprehension|The library is open seven days a week.
> F
= “every day except Sunday”.
? tfng|comprehension|It is the largest library in the city.
> NG
= O texto não compara com outras bibliotecas.
:::
