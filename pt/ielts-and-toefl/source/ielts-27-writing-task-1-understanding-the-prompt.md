## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** antes de escrever a Task 1, você precisa **ler o gráfico como um analista**: o que foi medido, em que **unidade**, em que **período** e quais são as **principais tendências**.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** escrever uma **introdução parafraseada** e uma **visão geral (overview)** de 2 frases para qualquer gráfico.
**Tarefa verificável:** produzir um plano com visão geral de gráfico.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- Do you read charts and graphs at work or in the news?

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Tipos de visual
- **line graph** · **bar chart** · **pie chart** · **table** · **diagram (process)** · **map**
### Verbos de introdução (paráfrase de *shows*)
- *illustrates · compares · gives information about · presents · provides data on*
### Unidades
- **percentage / proportion** (%) · **number of** (contáveis) · **amount of** (incontáveis) · **in millions / in thousands / in tonnes / in US dollars**
### Tempo
- *between 1990 and 2020 · from 1990 to 2020 · over a 30-year period · in 2015 · projected for 2030*
### Overview (visão geral)
- *Overall, … · In general, … · It is clear that … · The most noticeable feature is …*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **illustrate** = IL-lus-trate · **proportion** = pro-POR-tion · **percentage** = per-CEN-tage · **overall** = o-ver-ALL
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> The line graph illustrates the proportion of people who used public transport over a twenty-year period.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Os 5 W's do gráfico (análise em 2 minutos)
1. **What** foi medido? (número de turistas, % de lares…)
2. **Unit?** (%, milhões, toneladas)
3. **Who/Where?** (países, grupos, cidades)
4. **When?** (um ano, um período, projeção)
5. **Main features?** (maior/menor, tendência geral, mudança grande)
### A estrutura da Task 1 (≥150 palavras, ~20 min)
1. **Introdução** (1 frase): paráfrase do enunciado.
2. **Overview** (2 frases): as **2 características mais importantes** — **sem números**.
3. **Body 1** e **Body 2**: detalhes com números, agrupados de forma lógica.
### Modelo
> **Enunciado:** *The graph below shows the percentage of households with internet access in three countries between 2000 and 2020.*
> **Dados:** Country A 40% → 95% · Country B 20% → 80% · Country C 5% → 45%
> **Introdução:** *The line graph illustrates the proportion of homes with access to the internet in three nations over a twenty-year period, from 2000 to 2020.*
> **Overview:** *Overall, internet access rose considerably in all three countries. Country A had the highest figures throughout the period, while Country C remained the lowest.*

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: parafraseando enunciados
> *The chart shows the amount of money spent on food in four countries in 2020.* → *The bar chart compares how much was spent on food in four nations in 2020.*
1. *The table shows the number of students enrolled in five universities in 2010 and 2020.*
2. *The pie charts show the main sources of energy in a country in 1990 and 2020.*
3. *The graph shows the average price of a house in London between 1980 and 2020.*
### 4.2 Escolha as 2 características principais (overview)
> **Dados (bar chart, milhões de visitantes, 2022):** Museum A 6.2 · Museum B 4.8 · Museum C 1.1 · Museum D 0.9
Quais são as 2 coisas mais importantes? (sem números!)
### 4.3 Overview bom × ruim
a) *Overall, Museum A had 6.2 million visitors and Museum D had 0.9 million.* b) *Overall, Museum A was by far the most visited, while Museums C and D attracted far fewer visitors than the other two.*

## Gabarito do professor
- **4.1 (exemplos):** 1 The table compares enrolment figures at five universities in 2010 and 2020. 2 The pie charts illustrate the main energy sources in a country in 1990 and 2020. 3 The line graph presents the average cost of a house in London over a forty-year period.
- **4.2:** A muito à frente dos outros; C e D bem abaixo de A e B.
- **5 (exemplo):** *Intro:* The bar chart compares the proportion of adults who cycled at least once a week in four age groups in 2010 and 2020. *Overview:* Overall, cycling was most popular among the youngest adults in both years, and participation rose in most age groups. The most dramatic change was among people aged 60 and over, whose figure nearly doubled.
