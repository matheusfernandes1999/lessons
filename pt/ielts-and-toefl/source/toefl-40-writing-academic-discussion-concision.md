## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** escrever **com concisão**: cortar frases vazias (*“This is a very interesting and controversial topic”*), fórmulas decoradas e repetições, e trocar por **ideias**.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** revisar um texto longo e deixá-lo mais curto e mais forte.
**Tarefa verificável:** revisar resposta extensa para maior densidade argumentativa.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Diga a mesma ideia com menos palavras:
- *In my personal opinion, I think that…* → ______
- *Due to the fact that…* → ______
- *At this point in time…* → ______
- *It is a well-known fact that everybody knows…* → ______
**Revisão da aula 39:** dê uma razão com R + E + Ex sobre *Should cities invest in bike lanes?*

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Cortes rápidos (prolixo → conciso)
- *in my personal opinion, I think* → **I think**
- *due to the fact that* → **because**
- *at this point in time* → **now**
- *in order to* → **to** (quase sempre)
- *a large number of* → **many**
- *has the ability to* → **can**
- *make a decision* → **decide** · *give an explanation* → **explain**
- *each and every* → **every** · *final outcome* → **outcome** · *future plans* → **plans**
### Frases vazias que devem desaparecer
- *“This is a very interesting/controversial topic.”*
- *“There are many advantages and disadvantages.”*
- *“Everyone has their own opinion.”*
- *“In conclusion, as I said before,…”* (resposta de discussão não precisa de conclusão formal)
### Palavras precisas (no lugar de *good/bad/thing*)
- *good* → **effective, beneficial, affordable, reliable** · *bad* → **harmful, costly, unfair, risky** · *thing* → **factor, issue, benefit, problem**

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **concise** = con-CISE · **concision** = con-CI-sion · **redundant** = re-DUN-dant
- **precise / precious** · **effect / affect**
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Be concise: cut redundant words and choose precise ones.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### 3 perguntas para revisar cada frase
1. **Esta frase traz uma ideia nova?** Se não → corte.
2. **Posso dizer com menos palavras?** → encurte.
3. **Há uma palavra mais precisa?** → troque.
### Modelo — antes (≈ 140 palavras, pouca ideia)
> This is a very interesting and controversial topic, and everyone has their own opinion about it. In my personal opinion, I think that online classes are good. There are many advantages and disadvantages. Due to the fact that online classes are flexible, students have the ability to study at any time. This is a very good thing. Also, online classes are good because they are good for people who work. People who work can study at night. In order to conclude, as I said before, online classes are good and I think universities should offer more online classes in the future.
### Depois (≈ 85 palavras, mais ideias)
> I think universities should offer more online classes. First, they make higher education accessible to people who work full-time: a student with a day job can watch recorded lectures at night. Second, they cut costs for students, who save on transportation and housing near campus. I agree with Ana that online students may feel isolated, but short live group sessions can solve this problem without losing flexibility.
**O que mudou?** cortou 4 frases vazias · trocou *good* por ideias concretas · acrescentou 1 razão nova e 1 contraponto.

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale: palavra precisa
> Online classes are **good** → Online classes are **flexible / affordable / accessible**.
> Pollution is **bad** → harmful / costly / dangerous
> The **thing** is the price → The **problem/issue/main factor** is the price.
> Phones are **good** for learning → useful / effective / convenient

### 4.2 Corte e encurte (escrito)
1. In my personal opinion, I believe that students should do sports.
2. At this point in time, a large number of people use apps in order to learn languages.
3. Each and every student has the ability to improve.
4. The final outcome of the experiment was positive and good.
5. It is a well-known fact that everyone knows that exercise is healthy.

### 4.3 Uma ideia por frase
Divida esta frase longa em 2–3 frases claras:
> *Because many students work and also they have families and also university is far from their houses and the buses are bad, online classes are a good option that many universities should offer more because it is the future.*

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: revisão (15 min)
### A. Revise este texto — 10 min
Corte frases vazias, encurte expressões, troque palavras vagas e acrescente **uma** ideia concreta. Meta: 100–130 palavras.
> **Pergunta:** *Should companies allow employees to work from home permanently?*
> **Texto original (≈ 150 palavras):**
> Working from home is a very interesting topic that a lot of people are discussing nowadays, and there are many different opinions about it. In my personal opinion, I think that companies should allow it. Working from home is good. It is good because people don't need to go to the office. Due to the fact that they don't go to the office, they have the ability to save time. Saving time is a very good thing for everybody. Also, working from home is good for the environment. It is good for the environment because there are fewer cars. Of course, there are also some disadvantages of working from home, like every situation in life has advantages and disadvantages. In order to conclude, as I said before, working from home is good and companies should allow it in the future because it is good.
