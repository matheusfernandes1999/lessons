## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** **registro** — no Writing você escreve em linguagem **acadêmica/neutra**; no Speaking você pode ser mais **conversacional**. E **coesão**: ligar as ideias com conectores e referências.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** transformar um texto informal em acadêmico e conectar frases de forma natural.
**Tarefa verificável:** editar um parágrafo inadequadamente informal.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
Como você diria isso para um amigo? E em um ensaio?
- *Lots of kids are on their phones all the time.* → (ensaio) ______
- *Prices went up a lot.* → (ensaio) ______
- *I think it's a really bad idea.* → (ensaio) ______
**Revisão da aula 5:** junte com *although*: *It is expensive. It is popular.*

## 2. Vocabulário-chave (4 min)
### Informal → acadêmico
- *lots of / a lot of* → **many / a large number of / a great deal of**
- *kids* → **children / young people** · *stuff / things* → **items / aspects / factors**
- *get* → **obtain / receive / become** · *go up / go down* → **increase / decrease**
- *really / very* → **extremely / highly / significantly** (ou corte)
- *bad / good* → **harmful / beneficial / negative / positive**
- *a bit* → **slightly** · *nowadays* → **currently / at present** (nowadays é aceitável)
- *find out* → **discover** · *look into* → **investigate** · *put off* → **postpone**
### Evite no Writing
- Contrações: *don't → do not · it's → it is · can't → cannot*
- Perguntas retóricas e exclamações (*Isn't it terrible?!*)
- *etc.*, *and so on* · gírias · *you* genérico → **people / individuals / one**
### Coesão
- **Conectores:** *however, moreover, as a result, for example, in contrast, similarly*
- **Referência:** *this, these, it, they, such* → evita repetir palavras
- **Substituição:** *the former / the latter* · *this trend / this problem / this approach*

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **significantly** · **beneficial** (be-ne-FI-cial) · **individuals** (in-di-VI-du-als) · **moreover** (mor-O-ver)
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> Moreover, this approach is highly beneficial for individuals and society.

## 3. Explicação e modelo (4 min)
### Os 3 passos para “academizar” um texto
1. **Troque** palavras informais (lista acima).
2. **Retire** contrações, exclamações, perguntas e “you”.
3. **Conecte** frases com conectores e referências (*this trend, such measures*).
### Modelo
**Informal:**
> Lots of people don't do any exercise nowadays. It's really bad! You get fat and sick. Kids are always on their phones and stuff. The government should do something.
**Acadêmico:**
> At present, a large number of people do not exercise regularly. **This** lack of physical activity is harmful, **as** it can lead to obesity and various diseases. **Moreover**, many children spend most of their free time on electronic devices. **For this reason**, governments should take measures to promote active lifestyles.
Leiam juntos e marquem: palavras trocadas · contrações retiradas · conectores · referências.

## 4. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 4.1 Troque e fale (acadêmico)
> **Lots of** people **don't** exercise. → **Many** people **do not** exercise regularly.
> → Kids get loads of homework → Prices went down a bit → Phones are really bad for sleep → Lots of stuff is thrown away
### 4.2 Coesão: substitua a repetição
1. *Many cities have pollution problems. Pollution problems affect health.* → *…**This issue**…*
2. *Some people prefer trains. Other people prefer buses. Trains are faster, but buses are cheaper.* → *…**the former**… **the latter**…*
3. *The government introduced a tax. The tax reduced smoking.* → *…**This measure**…*
### 4.3 Speaking × Writing
O professor diz uma frase acadêmica; o aluno diz a versão para **Speaking Part 1** (natural, conversacional).
> *A large number of individuals consider exercise beneficial.* → *Loads of people think exercise is great.* (ok no Speaking!)

## 5. Tarefa cronometrada: edite o parágrafo (15 min)
### A. Edite — 9 min
Reescreva em registro acadêmico, com coesão (meta: 80–100 palavras).
> I think tourism is kind of good and bad. Lots of tourists come to my city every year and they spend loads of money, which is great for shops and stuff. But they also make the streets really crowded and dirty! You can't walk anywhere in summer. And prices go up a lot, so locals can't afford things. The government should do something about it, like making tourists pay a tax or whatever.
> ______
### B. Leia e compare — 3 min
O aluno lê. O professor verifica: sem contrações? sem *you*? conectores? referências?
### C. Fala — 3 min
Responda à mesma pergunta **oralmente**, em estilo Speaking (natural): *Is tourism good for your city?* (45 s)

## 7. Homework da semana
Estude **15–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Lista.** Crie sua tabela informal → acadêmico com 20 itens.
**Dia 2 — Contrações.** Reescreva um parágrafo de um blog em inglês sem contrações e com vocabulário acadêmico.
**Dia 3 — Coesão.** Escreva 6 pares de frases usando *this + substantivo* (*this trend, this problem, this measure*).
**Dia 4 — Speaking × Writing.** Escreva 3 frases acadêmicas e diga a versão conversacional em voz alta (grave).
**Dia 5 — Writing.** *Some people think that teenagers should not use social media. Do you agree?* 120 palavras, registro acadêmico.
**Dia 6 — Revisão.** Revise o texto do dia 5 procurando informalidades.

## Gabarito do professor
- **Warm-up:** Many young people spend a great deal of time on their phones. · Prices increased significantly. · In my view, this is a harmful idea/This idea has several drawbacks.
- **5A (exemplo):** *Tourism has both advantages and disadvantages for my city. Every year, a large number of visitors spend considerable amounts of money, which benefits local businesses. However, tourists also make the streets crowded and dirty, particularly in summer. Moreover, this demand causes prices to rise, so many residents can no longer afford basic goods and services. To address these problems, the government could introduce a tourist tax and invest the revenue in public services.*
