## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** um **mini simulado** com as 4 seções para ter um **ponto de partida** (baseline) e escolher **3 intervenções** prioritárias. Atenção: o resultado é uma **estimativa de treino**, não uma promessa de band.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** dizer em inglês seus pontos fortes, seus pontos fracos e as 3 ações das próximas semanas.
**Tarefa verificável:** completar mini simulado e priorizar três intervenções.

## 1. Warm-up (3 min)
- How do you feel about taking a mini test today?
- What do you expect to be your best section?
**Troque e fale:**
> I expect **reading** to be my best section, but I'm worried about **writing**.
> → listening / speaking → speaking / reading → writing / listening

### B. Reading (9 min) — True / False / Not Given + completar
> **The Return of Night Trains**
> In the 2000s, many European night-train services were cancelled because cheap flights had become more popular. Recently, however, overnight trains have made a comeback. In 2016, Austria's national railway company took over several routes that German operators had abandoned, and passenger numbers rose steadily. Supporters argue that night trains produce far fewer carbon emissions per passenger than planes, and they save travellers the cost of a hotel. Nevertheless, operators face challenges: sleeping cars are expensive to build, and night trains can carry fewer passengers than day trains because beds take up more space.
7. Night trains became less popular partly because of cheap flights. (T/F/NG)
8. Austria's railway company created all its night routes from scratch. (T/F/NG)
9. Most night-train passengers are business travellers. (T/F/NG)
10. Night trains carry fewer passengers than day trains because beds need more ______. (ONE WORD)
11. One cost advantage for travellers is not paying for a ______. (ONE WORD)
**Acertos:** __/5

### D. Speaking (7 min)
- **Part 1 (1 min):** *Do you like travelling? · How do you usually travel long distances?*
- **Part 2 (1 min de preparo + 1–2 min):** *Describe a journey you remember well. You should say: where you went, how you travelled, who you were with, and explain why you remember it.*
