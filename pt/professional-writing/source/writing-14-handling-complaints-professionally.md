## Objetivo da aula
**Foco:** responder a **reclamações** (*complaints*) de clientes: **agradecer**, mostrar **empatia**, **explicar** brevemente, oferecer **solução** com **prazo** — sem discutir nem prometer o que não pode cumprir.
**Ao final da aula você consegue:** escrever uma resposta profissional e acalmar o cliente por telefone.
**Tarefa verificável:** responder por escrito a uma reclamação fictícia e conduzir a ligação de acompanhamento.

## 1. Revisão e aquecimento (5 min)
- Revisão da aula 13: corrija uma data errada em uma frase.
- Have you ever complained to a company? How did they reply?
- What makes a complaint response good or bad?

## 2. Apresentação e exemplos (10 min)
### Estrutura (THANKS)
1. **Thank:** *Thank you for contacting us / for letting us know.*
2. **Empathise:** *I'm sorry to hear that your order arrived damaged. I understand how frustrating this must be.*
3. **Explain (breve):** *It seems the package was damaged during delivery.*
4. **Solve:** *We'll send a replacement today, free of charge.*
5. **Timeline:** *You should receive it by Friday 7 June.*
6. **Close:** *If there's anything else we can do, please let me know.*
### Reclamação (exemplo)
> *I ordered a coffee machine (order #3381) two weeks ago. It arrived yesterday with a broken lid. This is the second time this has happened. I'm very disappointed.*
### Resposta modelo
> *Dear Ms Taylor, Thank you for letting us know, and I'm very sorry to hear that your coffee machine arrived damaged — especially as this has happened before. I understand how disappointing this must be. We'll send you a replacement today by express delivery, free of charge, and you should receive it by Friday 7 June. You don't need to return the damaged item. I've also asked our delivery team to review how this product is packed. If there's anything else I can do, please let me know. Kind regards, Ana Silva, Customer Service*
### Linguagem: empatia sem admitir tudo
- *I'm sorry to hear that…* · *I understand your frustration.* · *I can see why this is disappointing.*
### Evite
- *You must have done something wrong.* · *That's not our policy.* (sem alternativa) · *We will never make a mistake again.* (promessa impossível) · respostas genéricas copiadas

### Pronúncia: ouça, compare e repita
- **complaint** = kəm-PLAYNT · **frustrating** = frus-TRAY-ting · **replacement** = ri-PLAYS-mənt · **disappointed** = dis-ə-POIN-tid
**Frase para repetir 3 vezes:**
> I'm very sorry to hear that your order arrived damaged. We'll send a replacement today.

## 3. Prática guiada (10 min)
### 3.1 Identifique os passos na resposta modelo
> Thank · Empathise · Explain · Solve · Timeline · Close — sublinhe cada um.
### 3.2 Reescreva com empatia
1. *Your complaint has been received.* · 2. *This is not our fault; the courier lost it.* · 3. *We can't refund you.* (há a opção de troca)
### 3.3 Troque e fale: empatia + solução
> **I'm sorry to hear that** your order arrived late. **We'll** refund the delivery fee.
> → the wrong size arrived / exchange it for free → the app isn't working / call you today to fix it → you were charged twice / refund the extra payment within 3 working days

## 4. Tarefa comunicativa (15 min)
**Reclamação fictícia (leia):**
> *Hi, I booked a double room at the Riverside Hotel for 2 nights (booking ref. RH2209). When I arrived, there was no record of my booking and I had to wait an hour for another room, which was smaller. I'm really unhappy. — Mark Evans*
**Escreva (8 min):** a resposta (90–120 palavras) com os 6 passos. Solução sugerida: reembolso de uma noite + upgrade na próxima estadia. Prazo do reembolso: 5 dias úteis.
**Ligação (5 min):** Mark (professor) liga ainda irritado. O aluno mantém a calma, mostra empatia, confirma a solução e o prazo.
**Reflexão (2 min):** que frase acalmou mais?
**Professor anota:** 6 passos ☐ · empatia ☐ · solução + prazo ☐ · ligação calma ☐

## Homework da semana (7 dias)
Estude **10–20 minutos por dia**.
**Dia 1 — Empatia.** Escreva 6 frases de empatia diferentes.
**Dia 2 — Reescrita.** Reescreva 5 respostas frias ou defensivas.
**Dia 3 — Resposta 1.** Responda a uma reclamação fictícia sobre entrega atrasada.
**Dia 4 — Resposta 2.** Responda a uma reclamação sobre cobrança duplicada.
**Dia 5 — Revisão.** Reescreva a resposta da tarefa com as correções do professor.
**Dia 6 — Oral.** Grave 60–90 s acalmando um cliente por telefone.
**Dia 7 — Revisão.** Faça o quiz abaixo.
