🔥 Super Revisão

Resumo Geral

  1. Pronomes pessoais: Eu, você, ele, ela, nós, eles — a base para formar frases.

  2. Verbo “to be”: Ser/estar, presente e passado, afirmativo, negativo, interrogativo.

  3. Artigos definidos e indefinidos: “a”, “an”, “the” — para indicar coisas específicas ou gerais.

  4. Números e plurais: Contar e formar plural com “-s” ou “-es”.

  5. Adjetivos e posições: Como descrever pessoas, lugares, objetos; posição antes do substantivo.

  7. Pronomes possessivos: Meu, seu, nosso — para indicar posse.

  8. Verbos no presente: Ações habituais e uso do presente simples.

  9. Present continuous: Ações que ocorrem no momento da fala.

  10. Pronomes objeto: Me, you, him, her, us, them — para substituir o objeto direto.

  12. Perguntas básicas: Como fazer perguntas com verbo “to be” e auxiliares.

  13. Verbos no passado: Formas regulares (-ed) e irregulares, afirmativo.

  14. Passado do verbo “to be”: Was/were, afirmativo e negativo.

  15. Negativas com “not”: Com verbo “to be” e com outros verbos (don’t, doesn’t, didn’t).

  17. Advérbios de frequência: Sempre, nunca, às vezes — para indicar frequência.

  18. Preposições de lugar e tempo: In, on, at — para falar onde e quando.

  19. Pronomes demonstrativos: This, that, these, those — para indicar proximidade.

  20. Conjunções simples: And, but, or — para unir frases.

  22. Comparativos e superlativos: Mais, menos, o mais — para comparar.

  23. Modal verbs básicos: Can, must, should — para habilidade, obrigação e conselho.

  24. Verbos no presente e presente contínuo: Uso, diferenças e estrutura.

  25. Verbos no passado (revisão): Regulares, irregulares, uso e formação.

  26. Frases negativas: Como formar negação no presente e no passado.

📖 Prática de textos

Lição 1 – Pronomes pessoais

I am Ana. You are my friend.

Lição 2 – Verbo “to be”

She is at school. Yesterday, she was at home.
I am tired today but I was happy yesterday.

Lição 3 – Artigos

I have a cat and a dog. The cat is small.
The dog is big. I saw a bird on the tree.

Lição 4 – Números e plurais

There are two books on the table. I have three pens and four notebooks.
The dogs are playing outside. The cats are sleeping.

Lição 5 – Adjetivos

The small house is blue. The big car is fast.
She has a beautiful dress and a new bag.
The old tree is tall. The young boy is smart.

Lição 6 – Pronomes possessivos

This is my book. Your phone is on the table.
His house is big. Our family is happy.
Their car is red. My friends are here.

Lição 7 – Verbos no presente

I work every day. She loves music. They cook dinner at home.
He plays football on Sundays. We study English in the morning.
She drinks coffee every morning. They live in New York.

Lição 8 – Present continuous

I am studying now. She is reading a book. They are watching TV.
He is running in the park. We are cooking dinner together.
You are listening to music. They are playing soccer outside.

Lição 9 – Pronomes objeto

He sees me. I help her. They call us every day.
She gives him a present. I visit them on weekends.
He likes you. We meet her every Monday.

Lição 10 – Perguntas básicas

Are you happy? Is he your brother? Do they work here?
What is your name? Where do you live?
When does she study? How are you today?

Lição 11 – Verbos no passado

I worked yesterday. She went to the market. They cooked dinner last night.
He played football on Saturday. We watched a movie on Sunday.
You studied hard for the test.

Lição 12 – Passado do verbo “to be”

I was at school yesterday. They were tired. She was not at home.
He was happy last week. We were busy all day.
You were late this morning. They were not at the party.

Lição 13 – Frases negativas

I am not hungry. She doesn’t like coffee. They didn’t go to the party.
He is not tired today. We don’t play tennis.
You didn’t finish your homework.

Lição 14 – Advérbios de frequência

I always study. He sometimes eats pizza. They never watch movies.
She usually goes to the gym. We rarely drink soda.
You often visit your family.

Lição 15 – Preposições de lugar e tempo

The book is on the table. I was born in July. We meet at 6 pm.
The keys are in my bag. The party is at night.
He works on Mondays. She is at school now.
The picture is above the sofa. The meeting is in the morning.
