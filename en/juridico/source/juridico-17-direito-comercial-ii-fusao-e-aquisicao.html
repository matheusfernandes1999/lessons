1. Introduction

  • Key terms related to dissolution and liquidation.

A. Overview: Why Mergers & Acquisitions Matter

Mergers and acquisitions (M&A) are essential for corporate growth, restructuring, and market expansion. These transactions involve complex legal agreements to protect investors, shareholders, and employees.

For example:

  • Due diligence ensures that a company analyzes risks before a merger.

  • Hostile takeovers occur when a company is acquired without board approval.

  • Dissolution involves closing a company legally and distributing its assets.

2. Key Legal Terms in Mergers & Acquisitions

A. Corporate M&A Terminology

Term

Meaning

Example in Context

Merger

Two companies combine into one entity

"The merger between the two banks created a financial giant."

Acquisition

One company purchases another

"Company A completed the acquisition of Company B for $2 billion."

Takeover

A company gains control of another

"The tech firm initiated a takeover of its smaller competitor."

Hostile Takeover

A takeover opposed by the target company’s management

"The board of directors rejected the hostile takeover bid."

Friendly Takeover

A takeover agreed upon by both companies

"The friendly takeover was approved by shareholders."

Share Purchase Agreement

A contract defining the sale of company shares

"The Share Purchase Agreement outlined the payment terms."

B. Due Diligence & Legal Risk Management

Term

Meaning

Example in Context

Due Diligence

The process of investigating a company before a transaction

"Legal due diligence revealed undisclosed liabilities in the company’s finances."

Liabilities

Financial or legal obligations of a company

"The company had to disclose all outstanding liabilities before the acquisition."

Regulatory Compliance

Adherence to legal requirements in an industry

"Regulatory compliance is mandatory in cross-border M&A deals."

Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

A contract preventing disclosure of confidential information

"The parties signed an NDA before discussing the merger."

Antitrust Laws

Laws preventing monopolies and promoting market competition

"The merger was blocked due to antitrust law concerns."

Letter of Intent (LOI)

A preliminary agreement before finalizing a deal

"The LOI outlined the key terms of the acquisition."

🔍 Exercise: Match each term with its function in the M&A legal process.

C. Corporate Dissolution & Liquidation

Term

Meaning

Example in Context

Dissolution

The legal process of closing a company

"The corporation filed for dissolution due to insolvency."

Liquidation

Selling off a company’s assets to pay creditors

"After bankruptcy, the company entered liquidation proceedings."

Bankruptcy

A legal status of financial insolvency

"The company declared bankruptcy after failing to pay debts."

Winding Up

The final phase of dissolving a business

"The board approved the winding up of the subsidiary."

Receivership

A court-appointed entity manages company assets

"The company was placed under receivership to protect creditors."

Text 1: Excerpt from a Merger Agreement
