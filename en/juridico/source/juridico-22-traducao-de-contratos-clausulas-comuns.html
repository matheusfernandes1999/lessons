A. Overview: Why Contract Translation Matters

Contracts define rights, obligations, and remedies for parties in legal agreements. A single mistranslation can alter the legal meaning of a contract, leading to disputes or invalidity.

For example:

  • A force majeure clause protects parties from liability in unforeseen events.

  • A governing law clause determines which jurisdiction applies in case of disputes.

  • The use of archaic legal terms (hereinafter, whereby) can complicate translations.

2. Key Legal Terms and Contract Clauses

A. Common Contract Clauses and Their Functions

Clause

Function

Example in Context

Governing Law Clause

Specifies which jurisdiction's law applies

"This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales."

Force Majeure Clause

Excuses liability for unforeseen circumstances

"Neither party shall be liable for delays caused by natural disasters."

Indemnity Clause

Protects against financial losses

"The Contractor agrees to indemnify the Client against all claims."

Severability Clause

Ensures the contract remains valid even if part is unenforceable

"If any provision is found invalid, the remaining terms shall remain in effect."

Entire Agreement Clause

States that the contract is the final agreement between parties

"This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties."

Termination Clause

Defines how the contract can be ended

"Either party may terminate this Agreement upon 30 days’ written notice."

B. Key Legal Terminology in Contracts

Term

Meaning

Example in Context

Party

An entity entering into a contract

"The parties agree to the terms outlined herein."

Obligation

A legal duty imposed by a contract

"The seller has an obligation to deliver the goods within 10 days."

Breach of Contract

Failure to comply with contractual terms

"Late payment constitutes a breach of contract."

Consideration

Something of value exchanged in a contract

"A valid contract requires consideration from both parties."

Waiver

Voluntary relinquishment of a right

"Failure to enforce a term shall not constitute a waiver."

3. Challenges in Contract Translation

A. Literal vs. Functional Translation

  • Literal translation maintains word-for-word accuracy but may sound unnatural in the target language.

  • Functional translation adapts phrases for legal clarity while preserving meaning.

English Contract Term

Literal Translation (Problematic)

Functional Translation (Better)

Null and Void

Nulo e vazio

Sem efeito legal

Hereinafter referred to as

Doravante referido como

A partir deste momento denominado

Without prejudice to

Sem afetar os direitos de

  2. "Without prejudice to the foregoing, the agreement remains binding."

  3. "The parties hereto agree to the following terms."

B. Ambiguity and Precision in Contract Language

  • Legal English often includes redundant terms (acknowledge and agree, deem and consider).

  • Some words have multiple legal meanings (assign = transfer rights OR designate responsibility).

  • Contract language must avoid ambiguity that could lead to disputes.
