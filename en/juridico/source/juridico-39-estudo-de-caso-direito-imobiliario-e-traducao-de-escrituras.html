A. Overview: Why Real Estate Law Matters

Real estate law governs the ownership, sale, and transfer of property. A title deed is the legal document that proves ownership and records rights over land or real estate.

✅ Property ownership must be clearly defined to avoid disputes.
✅ Real estate contracts must include key clauses related to payment, transfer, and legal obligations.
✅ Ambiguous translations can affect legal validity and lead to litigation.

For example:

  • A buyer may challenge a property sale if the title is unclear.

  • An improperly translated real estate contract may create enforceability issues.

  • Failure to translate encumbrances correctly may cause financial or legal problems for the new owner.

2. Key Legal Terms in Real Estate Law

A. Essential Property Law Terminology

Term

Meaning

Example in Context

Title Deed

A legal document proving ownership of property

"The buyer received the title deed after completing the purchase."

Encumbrance

A claim or restriction on a property’s use

"The mortgage is an encumbrance that must be cleared before sale."

Lien

A legal right over a property used as security for a debt

"The lender placed a lien on the property until the loan was repaid."

Mortgage

A loan secured by real estate

"The homeowner obtained a mortgage to finance the purchase."

Easement

A legal right to use another person's land for a specific purpose

"The utility company has an easement to install power lines."

Quitclaim Deed

A deed that transfers property without guaranteeing clear title

"The seller transferred the property using a quitclaim deed."

B. Common Clauses in Real Estate Contracts

Clause

Purpose

Example in Context

Property Description Clause

Provides a legal description of the property

"The property is legally described as Lot 12, Block 5 of Springfield Estates."

Purchase Price Clause

States the agreed-upon price and payment terms

"The buyer agrees to pay $500,000 for the property in installments."

Title and Ownership Clause

Confirms the seller’s right to transfer ownership

"The seller warrants that they hold clear title to the property."

Encumbrance Clause

Declares any existing liens, mortgages, or legal restrictions

"The property is subject to an existing mortgage of $200,000."

Closing and Transfer Clause

Defines the date and conditions for finalizing the sale

"The closing shall take place on July 1, 2024, at which time title shall transfer to the buyer."

3. Challenges in Translating Real Estate Legal Documents

A. Literal vs. Functional Translation

  • Real estate laws differ across jurisdictions, requiring careful adaptation.

  • Certain property law terms have no direct equivalents in other languages.

4. Reading Practice: Real Estate Legal Documents

The Grantor warrants that the property is free from liens and encumbrances, except as noted herein."

The parties agree that the sale shall close on August 15, 2024, at which time the Seller shall execute and deliver a Warranty Deed transferring title. The Buyer acknowledges that the property is being sold ‘as-is’ with no further warranties except as expressly stated herein."
