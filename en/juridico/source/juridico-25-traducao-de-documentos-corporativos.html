A. Overview: Why Corporate Document Translation Matters

Corporate documents define the legal framework of a company, its governance, and decision-making processes. Errors in translation can lead to legal disputes, compliance violations, or misinterpretation of corporate rights and obligations.

For example:

  • Bylaws establish the internal rules of a corporation.

  • Articles of Incorporation legally create the company.

  • Minutes of meetings record official corporate decisions.

3. Challenges in Translating Corporate Documents

A. Literal vs. Functional Translation

  • Legal terms must reflect corporate law standards in the target jurisdiction.

  • Some terms have no direct equivalents, requiring conceptual adaptation.

  • Corporate structures differ between Common Law and Civil Law systems.

English Term

Literal Translation (Problematic)

Functional Translation (Better)

Board Resolution

Resolução do Conselho

Deliberação do Conselho de Administração

Bylaws

Regulamentos

Estatuto Social

Minutes of Meeting

Minutos da Reunião

Ata da Reunião

B. Ambiguity in Corporate Governance Documents

  • Corporate terms may have different legal implications in different jurisdictions.

  • Stockholder vs. shareholder—these terms are often used interchangeably but may have different connotations.

  • "Shall" vs. "may"—obligation vs. discretion in corporate governance.
