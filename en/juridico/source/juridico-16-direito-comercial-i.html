2. Key Legal Terms in Commercial Law

A. Essential Commercial Law Terminology

Term

Meaning

Example in Context

Shareholder

An individual or entity that owns shares in a company

"The shareholders voted to approve the merger."

Quotaholder

A person who owns a percentage of shares in a limited liability company (LLC)

"Each quotaholder is responsible for their proportional investment in the company."

Procurement

The process of acquiring goods or services for business operations

"The procurement department issued a request for proposals."

Indemnity Clause

A contractual provision that protects one party from liability

"The seller agrees to indemnify the buyer against any third-party claims."

Force Majeure Clause

A clause that exempts liability for events beyond a party’s control

"The supplier invoked force majeure due to a natural disaster."

Liquidated Damages

A predetermined compensation amount for contract breaches

"The contract specifies liquidated damages of $50,000 for late delivery."

Confidentiality Agreement (NDA)

A legal agreement to protect sensitive business information

"Employees must sign a confidentiality agreement before accessing trade secrets."

🔍 Exercise: Identify which terms relate to corporate structure, risk management, or contractual obligations.

B. Common Clauses in Commercial Agreements

Clause

Purpose

Example in Context

Governing Law Clause

Determines which legal system applies to the contract

"This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales."

Jurisdiction Clause

Specifies the court that will resolve disputes

"Any dispute shall be settled in the courts of New York."

Non-Compete Clause

Restricts one party from competing in the same market

"The employee agrees not to work for competitors for two years."

Warranties and Representations Clause

Ensures both parties provide truthful statements

"The seller warrants that the goods meet industry standards."

Payment Terms Clause

Specifies payment obligations and deadlines

"Payment shall be made in three installments over six months."

3. Challenges in Translating Commercial Law Documents

A. Literal vs. Functional Translation

  • Business terminology differs between jurisdictions, requiring adaptation.

  • Some corporate structures and contract clauses have no direct equivalent in other languages.

  • Misinterpretation of financial or legal obligations can lead to non-compliance.

English Term

Literal Translation (Problematic)

Functional Translation (Better)

Sócio de empresa limitada (LLC member)

Liquidated Damages

Cláusula penal por descumprimento contratual

Force Majeure Clause

Cláusula de força maior

Cláusula de exoneração por eventos imprevisíveis

B. Avoiding Ambiguity in Commercial Law Translation

  • Unclear payment terms may lead to late payments or contract breaches.

  • Ambiguous non-compete clauses can be challenged in court.

  • Improper translation of legal disclaimers may expose businesses to liability.

4. Reading Practice: Commercial Law Case Studies

  • 📖 Instruction: Read the following case study excerpts aloud and analyze the legal reasoning used.

Text 1: Contract Dispute Over Payment Terms

"The plaintiff, a logistics company, entered into a service agreement with the defendant, a retail distributor. The contract stipulated that payment would be made within 30 days of delivery. However, the defendant failed to make payments for multiple shipments. The court ruled that the payment terms clause was legally enforceable, ordering the defendant to pay outstanding invoices plus interest."

  • 🔍 Comprehension Questions:

  1. What contractual obligation was breached?

  2. How did the court enforce the contract?

  3. Why was the payment terms clause important in this case?

Text 2: Breach of Non-Compete Agreement

"The defendant, a former executive, signed a non-compete clause prohibiting employment with competitors for 12 months after leaving the company. Three months later, the defendant accepted a position with a direct competitor. The plaintiff sought an injunction, arguing that the defendant breached the non-compete agreement. The court upheld the clause, restricting the defendant from working for competitors for the remainder of the agreement term."

  • 🔍 Comprehension Questions:

  1. What legal obligation did the defendant violate?

  2. What remedy did the court provide to the plaintiff?

  3. How does this ruling impact employment contracts?

5. Writing Exercise: Drafting a Commercial Contract Clause
