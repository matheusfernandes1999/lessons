Civil Law – Contracts, Torts, and Remedies

🎯 Objectives:

  1. Understand key civil law terminology used in contracts, torts, and remedies.

  2. Differentiate between contractual liability and tort liability.

  3. Identify legal remedies available for breaches of contract or civil wrongs.

  4. Apply civil law terminology in reading, writing, and translation exercises.

1. Introduction

📌 The session will cover:

  • The distinction between contract law and tort law.

  • Key legal terms related to contracts, civil liability, and damages.

  • Common remedies available in civil law.

  • The role of legal agreements in preventing disputes.

  • Why Civil Law Matters

  • Civil law regulates relationships between private parties, including contracts, property disputes, and personal injury claims. Unlike criminal law, which punishes offenses against society, civil law focuses on compensating victims and enforcing agreements.

  • For example:

  • A contract breach may result in compensatory damages.

  • A tort (civil wrong) such as negligence may lead to liability for damages.

  • Courts can issue injunctions to prevent ongoing harm.

  • ❓ Discussion Questions:

  • "What is the difference between a breach of contract and a tort?"
    “Why do contracts include detailed clauses about damages and liability?"
    "What are some examples of civil law cases in your country?"

2. Key Legal Terms in Civil Law

A. Contract Law Terminology

Term

Meaning

Example in Context

Offer

A proposal to enter into a contract

"The seller made an offer to sell the property for $500,000."

Acceptance

Agreement to the terms of an offer

"The buyer accepted the offer, creating a binding contract."

Consideration

Something of value exchanged in a contract

"A valid contract requires consideration from both parties."

Breach of Contract

Failure to fulfill contractual obligations

"The supplier breached the contract by failing to deliver the goods."

Specific Performance

A court order to fulfill contractual obligations

"The court ordered the builder to complete the construction project."

B. Tort Law Terminology

Term

Meaning

Example in Context

Tort

A civil wrong that causes harm

"Negligence is the most common type of tort."

Negligence

Failing to take reasonable care, causing harm

"The driver’s negligence led to a serious accident."

Strict Liability

Legal responsibility regardless of intent

"A manufacturer may be strictly liable for defective products."

Defamation

False statements harming someone’s reputation

"The journalist was sued for defamation."

Duty of Care

A legal obligation to avoid causing harm

"Employers have a duty of care toward their employees."

C. Legal Remedies for Civil Wrongs

Remedy

Meaning

Example in Context

Compensatory Damages

Money awarded to cover losses

"The plaintiff received $50,000 in compensatory damages."

Punitive Damages

Additional money awarded to punish wrongdoing

"The court imposed punitive damages to deter similar conduct."

Injunction

A court order preventing an action

"The judge issued an injunction stopping the company from polluting."

Restitution

Returning benefits wrongly received

"The defendant was ordered to make restitution to the victim."

Rescission

Cancelling a contract and restoring parties to their original position

"The contract was rescinded due to fraud."

3. Reading Practice: Civil Law in Context

  • 📖 Instruction: Read the following legal texts aloud and analyze the terminology used.

Text 1: Excerpt from a Contract Dispute Case

"The plaintiff alleges that the defendant breached the agreement by failing to deliver goods on the specified date. As a result, the plaintiff seeks compensatory damages for financial losses incurred. The court may also consider ordering specific performance to ensure compliance with the contract terms."

  • 🔍 Comprehension Questions:

  1. What does the term "breached the agreement" mean?

  2. What legal remedies does the plaintiff seek?

  3. What is the difference between compensatory damages and specific performance?

Text 2: Excerpt from a Negligence Case

"The defendant, a construction company, failed to comply with safety regulations, resulting in severe injury to a worker. The plaintiff claims that the defendant breached its duty of care and is liable for negligence. The court will determine the appropriate damages based on the extent of harm suffered."

  • 🔍 Comprehension Questions:

  1. How did the defendant breach its duty of care?

  2. Why is the defendant liable for damages?

  3. What is the difference between contractual liability and tort liability?

4. Writing Exercise: Drafting a Legal Complaint

✍️ Prompt: Write a short legal complaint for a breach of contract or negligence case. Include:

  • A statement of facts describing what happened.

  • The legal claim (e.g., breach of contract, negligence).

  • The remedy sought (e.g., damages, injunction, specific performance).

✅ Example Answer:
"The plaintiff entered into a contract with the defendant to purchase 100 units of equipment. The defendant failed to deliver the goods as agreed. As a result, the plaintiff suffered financial losses. The plaintiff requests that the court award compensatory damages in the amount of $10,000 and order specific performance of the contract."

6. Conclusion

✅ Contract law governs agreements; tort law addresses civil wrongs.
✅ Legal remedies include damages, injunctions, and rescission.
✅ Civil law focuses on compensation rather than punishment.
