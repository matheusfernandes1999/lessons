Administrative Law

🎯 Objectives:

  1. Understand key legal terms used in administrative law, government regulations, and compliance.

  2. Differentiate between laws, statutes, regulations, and policies.

  3. Recognize the role of government agencies and their authority in enforcing regulations.

  4. Apply administrative law terminology in reading, writing, and translation exercises.

1. Introduction

  • 📌 The session will cover:

  • The role of administrative law in regulating government actions.

  • The difference between statutes, regulations, and policies.

  • The importance of compliance and enforcement in legal and business settings.

  • How government agencies interpret and apply administrative law.

  • Why Administrative Law Matters

  • Administrative law governs how government agencies operate, including their power to enforce laws, issue regulations, and resolve disputes. It affects businesses, individuals, and public institutions in areas such as taxation, environmental law, and corporate compliance.

  • For example:

  • A statute passed by Congress may authorize an agency to create regulations.

  • Agencies such as the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) or Federal Trade Commission (FTC) enforce these rules.

  • Failure to comply can result in fines, lawsuits, or revocation of licenses.

  • ❓ Discussion Questions:

  • "What is the difference between a law and a regulation?"
    "Why do businesses need to comply with administrative regulations?"
    "What happens if a company violates environmental or labor regulations?"

2. Key Legal Terms in Administrative Law

A. Fundamental Terms

Term

Meaning

Example in Context

Statute

A law enacted by a legislature

"The Clean Air Act is a federal statute regulating pollution."

Regulation

A rule issued by an agency under authority of a statute

"The FDA issued new regulations on food labeling."

Compliance

Adhering to laws, regulations, or policies

"All companies must ensure compliance with labor laws."

Enforcement

Government action to ensure compliance with laws

"The SEC enforces securities regulations to prevent fraud."

Due Process

The right to fair treatment under the law

"The agency must provide due process before revoking a license."

Administrative Agency

A government body that enforces laws and regulations

"The IRS is an administrative agency responsible for tax collection."

Sanctions

Legal penalties for non-compliance

"Violating data privacy laws may result in financial sanctions."

Judicial Review

Court review of administrative decisions

"The court granted judicial review of the agency’s ruling."

Licensing

The process of obtaining legal authorization to operate

"Doctors must obtain a medical license before practicing."

🔍 Exercise: Identify whether each term is related to law-making, enforcement, or compliance.

B. Understanding Laws vs. Regulations

Statutes (Laws)

Regulations

Passed by legislative bodies (e.g., Congress, Parliament).

Issued by government agencies (e.g., FDA, EPA).

Broad legal principles that define rights and obligations.

Specific rules on how laws should be implemented.

Violations lead to criminal or civil penalties.

Violations result in administrative penalties (e.g., fines, revocation of permits).

3. Reading Practice: Administrative Law in Context

  • 📖 Instruction: Read the following text aloud and analyze key legal terms.

Text 1: Excerpt from a Government Regulation

"All businesses operating in the United States must comply with data protection laws. Under Section 5 of the Federal Trade Commission Act, the FTC is authorized to investigate and enforce compliance with consumer protection regulations. Any company found violating these regulations shall be subject to administrative fines and mandatory corrective actions."

  • 🔍 Comprehension Questions:

  1. What does it mean to “comply with data protection laws”?

  2. What is the role of the FTC in enforcing compliance?

  3. What legal consequences exist for violating administrative regulations?

Text 2: Excerpt from a Court Decision on Administrative Law

"The Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) issued new regulations restricting carbon emissions. A group of businesses challenged the regulations, arguing that the agency exceeded its authority. The Supreme Court ruled that the agency acted within the scope of its statutory power, affirming that administrative agencies have discretion in implementing regulations."

  • 🔍 Comprehension Questions:

  1. What does it mean for an agency to "act within the scope of its statutory power"?

  2. How does judicial review impact agency regulations?

  3. Why might businesses challenge administrative regulations in court?

4. Writing Exercise: Drafting a Legal Notice for Compliance

✍️ Prompt: Write a compliance notice informing employees of a new administrative regulation affecting your company.

✅ Example Answer:
"Effective 1 January 2025, all employees must comply with new workplace safety regulations issued by the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA). The company shall conduct mandatory training sessions to ensure compliance. Failure to adhere to these regulations may result in disciplinary actions or administrative penalties."
