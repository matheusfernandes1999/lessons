Why This Matters

Legal English relies on precise grammar and formatting to ensure clarity and enforceability. A misplaced modal verb can change obligations, incorrect punctuation can create ambiguity, and outdated terminology can reduce readability.

For example:

  • "The tenant shall pay rent." (creates a binding obligation)

  • "The tenant may pay rent." (gives optional permission)

Similarly, punctuation changes meaning:

  • "The court ruled that the defendant was guilty, and the witness testified." (Two separate actions.)

  • "The court ruled that the defendant was guilty and the witness testified." (Could imply the witness's testimony proved guilt.)

Understanding these elements helps legal professionals draft accurate, enforceable, and modern legal documents.

Grammar Point:

Modal Verbs & Subjunctive Mood

A. Modal Verbs in Legal English

Legal English uses modal verbs to express obligation, permission, necessity, or possibility.

Modal Verb

Legal Meaning

Example

Shall

Mandatory obligation (creates a duty).

"The tenant shall pay rent by the 1st of each month."

Must

Absolute requirement (non-negotiable).

"The applicant must submit all documents by 31 December 2024."

May

Permission or possibility.

"The court may adjourn the hearing if necessary."

Should

Recommendation (not legally binding).

"Parties should attempt mediation before litigation."

⚠️ Common Mistake: Confusing shall and must. Shall imposes a duty, while must states a factual necessity.

B. Subjunctive Mood in Legal English

The subjunctive mood is used for hypotheticals, legal demands, and formal recommendations. The structure follows "that + base verb" (no -s for third-person singular).

Use Case

Example

Legal Requirements

"It is essential that the defendant be present."

Hypotheticals

"If the contract be invalid, the clause shall not apply."

🔍 Exercise: Rewrite the following sentence using the subjunctive mood:

  • "The law requires the company to file the report."
    ✅ "The law requires that the company file the report."

2. Punctuation in Legal Writing

A. Key Punctuation Rules

Punctuation clarifies legal meaning and prevents disputes.

Mark

Rule

Example

Comma (,)

Separates clauses, lists, or non-essential phrases.

"The Seller, acting in good faith, shall deliver the goods."

Semicolon (;)

Links related independent clauses.

"Payment is due tomorrow; late fees apply thereafter."

Colon (:)

Introduces lists, explanations, or quotes.

"The court ruled: ‘The defendant is liable.’"

Em Dash (—)

Adds emphasis or interrupts a thought.

"The witness—despite objections—testified."

⚠️ Ambiguity Alert:

  • "Woman without her man is nothing."

  • "Woman—without her, man is nothing."

B. Practice Exercise:

Correct the punctuation in the following sentence:
"The parties agree the goods shall be delivered on 5 May 2024 the buyer may inspect them upon receipt."
✅ "The parties agree the goods shall be delivered on 5 May 2024; the buyer may inspect them upon receipt."

3. Legal Writing Standards

A. Dates & Numbers

  • Dates: Use 1 January 2024 (avoid 1st or th).

  • Numbers:

    • Spell out 1–10 (ten days).

    • Use numerals 11+ (11 days).

    • Money: £500 (five hundred pounds).

B. Citations

  • Statutes: Companies Act 2006 (no comma).

  • Cases: Donoghue v Stevenson (v. for versus; italicize case names).

C. Terminology: Avoiding Archaic and Redundant Phrases

Example 1: Lease Agreement (Rental Contract)

RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into on 1 January 2024, by and between:

  • Landlord: John Smith ("Landlord")

  • Tenant: Jane Doe ("Tenant")

1. Term of Lease
The lease shall commence on 1 February 2024 and shall continue for a period of twelve (12) months, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.

2. Rent Payment
The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of $1,500 (one thousand five hundred dollars), due on the first day of each calendar month. Late payments shall incur a penalty of $50 (fifty dollars) per day.

3. Maintenance and Repairs
The Tenant shall be responsible for maintaining the premises in good condition and must notify the Landlord of any necessary repairs. The Landlord may enter the premises with 24 (twenty-four) hours’ notice to conduct inspections.

4. Termination
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement if the Tenant fails to comply with the lease terms. In the event of termination, the Tenant must vacate the premises within 30 (thirty) days.

Example 2: Contract Clause (Commercial Agreement)

SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into on 1 January 2024, by and between:

  • Provider: ABC Consulting, LLC ("Provider")

  • Client: XYZ Enterprises ("Client")

1. Scope of Services
The Provider shall render consulting services, including but not limited to market analysis, strategy development, and financial planning. The Client may request additional services, which must be agreed upon in writing before execution.

2. Term of Agreement
This Agreement shall commence on 1 February 2024 and shall remain in effect until 31 December 2024, unless terminated earlier pursuant to Clause 7.

3. Responsibilities of the Parties

  • The Provider shall deliver reports and strategic recommendations no later than 15 (fifteen) days after completion of each project phase.

  • The Client must provide access to all relevant data and cooperate with the Provider in a timely manner.

  • The Provider may delegate certain tasks to subcontractors subject to the Client’s approval.

4. Payment Terms
The Client shall pay the Provider a total sum of $25,000 (twenty-five thousand dollars) for the services described herein. Payment must be made in two installments: 50% upfront and 50% upon completion of the services. Late payments shall be subject to an interest fee of 1.5% per month.

5. Confidentiality
Both parties shall maintain confidentiality regarding proprietary or sensitive business information exchanged under this Agreement. Information disclosed by either party must not be shared with third parties without prior written consent, except as required by law.

6. Limitation of Liability
The Provider shall not be liable for any indirect, consequential, or special damages arising from the services provided under this Agreement, except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct.

7. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 (thirty) days' written notice. However, in cases of breach of contract, the non-breaching party shall have the right to terminate immediately. Any outstanding payments must be settled within 15 (fifteen) days of termination.

8. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York. Any disputes shall be resolved through arbitration in accordance with the rules of the American Arbitration Association (AAA).

9. Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, whether written or oral. Any amendments must be made in writing and signed by both parties.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first written above.

This matter having come before the Court on 15 September 2023, the Court finds as follows:
