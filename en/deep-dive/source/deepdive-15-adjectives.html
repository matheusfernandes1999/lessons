🎯 Overview

What are adjectives?

Adjectives describe or modify nouns. They give information about appearance, size, age, color, origin, material, and opinion.

Examples:

  • A beautiful red Italian leather sofa

  • An old wooden chair

Why is this important?

In English, adjective order matters. Unlike Portuguese, where “uma casa bonita azul” is acceptable, English requires a fixed order:
→ a beautiful blue house ✅
→ a blue beautiful house ❌

Additionally, understanding adjective formation allows you to expand vocabulary and use richer, more precise language.

🔍 Deep Dive

1. Adjective Order in English

The general rule is:

Opinion > Size > Age > Shape > Color > Origin > Material > Purpose > Noun

Examples:

  • a lovely small round blue Italian ceramic coffee table

  • an amazing big old brown leather armchair

🧠 Compare with Portuguese:

  • “uma pequena caixa vermelha de madeira chinesa”
    → a small red Chinese wooden box

Order is not flexible in English. Changing the order can sound awkward or unnatural.

2. Using 3, 4 or More Adjectives

More adjectives = more care with structure.

Correct:

  • She adopted a sweet little black stray cat.

  • They bought a beautiful large antique Persian rug.

Wrong:

  • She adopted a black little sweet stray cat. ❌

📌 Tip: Don’t overuse adjectives unless needed. Clarity over decoration.

3. Adjective Formation

You can form adjectives from nouns, verbs, or even prefixes/suffixes.

Base

Adjective

Example

child

childish

That was a childish comment.

destructive

destructiveous

It's a destructiveous place.

create

creative

She's a very creative person.

use

useful

This is a useful tool.

help

helpless

He felt helpless in that storm.

Common suffixes: -ful, -less, -ous, -ive, -al, -ic, -ish

🧠 Avoid literal translations:
“interessante” → interesting ✅
But: “prejudicial” ≠ prejudicious ❌ (harmful or damaging instead)

👥 Real-life Dialogues

1. Shopping:
A: I love this beautiful long red silk dress.
B: It’s elegant and perfect for the party!

2. Travel:
A: We stayed in a charming small wooden cabin by the lake.
B: Sounds peaceful!

3. Office:
A: She gave a detailed technical clear explanation.
B: Hmm, shouldn’t that be: “a clear detailed technical explanation”?

4. Personal life:
A: He’s just a lazy irresponsible young man.
B: True, but he’s also smart and creative.

🔁 Deep Review

Summary:

  • Adjectives follow a fixed order in English.

  • You can use multiple adjectives but they must be in a logical sequence.

  • Form adjectives using common suffixes and root transformations.

  • Use adjectives for precise, rich descriptions, but avoid overload.
